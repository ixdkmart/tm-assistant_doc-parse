Policy - Requests for Flexible Working Arrangements

INTRODUCTION AND PURPOSE
This policy recognises that flexible working arrangements can assist team members to balance their personal and work responsibilities. Flexible working arrangements are about a team member and Kmart making changes to when, where and how a person will work to better meet individual and business needs. The availability of these arrangements is subject to a team member's situation and Kmart operational requirements and/or other limitations.

The purpose of this policy is to:
• provide awareness of flexible working options and Kmart's obligations; and
• outline the process to make a request for flexible working arrangements.

POLICY APPLICATION
This policy applies to all eligible Kmart Australia employees (hereafter referred to as team members).

In accordance with the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) ('FWA') (as amended):

a) a team member (other than a casual) who has completed at least 12 months of continuous service with Kmart immediately before making the request; or

b) a casual team member, who immediately before making the request, has been employed by Kmart on a regular and systematic basis for a sequence of periods of employment during a period of at least 12 months, and has a reasonable expectation of continuing employment by Kmart on a regular and systematic basis,

may request changes to their working arrangements, if they need flexibility for any of the following reasons:

• The team member is pregnant.
• The team member is the parent, or has responsibility for the care, of a child who is of school age or younger.
• The team member is a carer (within the meaning of the Carer Recognition Act 2010).
• The team member has a disability.
• The team member is 55 or older.
• The team member is experiencing family and domestic violence.
• The team member provides care or support to a member of the team member's immediate family, or a member of the team member's household, who requires care or support because the member is experiencing family and domestic violence.

Where a team member's employment is covered by the terms of an industrial instrument, such as an enterprise agreement or a modern award, the terms of the industrial instrument will apply. However, if this policy contains a more beneficial term, this policy will apply to the extent of any inconsistency.

This policy should be read in conjunction with any other relevant policies or procedures.

This policy can be varied or withdrawn at any time and does not form part of, and is not incorporated into, a team member's contract of employment.

POLICY: Requests for Flexible Working Arrangements

Requests for flexible working arrangements must be:
• in writing; and
• set out details of the change sought and the reasons for the change (including, where possible, suggestions on how the changes could be accommodated).

Requests should be made by providing a completed 'Request for Flexible Working Arrangements Form' (available on 'Kit') to your line manager.

Examples of changes in working arrangements that can be requested include changes in the hours of work, changes in when work is performed or changes in the location of work.

For example, a team member who is a parent or has responsibility for the care of a child and is returning to work after taking parental or adoption leave, may request to work part-time to assist them to care for the child.

Response to a Request for Flexible Working Arrangements

Kmart must respond to a request for flexible working arrangements within 21 days of receiving the request.

Kmart's response will either:
1. state that Kmart grants the request; or
2. set out the agreed change, if, following a discussion between Kmart and the team member, Kmart and the team member agree to a change to the team member's working arrangements that differs from that set out in the original request; or
3. state that Kmart refuses the request. In this case, the response will:
   o include details of the reasons for the refusal; and
   o set out the particular business grounds for refusing the request and an explanation of how those grounds apply to the request; and
   o either:
     o set out any alternative changes in the team member's working arrangements (which would accommodate, to any extent, their circumstances) that Kmart would be willing to make; or
     o state that there are no such changes; and
   o set out the effect of sections 65B and 65C of the FWA.

Kmart may refuse a request only if:
• Kmart has discussed the request with the team member and genuinely tried to reach an agreement with them to accommodate their circumstances (however an agreement was not reached); and
• Kmart has had regard to the consequences of the refusal on the team member; and
• the refusal is on reasonable business grounds, which may include (but are not limited to) the following:
   o the new working arrangements requested by the team member would be too costly for Kmart;
   o there is no capacity to change the working arrangements of other team members to accommodate the new working arrangements requested by the team member;
   o it would be impractical to change the working arrangements of other team members, or recruit new team members, to accommodate the new working arrangements requested by the team member;

Requests for Flexible Working Arrangements under Other Circumstances

Team members who do not meet the criteria under this policy to request Flexible Working Arrangements may still be able to request flexible working arrangements under the Flexible Work Policy (as amended from time to time), which can be found on 'Kit'.
