Policy - Rights to Work in Australia

INTRODUCTION AND PURPOSE
Australia has strict laws that deal with a person's right to work in Australia and Kmart must comply with these laws.

To work at Kmart persons must have a lawful right to work in Australia. It is illegal for Kmart to employ or engage a person who does not have a right to work in Australia.

Non-compliance with Australia's migration laws can result in Kmart facing significant sanctions and fines, including criminal prosecution and the withdrawal of the right for Kmart to sponsor other international staff in Australia.

Individuals, such as team members and managers can also be held personally liable for breaches of migration law and can face significant fines and penalties including imprisonment.

It is therefore imperative that all relevant team members comply with this Policy and any other supplementary procedures Kmart has in place to ensure compliance with Australia's migration laws.

The purpose of this policy is to set out:
❖ who may be lawfully employed or engaged by Kmart; and
❖ the obligations of managers and team members to assist Kmart ensure that it complies with its obligations under Australian migration laws.

POLICY APPLICATION
This Policy applies to all Kmart team members:

❖ who are responsible for or engaged in the recruitment of new team members in Australia, including via third parties such as labour hire or employment agencies;
❖ who are responsible for overseeing the ongoing employment or engagement of any team member whose right to work in Australia is subject to a Visa; and
❖ whose right to work in Australia is subject to a valid Visa.

For the purposes of this Policy:

❖ Visa means any valid legal instrument issued by the Department of Immigration and Border Protection (DIBP) under Australian migration laws, which permits a person to work in Australia (including subject to specified conditions);
❖ Visa Holder means a job applicant or person employed or engaged by Kmart, whose right to work in Australia is subject to a valid Visa, i.e. any person who is not an Australian Citizen; and
❖ VEVO means a Visa Entitlement Verification Online check, being an online check on the DIBP's website that is used to confirm a person's right to work in Australia, and what (if any) conditions apply to that right to work

POLICY
What is meant by the Right to Work?

Only Australian Citizens have an automatic right to work in Australia. Everyone else is

required to hold a current and valid Visa to be employed or engaged by Kmart.

In some cases, specific work limitations may apply under a Visa, for example it may:

❖ restrict the number of hours in a week or fortnight that a Visa Holder is lawfully permitted to work in Australia; and/or
❖ limit the amount of time a Visa Holder may continue to work in Australia (e.g.: a working holiday visa will generally limit work to a total of 6 months).

It is illegal for Kmart to offer or allow a Visa Holder to work in breach of restrictions in their Visa.

Obligations of team members responsible for recruiting employees

Only the following people are permitted to make a decision to recruit a Visa Holder to work at Kmart, and are therefore responsible for ensuring compliance with this Policy and Australian migration laws:

❖ Store Level - applicable Store Manager; and
❖ Head Office - applicable Line Manager

The applicable Store Manager or Line Manager must also obtain signoff from the Human Resources National Recruitment Manager (or authorised delegate) before hiring any Visa holders.

Establishing a person's right to work in Australia

The applicable Store Manager or Line Manager must take reasonable steps to:

❖ confirm that every job applicant has a lawful right to work in Australia, and
❖ identify what (if any) work limitations they are subject to,

before making a job offer to the applicant. This includes (as a minimum):

❖ requesting acceptable proof of the applicant's right to work in Australia (e.g.: a copy of their Visa);
❖ viewing acceptable photo identification to verify the identity of the applicant;
❖ obtaining the applicant's consent to conduct a VEVO check to confirm their right to work in Australia and work conditions (if any);
❖ referring the applicant's signed consent to Human Resources so that they can conduct the VEVO check;
❖ confirming with Human Resources that a VEVO check has taken place, and that the applicant is entitled to work in Australia and subject to what conditions; and
❖ taking and providing Human Resources with copies of all relevant right to work documentation that has been obtained from the applicant, in accordance with Company procedures.

Human Resources must ensure any information gathered in the course of checking an applicant's right to work in Australia, particularly personal information, is securely stored in accordance with Company procedures.

What documents prove a person's right to work in Australia?

The following documents are required to provide acceptable proof of a person's right to lawfully work in Australia.
Except for Australian citizens, a VEVO will be conducted by Human Resources to verify a person's right to work before they commence employment. A copy of the VEVO Check will be forwarded to Stores and this copy must be retained on the team member's personnel file.

Australian Citizens
Proof of Australian Citizenship can be demonstrated by:
❖ an Australian Passport; or
❖ an Australian Citizenship Certificate and an acceptable form of photo ID (e.g.: State/Territory drivers' licence, student ID card or Proof of Age card); or
❖ if born before 20 August 1986, an extract or full Australian Birth Certificate and an acceptable form of photo ID; or
❖ if born on or after 20 August 1986, a full Australian Birth Certificate, acceptable form of photo ID and documentary evidence that at least one parent was an Australian citizen or permanent resident at the time of the child's birth.

Australian permanent residents
All Australian permanent residents must produce a valid and current international passport with a Certificate of Permanent Residency.

New Zealand citizens
New Zealand citizens must produce a valid and current passport.

Other foreign citizens
All other foreign citizens must produce a valid and current international passport and a valid Visa.

Photo identification
Acceptable valid and current photo ID must be produced to confirm identity (e.g.: government issued photo ID, such as drivers' licence, or student cards).
If the job applicant is a Visa Holder, the photo ID produced must match their personal details attaching to the relevant Visa.

Where a job applicant cannot produce acceptable photo ID:
❖ the job applicant must provide a recent passport size photograph (i.e.: not older than 3 months), which has been lawfully certified by a permitted person who:
❖ has known the job applicant for at least 12 months;
❖ is not related by birth, marriage or defacto relationship; and
❖ a statutory declaration must be completed and provided by the person

Offers of employment to Visa Holders with work related limitations

Where a Visa Holder has specific work limitations under their Visa, work must only be offered which is consistent with those limitations. For example:

❖ if a Visa permits a maximum of 20 hours' work per week, the Visa Holder must not be offered or allowed to work in excess of 20 hours per week; and

❖ if a Visa is valid for 2 years, the Visa Holder must not be offered or allowed to work in excess of and must not continue to be employed beyond the end of, that period unless the Visa is reissued by the DIBP. Any extension of a Visa Holder's work must not exceed the period of the reissued Visa

Any offers of work must also be consistent with the terms of any applicable industrial instrument that will apply to the team member in question (for example, the General Retail Industry Award 2010 or Kmart Australia Ltd Agreement 2012, or any successor instruments).

Obligations of Team members responsible for engaging other workers such as contractors

Workers engaged directly by Kmart

If a worker is being engaged directly by Kmart, the same process as set out above (for the recruitment of employees) must be followed.

Workers engaged through a third party labour hire/employment agency

If Kmart uses a third party (e.g.: a labour hire or employment agency) to engage any workers, Kmart must ensure that any workers who will perform work at Kmart under the arrangement have the right to work in Australia.

The applicable Store Manager or Line Manager must:

❖ request the labour hire or employment agency to confirm the worker's right to work in Australia, by providing relevant documentation including a copy of the applicable Visa;

❖ check any documentation provided by the labour hire or employment agency, and provide a copy of the same to Human Resources;

❖ ensure that any contract Kmart has with the labour hire or employment agency to provide the worker contains a clause that requires the agency to warrant that any workers supplied to Kmart:

❖ have valid and current working rights in Australia; and

❖ are not subject to any work limitations that would prevent them from performing the work required to be performed by Kmart.

Stores should seek advice from Human Resources where third party agencies seek to provide workers to Kmart, before engaging any workers

Obligations of team members responsible for overseeing ongoing employment or engagement

The following people are responsible for overseeing the ongoing employment of team members and/or engagement of workers in order to ensure compliance with this Policy and Australian migration laws:

❖ Store Level – applicable Store Administrative Manager (SAM); and
❖ Head Office – applicable Line Manager

Monitoring a team member's right to work in Australia during the course of their employment with Kmart

If a Visa Holder has been hired, Kmart must ensure that:

❖ the Visa Holder maintains their right to work in Australia throughout the course of their employment; and
❖ the work the Visa Holder performs complies with any work limitations specified in their Visa;
❖ all appropriate enquiries regarding the renewal of the Visa are made before the Visa expires, including finding out what limitations apply to the reissued Visa.

VEVO checks may be required to be undertaken periodically to ensure a Visa Holder continues to have a right to work, or to ensure that any work conditions placed on the Visa Holder have not changed

Generally, periodic VEVO checks will only be required to confirm the current working rights of foreign nationals (e.g.: who are not Australian or New Zealand citizens or Australian Permanent Residents) and will be conducted on a 3-month basis. SAMs and Line Managers should contact Human Resources if a periodic VEVO check is required.

If a Visa Holder's Visa is due to expire, SAMs and Line Managers should seek advice from Human Resources prior to the expiry date of the Visa. If a Visa is not reissued, it is likely the Visa Holder's employment will be terminated.

A Visa Holder must not be allowed to continue working once their Visa expires.

Further, if work limitations change under a renewed Visa, the Visa Holder must not be allowed to work contrary to those changed limitations.

If a Visa is reissued, it must be sighted by the applicable SAM or Line Manager and a copy of the Visa must be taken and retained on the team member's file.

Obligations of all team members WHO Are visa holders

To ensure compliance with Australian migration laws, Kmart may from time to time conduct periodic checks of working rights for existing team members, including by requesting that team members provide proof of their right to work in Australia.
Any team members who are requested by Kmart to provide evidence of their right to work in Australia are required to comply and cooperate fully with such requests, this includes but is not limited to providing their consent to a VEVO check.
Visa Holders, must immediately report any changes to their Visa status or any work
