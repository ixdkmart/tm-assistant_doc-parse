Service Desk
Returns: Mobile Phones Quick Reference Card

DOA = Dead on Arrival: Phone not working at all when purchased
ELF = Early Life Failure: Phone has failed after working initially

[Carrier/Brand Information across top row]
OPTUS | TELSTRA/BOOST MOBILE | VODAFONE | ROADHOUND (unlocked phones) | MI (Redmi phones)

DOA / ELF Period:
- OPTUS: 2 years from purchase (all returns)
- TELSTRA/BOOST: 30 days from purchase
- VODAFONE: 28 days from purchase
- ROADHOUND: 30 days from purchase
- MI: 30 days from purchase

Warranty:
All carriers/brands: 2 years from purchase

Important checks:
Are there signs of physical damage such as scratches, cracked or broken screens, or liquid damage? Are there signs of unauthorised repairs or tampering? Are any accessories and manuals missing?
If the answer to any of the above questions is yes, the mobile phone cannot be returned.
Inform the customer that any data on the phone must be backed up and removed prior to the return, as phone data may be lost.

[Service Desk Team Process section]
For each carrier, detailed steps are provided for handling returns both within and outside the DOA/ELF period, including:
- Checking original receipt
- Assessing fault status
- Photographing documentation
- Completing required forms
- Sending faulty phones to Returns & Repairs area

[RIO Team Process section]
Detailed processes for Returns & Repairs area handling, including:

- Contact information for carriers
