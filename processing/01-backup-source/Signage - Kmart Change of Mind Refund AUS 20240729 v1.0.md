Change of Mind Refunds

Our returns policy is offered in addition to your rights under the Australia Consumer Law, because we want you to be happy with your purchase. If you wish to return an item you have purchased because you have changed your mind, Kmart will offer you either an exchange voucher or refund back to the original method of payment, provided that:

• You return the item/s within 60 days of purchase.
• You produce your original Kmart receipt or adequate proof of purchase (see policy for full list).
• The item is in re-saleable condition (in original packaging, unworn/unused/unassembled with original ticketing).
• The product is not one where a change of mind is not available.

Goods on which change of mind return is not available:
• Cosmetics (if the hygiene seal is broken)
• Beauty products
• Fragrances
• Hosiery
• Underwear (excluding Bra's)
• Swimwear
• Pierced jewellery
• Printer cartridges
• Personalised printed material
• Bedding and accessories (where the seal and/or packaging has been opened)
• Tents (where the bag has been opened or components have been used)
• Trampolines & furniture (that has been opened or components have been used/assembled)
• Gift cards, phone recharge or third-party gift cards
• Food items

To ensure you are fully aware of your consumer rights and our obligations to you, please visit:
kmart.com.au/returns-policy
to view the full policy.
