Policy - Kmart Workwear Clothing Discount Australia

INTRODUCTION & PURPOSE
The Kmart Workwear Clothing Discount ("discount") provides store and distribution centre (DC) team members with a 20 per cent discount, for purchases of approved Kmart workwear.

The Team Member Discount Card and Kmart Uniform policies should be read in conjunction with this policy.

The approved list of workwear and process to apply the discount are set out in this policy.

ELIGIBILITY
Salaried store and DC team members are eligible from commencement.
Wages store and DC team members are eligible after passing three months' employment.
State support functions are not eligible for the discount.

COUNTRY APPLICATION
Australia

USAGE & ENTITLEMENT
The discount is for the purchase of approved workwear only, for team members' personal use. Purchasing for family or friends is prohibited under this policy.

Purchases made under this policy require prior approval by a member of the store leadership team, along with an active team member discount card.

Store team members can only receive their discount at their store work location. DC team members can receive their discount at the stores local to their DC, as specified in this policy.

APPROVED KMART WORKWEAR
The below inclusions list sets out the clothing and footwear that is approved for the workwear discount, in alignment with the Kmart Uniform Policy. Items in the exclusions list are not eligible for the workwear discount but a team member may receive a 10% discount on these items if they hold a team member discount card.

Inclusions:
• Chinos – black, navy or beige
• Business Skirts* – black, navy or beige
• Business Shorts* – black, navy or beige
• Denim Jeans – black, beige or mid to dark navy
• Underwear / Socks
• Black Footwear – Toes and heel fully enclosed (no heels)
• Safety Footwear
• Black Cardigans and Jumpers

Exclusions:
• Sleepwear
• Jewellery / Handbags / Sunglasses
• Hats / Caps / Hair Accessories
• Cosmetics / Personal care
• Activewear (except all black active footwear)
• Jackets
• Belts / Scarves
• Long/Short Sleeve Tops and Shirts
• Singlets / Hoodies
• Non-black jumpers / Cardigans / Vests / Windcheaters
• Dresses

*Skirts and shorts must be of a style and length suitable for the business environment and the work performed in stores, in line with the Kmart Uniform Policy.

APPROVAL AND PROCESSING
Team members must complete a Clothing Offer Approval Slip (obtained from a member of store leadership), including quantities and descriptions of what is being purchased using the discount. The slip must be presented to a member of the store leadership team to verify items are on the inclusion list. All items purchased using the discount must receive prior approval from a member of the store leadership team (excluding Duty / Extended Trade Managers). DC Team members can show the store manager their ID badge and team member discount card as proof of their eligibility.

To ensure the discount is processed correctly at POS, all transactions must be completed at a Living Desk register. To assist stores in processing the discount, a Type '2' / Team Member Clothing Offer override is established to automatically calculate 20 per cent off the purchase price of each approved item.

The standard 10 per cent team member discount does not apply in addition to the 20 per cent workwear clothing discount. However, items that are excluded are still eligible for the 10 per cent team member discount, where a team member has a team member discount card, subject to the team member discount policy.

FAQ
Does a team member need to make the purchase from their own store?
• Yes, the Kmart Workwear Clothing Discount only applies in the store that a team member works (or a local store local to the DC for DC team members).
• In the event a required item required is not available in the team member's store, an ITC from another store can be organised.
• Any returns of purchases made under this policy, should also be returned to the team member's own store.
• The 10 per cent Team Member Discount can be activated at any Kmart or Target store

If a team member shops at Target will they receive 20 per cent off the same workwear items/inclusions?
• Kmart team members with a team member discount card can obtain a 10 per cent discount of Target apparel and products online or in-store. They also receive 10 per cent off Kmart non-uniform apparel and other products online and in-store.

Can team members buy uniform items in the inclusion list for family and friends?
• The 20 per cent uniform discount is for personal use only. Other items can be purchased for the team member's immediate household at Kmart or Target using the general team member discount.

Can team members utilise their discount when not rostered?
• Yes, team members can utilise their uniform discount any time the store is open.

Can team members receive their discount when I am on a leave of absence?
• Yes, team members on leave of absence can continue to use their uniform discount.

Do team members have to purchase clothing and footwear for a specific gender under the Kmart Workwear Clothing Discount policy?
There are no restrictions relating to team members purchasing clothing aligning to their gender identity. Kmart encourages team members to purchase uniform that they feel most comfortable in.

What if a team member does not have their team member discount card with them?
• Team members must present their Team Member Discount Card for the transaction to commence and apply the 20 per cent discount.
• The discount card MUST be the team member's personal card.
• The generic Kmart discount card or another team member's discount card is NOT to be used if the team member has forgotten, lost or misplaced their own discount card as it does not have the team member's name on either card.

What if a team member has worked for less than 3 months and does not have a team member discount card?
• The Kmart Clothing Offer is only applicable for team members that have passed three months' employment and received their team member discount card.
• At managers discretion, for team members who have not yet obtained a team member discount card but have passed three months' service, the discount may be offered, with all details of the team member's transaction and name to be recorded on the provided spreadsheet.
• At the end of each year, stores will be required to provide specific team member details from the information to National Office for Fringe Benefit Tax (FBT) report purposes.

What if a team member chooses an approved item and it does not calculate 20 per cent off?
• The Operator is to seek approval from a member of the Management Team that the item meets the Kmart Workwear Clothing criteria.
• The operator is to then process the item as a Type '9' override and enter the discount rate of 11% (this rate is required in order to calculate the discount, and 10 per cent team member discounts to equal 20 per cent at POS). As the Team Member Discount WILL apply on this item, the remaining 10 per cent will be deducted at the end of the transaction, making up the full 20 per cent.
• Please Note: In order to record the total of the transaction for FBT purposes Type 2 is to be used in every instance. Type 9 should only be used in exceptional circumstances where an item has not deducted the 20 per cent.
• The Store Manager is to review the Override Report the next day and provide an explanation for both Type 2 and 9, as per current process.

Can team members purchase Clearance/Quit Cycle applicable clothing items?
• Yes, team members can purchase Clearance and Quit Cycle items under this policy.

Can team members use their FlyBuys Card?
• Yes, team members can scan their FlyBuys card when making transactions under this policy.
