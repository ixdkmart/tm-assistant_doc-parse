Merchandise Protection What Good Looks Like

Merch Protection Station/Cage

All products listed in the Merchandise Protection Standards Spreadsheet must not be placed onto the shop floor without merchandise protection. It should be applied in the back of house area from your MPS cage/station.

Merch Protection Station / Cage Setup

Station:
• Print and laminate RLO signage (what good looks like, standards etc). and place on the wall above the bench for team members to reference.
• Use plastic crates in lower shelves to store spider wraps, tags etc.

Cage:
• Middle shelf in place with a flat cardboard box to use as a space to place the product while attaching equipment.
• Print and laminate RLO signage (what good looks like, standards etc). and cable tie to the upper sides of the cage so team members can reference.
• Use plastic crates in lower shelves to store spider wraps, tags etc.

Storage & Standards for Merch Protection Equipment

It is important to take care of the Merch Protection equipment after each use, and store correctly. This ensures it is in working condition, and easy to apply to the next product.

Spider Wrap Storage

The Correct Way:
• Wind after each use to easily access & apply for next use.

The Incorrect Way:
• If you do not wind Spider Wraps after use, they become a tangled mess!

Applying Merch Protection Equipment

All products listed in the Merchandise Protection Standards Spreadsheet must not be placed onto the shop floor without merchandise protection. It should be applied in the back of house area from your MPS cage/station.

Applying Merch Protection Equipment to Products

SPIDER WRAPS
The Correct Way:
• Covers all side of the product.
• Is locked & secure so it cannot be pulled off.

The Incorrect Way:
• DO NOT leave the Spider Wrap on too loose.
• Spider Wrap cover all sides of the product.

HARD TAGS
The Correct Way:
• Place within the seam of a product, so not to damage the material.

AUTO PEGS
The Correct Way:
• Not to be placed onto barcodes.
• Not to be placed over product information.

EAS TAGS (LEGO)
The Correct Way:
• On the front of the box, at the bottom right side
• Stickers placed flat without bending.

The Incorrect Way:
• Not to be placed onto barcodes.
• Not to be placed over product information.

PLEASE NOTE:
• For Lego that displays the recommended age at the bottom right corner of the box, place the EAS sticker at the bottom right side of the box, just above the age.
• For Lego collectors keep boxes without EAS labels in the receiving area & take to a manned POS register to process the sale.

Merch Protection Specific Products

Suitcases
The Correct Way
• Use hard tags and loop through the zipper tag holes (not through fabric of the suitcase).
• For hard suitcases, use a Spiderwrap.
