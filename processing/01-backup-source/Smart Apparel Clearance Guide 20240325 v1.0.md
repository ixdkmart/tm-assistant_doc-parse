Smart Apparel Clearance Guide

This guide steps through the process for apparel clearance to increase clearance sell through percentage, and ability to easily merchandise these items onto the shop floor, whist promoting a strong price message to promote to customers with minimal REM investment.

Clearance prices will scan from 4:15pm on the day prior to the clearance effective date (Mondays). It is suggested stores complete the clearance markdowns prior to 9.00am Tuesday where possible to minimise disruptions to the morning sequence and other tasks.

The apparel clearance process also enables store to markdown the price of apparel in instance such as end of range and oddments, to assist in selling stock quickly and keeping a well-presented clothing department. The requirements are explained within this guide.

Process Updates
• All footwear departments (including men's, women's, kids, infants, and active footwear) have been removed from RBU clearance weeks and will be live in week 1 with Women's Accessories.
• Cosmetics (D20) and Personal Care (D85) have been removed from the cyclical apparel schedule and will follow GM clearance timings.
• Markdown cycle is aligned with calendar dates verse last year, whilst considering events e.g., Men's markdowns in line with Father's Day.
• Store Task Tool (STT) approximate hours required for apparel markdowns uploaded on the Thursday prior to the RBU markdown week.
• Clear definition for 'End of Range' and 'Oddments' and process updates for Markdown Function now available on PDT.

       | • PDTs

In the instance where stocktake is held in the same week, complete the markdown process after all stock has been counted and investigated.

Markdown Optimisation
Markdown optimisation is a tool used by the merchandise team to choose the optimum price to sell clearance items at the highest margin while clearing the stock quickly. This often results in styles being marked down by climatic region, meaning different states may have different prices. When this occurs, the status changes to clearance for all states.

Whilst check scanning, if you notice a style that is on clearance, but has not had a price change, there is no requirement to price or shelf talker this item

After Clearance - Consolidate & Clear Process

Complete the below ongoing, after clearance markdowns have been actioned. Do not take any additional reductions until the following Monday from original clearance scanning date. Only apply to clearance scanning products.

Step | Action
-----|--------
Step 1 | As stock sells through, start to consolidate single price pointed product to ends and the last module of a gondola.
Info | Six ways should only be used as an exception.
Step 2 | Change ticket frames and move/add generic price shelf talkers as necessary
Step 3 | Continue to consolidate product as required, and eventually move to the back of the department when volumes are depleted. This will enable new stock to be placed in the relevant departments on arrival
Step 4 | Use the End of Range (EOR) process as styles sell down within departments and price with a pathfinder or PDT and printer using the Markdown function
Step 5 | Merchandise EOR using a back end if possible, or in the last module on a gondola using a single apparel arm
Step 6 | If two styles per single apparel arm can't be achieved, use a 600mm side arm within the gondola
Step 7 | Generic clearance shelf talkers need to be applied where necessary.

End of Range Process
End of Range (EOR): The last three units or reduced sizing range (three sizes or less available) of a Clearance scanning product. When actioning EOR in store, the following steps need to be applied:

Step 1a
Check that the style/keycode/option scans as clearance
*EOR only applies to products that scan as clearance and is not to be applied to full priced scanning products.

Step 1b
There will be occasions when a clearance line will have 'Stock on Order'. If this occurs, it is advised not to apply further price reduction with the pathfinder. This may lead to price confusion for the customer when the stock on order or stock in transit arrives and hasn't had a pathfinder label applied with the reduced price

Info
Prior to actioning any price adjustments, check SOH which may indicate stock may be merchandised at an off location or still in the stockroom which may alter you decision to adjust the price.

Step 2
Price with a pathfinder gun or PDT Markdown function.
It is recommended that when stores take their own in-store markdown on a clearance or full priced style / keycode that it is no more than 50% off the current scanning price.

Step 3a
1 style (max 2) per arm.
Single apparel arm with a generic clearance shelf talker

Oddments Process
Oddment: The last three units only, style is still on range, no further drops landing in store and is Non-Clearance product scanning at full price. When actioning an Oddment in store, the following steps need to be applied:

Step 1a
Using the PDT, Check that 'Stock on Hand' (SOH) is less than three across all sizes within the keycode/style.
Check there is no 'Stock on Order' (SOO) or 'Stock in Transit' (STT) for that keycode/style.

Info
If not further stock is due to arrive, and there is less than three units for that option, apply steps 2 – 3 for EOR to Oddments

Note: When a pathfinder is used for oddments, a generic 'Clearance Shelf Talker' can be used to display the reduced price. A flip ticket can be used where applicable with the 'clearance' insert being displayed.

Multi Price Point Fixture Process
Wherever possible, ends, 6-ways or modules must be price pointed. Price is one of the pillars of Kmart's strategy and a key to our success and must always be called out to the customer as a priority.

In some instances, as clearance merchandise sells through and is consolidated, it may no longer be possible to maintain price pointed fixtures. To help call out the clearance message in the absence of price, stores have the ability to utilise a 'Clearance' ticket frame insert.

The inserts are to be applied to the ticket frame under the following circumstances only:
• The entire back end, module or 6-way is clearance
• There is insufficient volume to create a price-pointed fixture and call the price out to the customer
• Items are still priced with a yellow pricing sticker
• Generic clearance shelf talkers are still applied to price pointed arms on the fixture, where possible
• Cannot be used for power tables

Pathfinder & Markdown App

There are two types of markdown functions that operate differently depending on the desired outcome; Manager Markdown / Fix Price Markdown.

It is recommended that the 'Fix Price Markdown' is used as the primary pathfinder option to override the scanning price of an apparel item.

The primary difference to the Manager Markdown function is due the Fix Price Markdown function having the ability to:
1. Set a determined price and then,
2. Scan multiple APN / Sizes within a style
3. Produce markdown stickers containing the APN with the override price for all sizes applicable
4. Correct SOH integrity adjustments for all sizes

Info:
It is often seen that stores will scan one size and print off multiple markdown stickers and apply to all sizes. This will result in all sales going against the one size which may impact reporting and future store allocations

When should I use this function?

The Fix Price function should be used in the following situations:

End of Range: Use the pathfinder to apply the new reduced price to clearance products with three or less units left or reduced sizing (three sizes or less available), particularly when making space for new lines.

Oddments: Use the pathfinder to apply the new reduced price to items where it is the last three units only, style is still on range, no further drops landing in store, and is non-clearance scanning at full price, particularly when making space for new lines.

PDT Store App Process

Stores must use the Pathfinder or Markdown App on a PDT when completing any in store, non-scanning price changes to mitigate SCO interventions and improve the customer experience. The Store Markdown' app has been added onto the PDT for managers to access in addition (or instead of) the pathfinder, with orange labels being utilised through a portable printer.

Step | Action
Step 1 | Open the 'Store Markdown' function.
Step 2 | Select a portable printer, ensuring orange labels have been loaded.
Step 3 | Select the markdown reason; For managing apparel EOR and Oddments, 'Last 3 Items' should be selected.
Step 4 | Enter the required 'Markdown Price'.
Step 5 | Scan the APN or enter the keycode and an orange label will be printed.

Info: If further items are scanned at this point, the markdown labels will be printed with the same reason code and markdown price.
To assist with data integrity and process, all apparel and footwear items will be defaulted to a print label of 1, therefore requiring each individual size to be scanned.

Info: For all instructions on how to use the Store Markdown function, the guide can be located on Kit via: Resources > Stores > Operations > Back Office > System Releases > PDT Store Markdown Labels
