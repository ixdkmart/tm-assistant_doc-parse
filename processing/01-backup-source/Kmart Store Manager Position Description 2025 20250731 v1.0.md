Store Manager
Position Description

As Australia's largest and most loved retailer, we've been brightening the everyday lives of families for over 50 years. With stores, distribution centres, and offices across Australia, New Zealand, and beyond, our 47,000-strong diverse and inclusive team works together to offer families on-trend products at incredibly low prices. We're results-driven and evolving rapidly, offering challenging work you can be proud of. At Kmart, we value your uniqueness because it's not just what you do, but who you are, that makes everyday living brighter.

Position Title: Store Manager
Function: Stores
Reports To: Zone Manager
Level of Role: Leader of Leaders/Senior Manager
Years of Experience Required for Role: 3-5 years
GRIA Level: Retail Employee level 8

Kmart Store Manager's play a critical role in driving store performance and team success by leading operational excellence, delivering exceptional customer experiences, and developing high-performing teams. As a key leader, you are responsible for maintaining compliance, managing financial outcomes, and creating a positive work culture that aligns with the organisation's strategic goals. You lead by example, balancing day-to-day priorities with long-term growth and development.

Position Relationships:
Key Internal Stakeholders: Line Managers, Team Members, State Support Team, Distribution Centers, National Office Teams
Key External Stakeholders: 3rd Party Providers, Customers, Centre Management, Government Officials
Number of Direct Reports: 6+
Number of Indirect Reports: 50+

Experience and Expertise:
• Store Manager's are skilled in motivating, developing and leading large teams while maintaining a strong focus on values, behaviors and effective communication.
• Leading with a proactive and solutions focused mindset with the ability to think strategically, interpret data and respond swiftly to changing business and customer demands.
• Have a proven ability to build strong relationships at all levels, managing expectations and collaborating effectively as a team player.
• Strong attention to detail, supported by excellent planning skills and the ability to prioritise multiple tasks while maintaining store productivity and efficiency.
• Remain composed under pressure, able to adapt to challenging situations and maintain a high level of self-control.
• Remain open to feedback, focused on self-improvement and driven to identify opportunities to enhance team and business performance.

Technical Ability:
• Excel in data-driven decision-making by utilising analytical tools (Power BI, Excel) to forecast sales, manage budgets and optimise store performance.
• Have strong leadership and change management skills, with a focus on team-building and effective communication to foster a positive work culture.
• Demonstrate solid commercial acumen, including financial analysis, store budgeting, forecasting and strategic planning.
• Manage and deliver wage budgets while ensuring employee entitlements are met under GRIA and KNA.
• Uphold operational excellence by efficiently managing stock, scheduling, compliance and ensuring adherence to store procedures and safety standards.
• Ensure store processes are implemented and driven aligned to business expectations.

Position Dimensions:
Budget Accountability: Site Location: Annual Store Budget, EBIT Outcome, Remuneration Outcome, Store Shrink

Capabilities required in role:

Drives Results | Pushes others to achieve results. Leads others to persist despite setbacks or obstacles. Fosters a sense of urgency in the team for reaching goals and meeting deadlines. Drives a track record of success for the team.

Builds Effective Teams | Coaches others on how to build effective teams. Communicates clear goals and roles to team members. Ensures that the team has the right mix of skills and leverages the strengths of individual members. Rewards and recognises team efforts and accomplishments.

Commerciality | Educates others on the importance of complying with budget requirements. Holds people accountable for making good financial decisions. Makes sense of financial data and its implications for the business. Uses relevant financial and quantitative indicators to measure business performance.

Ensures Accountability | Accepts responsibility for successes and opportunities in own work and the team's work. Holds self and team accountable for outcomes (e.g. achieving goals and complying with policies and procedures). Monitors metrics and milestones to chart progress against expectations and accountabilities. Designs feedback processes into the work.

Strives for Improvement | Analyses problems and process breakdowns to ensure that improvements are made. Focuses others on driving continuous improvement and quality outcomes. Employs systems, processes, and workflows that help others deliver results without intensive managerial involvement. Optimises and integrates large-scale systems to improve quality and service.
