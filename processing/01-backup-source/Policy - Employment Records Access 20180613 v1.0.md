Policy - Employment Records Access

INTRODUCTION AND PURPOSE
Kmart is committed to handling and managing employment records in accordance with the Privacy Act's National Privacy Principles wherever possible, despite the fact that most of the company's practices in this area would be technically exempt under the Act.

This approach is designed to ensure that team members receive a similar level of consideration and protection as contractors, customers and other individuals.

POLICY APPLICATION
This policy applies to all Kmart team members.

COUNTRY APPLICATION
Australia

POLICY
Records Access

Kmart recognises that team members may seek access to employment records. Some team members may access some employment records through SAP HR (Team Member Tools).

A team member currently employed by Kmart may access his/her employment-related records subject to the exceptions below.

A team member may not access employment records where access would:
• have an unreasonable impact on the privacy of others;
• be inappropriate as the information relates to legal proceedings or advice;
• be inappropriate as the information would reveal commercially sensitive decision-making processes or information regarding the company's negotiations with the individual;
• prejudice investigations or ongoing management of investigation outcomes;
• otherwise not adhere to the National Privacy Principles;
• cause the company to be in breach of any legal obligations or restrictions (for example, workers compensation legislation, health records legislation, confidentiality requirements or other contractual obligations); or
• prejudice the interests of any Kmart company or team member.

An exception applies if the law requires Kmart to provide access.

If a request for access is denied, the reason will be explained to the team member concerned.

Team members may require any inaccurate or out-of-date records to be updated. However, the company is under no obligation to destroy or delete records in most cases, as the majority of employment records need to be kept for significant periods for legal and business reasons.

Requests by external parties for access to or copies of employment records must be in writing and information may only be provided in accordance with the law and Kmart policies and procedures.

Sorting the Records into Restricted or Accessible

Prior to meeting with the team member who has requested access, the line manager must sort the file to ensure that the team member only accesses records in accordance with the Employment Records Access Policy.

The table in the Appendix to this policy indicates where the team member has access to view or have a photocopy made for them. Note that team member files comprise different materials and not all files contain the same types of information.

If restricted information forms part of document that also contains accessible information:

• and the document is in hardcopy, the restricted information must be properly obscured (cover over the restricted information prior to copying) and the document copied before being shown to the team member;

• and the document is electronic, the accessible data must be copied into a separate document, or the restricted data temporarily removed or obscured if it is necessary for the team member to see a copy of the original document.

Copying Employment Records

Team members are able to photocopy or print their records. In some cases, team members will only be able to have copies or extracts from part of a document in order to protect the confidentiality or privacy of other information in the same document.

External Inquiry

Requests by third parties (for example, spouses or other family members, Centrelink, solicitors, police, government departments) for access to employment records must be in writing.

Access may only be granted in accordance with the law (for example, where a subpoena has been issued) and in accordance with relevant company policies and procedures.

In an emergency situation, limited employment details may be provided to the police (at least at the level of Duty Sergeant or equivalent), provided that strict confidentiality is maintained.

Return to File

Hardcopy files must be returned to the source within three days of team member access.
