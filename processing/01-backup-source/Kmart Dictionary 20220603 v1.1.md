Term | Definition

PPE
Personal Protective Equipment | Any designated item of clothing or equipment that employees wear or use to limit their exposure to hazards and reduce the risk of injury.

PIR | Post Implementation Review

PP
Pick N Pack | A code that may appear on a Shelf Edge Label that refers to the source of supply.

PO
Purchase Order | A record of agreement made with a supplier that includes a detailed list of retail items, cost discount terms and method of delivery.

POS
Point Of Sale | The physical location within a store at which a sale is or may be made, i.e., cash register. This term can also be used for signage.

POS System
Point Of Sale System | An automated information system that supports point of sale operations. Normally, this would include the recording of sales transactions and may include register balancing, automatic price look up, price checking and other functions.

POS $ or POS % | The profit made from the sale of merchandise. POS $ refers to the dollar profit made and POS % refers to the percentage or the sale that was profit.

Power Tables | Kmart's most profitable fixture, selling high volume basic apparel clothing.

Price Point | A sell price that applies to one or more merchandise items.

P2T
Push to Talk | A portable radio system used by team members and managers for customer service and replenishment purposes.

Product Recall | A product recall requires all stock to be removed from show and labelled not for sale due to an issue that may result in a safety hazard for the customer. A product recall becomes the first priority of the business when initiated.

Product Withdrawal | A product withdrawal also requires all stock to be removed from show and labelled not for sale, however a withdrawal means that the product has a non-safety related issue with it. For example, a supplier may withdraw its product because the taste and quality of the product is not as it should be.

Promotional Offer | The advertising of a product or service.

S

Term | Definition

Seasonal Clearance Markdown | A reduction to the regular price initiated to facilitate seasonal stock clearance.

SCM
Shipping Container Marking | The SCM contains textual and barcode information, which uniquely identifies each carton and cross- references carton contents back to the ASN. Each SCM must be unique within the ASN and when scanned must cross reference back to the ASN.

SCO/SSCO | Self Service Checkout (pronounced "sko")

SDA
Shop, Distributor & Allied
Employees Association | Retail employee union.

SDP
Shop Down Pallet | A pallet which arrives to store merchandised and is normally placed near checkouts.

Sell Price | The price at which an item of merchandise is to be sold to a customer.

SEL
Shelf Edge Label | A label attached to a shelf, basket or peg within a store that describes various attributes of the merchandise. A shelf edge label displays the regular "sell" price to the customers.

Sequence | The process which follows decant (see above) and involves replenishing the shopfloor with the stock that has arrived in the last 24 hours.

Showcase | Glass cabinet used for displaying high theft potential merchandise.

Shrinkage | An actual loss of stock or sales dollars due to error or fraud, or a calculated loss of stock due to inaccuracies in stock on hand record.

SIT
Stock In Transit | Merchandise distributed from the dispatch centre (DC) that is currently due to be delivered to a store.

SMIT
Store Manager in Training | Line Managers who are undertaking a training program to become a Store Manager. Also known as the stretch program.

SOH
Stock On Hand | An amount of stock, measured in either units or dollars, which is physically situated at a specific location, or set of locations, at a particular point in time.

SOO
Stock On Order | Merchandise that is currently in on order, but not yet delivered to stores.

SRP | Shelf Ready Package

SRS
Store Receiving System | A computer system used by stores to enter data for merchandise received as a direct-to-store delivery or items delivered from DCs.
