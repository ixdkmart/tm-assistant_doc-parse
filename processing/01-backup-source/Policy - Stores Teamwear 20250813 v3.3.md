Policy – Stores Team Wear

INTRODUCTION
AND PURPOSE
The purpose of this Policy is to outline expectations regarding dress standards for Team Members and Managers who are employed to work in stores.

This Policy sets out the expected standards of presentation that must be followed to ensure Kmart Team Members and Managers always present a professional and business-like appearance.

This Policy is intended to ensure:

• Team Members are readily identifiable;
• Customers can expect a consistent, identifiable look for the business; and
• Our Team Members are presented in a professional manner; and
• Team Members are comfortably and safely dressed for the work they perform.

POLICY APPLICATION
This Policy applies to all Kmart store Team Members and Managers working in Australia and New Zealand stores. This Policy also applies to non-stores team Members who are working at or a visiting a Kmart store for a work-related purpose. The requirements for Team Members working in other areas of the business are set out in Kmart's Personal Presentation Policy.

Where a Team Member is covered by an enterprise agreement or award, and the enterprise agreement or award provides for a more beneficial entitlement than set out in this policy, the applicable terms of the enterprise agreement or award will apply in the case of any inconsistency.

COUNTRY APPLICATION
Australia and New Zealand

POLICY
Kmart's Team Wear

Tops with the Kmart logo are provided to Team Members free of charge and must be worn by Team Members. Team Members may choose from the following range of options:

• A long sleeve gingham shirt;
• A short sleeve gingham shirt; or
• A short sleeve polo top.

A combination of these shirts may be issued in accordance with the allocations set out in this Policy.

Polar Fleece Tops

Either a polar fleece jumper or vest with the Kmart logo will be issued to Team Members working in stores located in cold locations. All other team Members will be able to request a polar fleece jumper or vest free of charge.

Team Members must ensure that if they are wearing undergarments beneath a Kmart top that the undergarment is not visible. Long-sleeved undergarments cannot be worn under a short-sleeved Kmart top.

Allocation of Kmart tops
• Full-time Team Members are issued with a total of 5 tops;
• Part-time Team Members are issued with a total of 4 tops;
• Casual Team Members are issued with a total of 2 tops.

Team Members may select any combination of the polo and long and short-sleeved tops above. Items will be replaced if subject to reasonable wear and tear.

Preferred Bottoms

Kmart does not supply bottoms, however, there are a range of bottoms that can be worn with Kmart tops:

• Black, navy or beige pants or chinos (pants can be 3/4 length); or
• Business skirt in black, navy or beige skirt (no denim); or
• Business shorts in black, navy or beige (no denim); or
• Denim jeans (beige, black or mid to dark navy in colour and can be 3/4 length).

Skirts and shorts must be of a style and appropriate length suitable for the business environment and the work performed in stores, with no holes or fraying.

Bottoms can be purchased from Kmart however Team Members are not required to do so and may wear their own bottoms.

Socks must be plain black, navy or white and have no holes or fraying

Shoes

All Team Members must wear black footwear, except for 'Safety Footwear' as set out below in this Policy.

Footwear must have the toes and heel fully enclosed and have non-slip rubber soles that provide suitable padding and arch support. Shoes must have a low or wedged heel suitable for the business environment. The following types of footwear are not acceptable: open toe/open back, sandals/thongs/slides, kung fu slipper type shoes, high heels, canvas shoes and ballet flats.

Runners may be worn if they are solid black with no marks and minimal branding. Runners must have non-slip rubber soles that provide suitable padding and arch support.

Safety Footwear

Safety footwear must be worn for the specific tasks that are listed in the Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Matrices for back of house (BOH)/stockroom and the Selling Floor. Safety footwear required under the Australian and New Zealand safety standards is steel capped boots or a composite safety shoe or boot. Note, a composite safety shoe is a safety shoe that has passed the required impact and pressure tested to meet Australian and New Zealand standards, however, it is a lighter shoe made from alternative materials. Approved safety footwear in a colour other than black is acceptable.

Where safety footwear must be worn as per the PPE Matrices, Kmart will provide a Team Member with steel capped boots/shoes or a composite safety shoe or boot. If a Team Member wishes to purchase their own safety footwear, for Australian Team Members, Kmart will reimburse them to a total maximum value of $80.00. For New Zealand Team members, reimbursement amounts, and eligibility is outlined in the Kmart New Zealand/First Union Collective Employment Agreement.

The Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) procedure should also be read in conjunction with this policy.

Gloves

Gloves will be supplied and made available by Kmart for all Team Members. Gloves will be minimum Cut 3 resistance in line with Australian and New Zealand standards and can be stored on a Team Member using a glove clip.

Safety glasses

Safety glasses will be supplied by Kmart for all Team Members who are using the compactor machine. Safety glasses will be in line with Australian and New Zealand standards and should be always worn during the compactor process.

Appropriate sun protection (sunscreen & hat) should be worn by Team Members performing regular work outside of Kmart premises (e.g. Team Members collecting trolleys). Sunscreen will be made available to relevant Team Members as required.

Customer Service Vest and Hi-Vis wear

For those required to wear customer service vests these are to be worn over the Kmart uniform. In stores where Team Members are working around the receiving bay and/or interacting with vehicles because there is no dock leveller, a hi-vis vest or hi-vis t-shirt must be worn at all times, whilst undertaking these duties. The hi-vis vest must always be worn over the Kmart uniform top when working in traffic areas. The hi-vis vest is provided by Kmart.

In hot climate locations, and if necessary for the comfort of the Team Member, a hi-vis t-shirt will be supplied. The Team Member may wear the t-shirt instead of the Kmart uniform top and hi-vis vest.

Hi-vis clothing and reflective stripes are to meet relevant Australian and New Zealand standards.

Belts

Any belts worn must be black.

Name Badges

To assist customers and Team Members to be able to easily identify Kmart Team Members on the shop floor all Team Members must wear their name badge at all times and in all areas of the store. The name badge is to be worn at chest level on the left-hand side in a manner easily readable. Name badges are to be kept in good condition and clearly visible. Team Members are not permitted to modify badges, add promotional under-badges or stickers unless authorised by the business.

Toolkit

The blue Kmart utility belt may be worn with the uniform.

Exemption to wearing Kmart's Team Wear

From time to time, to support and promote specific events such as wearing purple for 'Wear it Purple Day' or wearing yellow for 'R U Ok? Day', Kmart may permit Team Members to wear clothing other than the Team Wear prescribed in this Policy. Kmart will specify events or days where this is permitted. On these days, any clothing and accessories must continue to meet prescribed safety standards and always be neat, professional and appropriate for the work environment.

General presentation standards

Kmart's dress standards are important in making us easily recognisable to customers, promoting our team atmosphere and ensuring that our store team always look great. It is important that Team Members present themselves in a business-like manner, supportive of Kmart's desired team image as set out by this policy.

Team Members must always maintain high personal hygiene standards when at work and must comply with any public safety standards, such as health regulations and other applicable laws.

Clothing items must be maintained appropriately. This includes not wearing shabby or dirty clothing and Kmart tops and preferred bottoms being ironed appropriately.

Hair, beards, and moustaches

Hair and any beards or moustaches should be:
• Clean, neat and well-groomed;
• Business-like; and
• Practical and safe for the work environment

This helps ensure that our Team Members perform their work safely and represent Kmart professionally to our customers and others we interact with at work.

Jewellery, body piercing and tattoos

Should be kept to a minimum, be practical for the work environment and allow Team Members to perform their work safely. Studs are preferred to rings to mitigate the risk of injury.

Acrylic nails/nail extensions

Should be practical for the work environment and allow Team Members to perform their work safely.

Earphones and headphones

The use of earphones and headphones, including ear pods and air pods, whilst working in any part of the store is a safety risk and is prohibited, unless required for a work purpose. Company provided 'Push to Talks' is the only acceptable form of headset to be used by duties in store.

Line Managers responsibilities:

Line Managers must:
• ensure all Team Members comply with this Policy.
• reinforce this Policy to all new Team Members during the onboarding process and store discovery.
• review options with a Team Member who believes they have a legitimate reason not to comply with this Policy (the need to review options may be the result of an injury or illness or if the adherence of this Policy could be discriminatory for the Team Member concerned because of a protected attribute).
• coach a Team Member who does not maintain professional personal standards required by this Policy (unless such coaching would be discriminatory).

• take appropriate disciplinary action where a Team Member consistently fails to comply with this Policy (unless such disciplinary action would be discriminatory); and

• assess and manage legitimate claims of reimbursement for damage to personal clothing incurred during the course of employment.

Assessing and Managing Claims for Damage to Personal Clothing

It is preferable to clean or repair damaged clothing where items of personal clothing are unavoidably damaged in the course of work. Replacement may be considered more appropriate where it is deemed to be more economical or repairing/cleaning the item would not return it to the condition immediately before being damaged.

Damage to clothing must:
• occur during work hours;
• not be caused by normal wear and tear;
• not be the Team Member's fault.

A Team Member will not be reimbursed for any damage to their clothing where it can be shown that the Team Member failed to wear supplied protective clothing which could have prevented the damage to the Team Member's clothing. Reimbursement does not apply to Kmart provided clothing or footwear. If the damage to clothing and property is related to a workplace injury, the associated costs to cover the damage may, in some circumstances, be included in a workers compensation claim.

Breach of this Policy

Breach of this Policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment.
