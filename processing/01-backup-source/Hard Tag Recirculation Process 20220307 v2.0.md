Hard Tag Recirculation Process

Follow the below steps to return all hard tags and pins for recirculation.
This process is for hard tags/pins/steel flex string only, and is not applicable to other Loss Prevention materials such as Spiderwraps, AutoPegs and Security Cases.

Step 1
Set-up a 'extra small online box' and place 1 x 'Security Tags Enclosed' sticker onto the side of the carton. If a 'extra small online box' cannot be sourced, then substitute for another box no greater than 30cm x 30 cm x 30cm.
Note: It is important that these dimensions are adhered to comply with international transport requirements.

Step 2
At the registers / service desk / online processing area, sort hard tags & pins from spider-wraps, auto pegs, and other reusable tagging materials.
Tip: Register drawers can hold 2 x 10cm wide containers

Step 3
Transfer security tags and pins from service areas and place into prepared 30cm x 30xm x 30xm carton. Continue this process until carton is full.
Once full, seal the box with tape.

Step 4
Place carton on top of full coat hanger box, ensuring 'Security Tags Enclosed' sticker is facing outwards to be easily identifiable. Secure the box to the lid using tape to hold in place during transport.
Note: Only one box is to be placed onto each hanger box

Step 6
Backload to the DC, as per current process, ensuring you record on the W52 that a hanger pallet has been returned.

Hard Tag Recirculation Process

Below is a list of what can be returned, and what should be held in store to maintain merchandise protection standards

Return:
- Hard Tags (eg from product received tagged; Everlast, Work Boots, Outdoor Clothing)
- Hard Tag Pins (all types)
- Steel Flex Strings

Keep in Store:
- All Spider wraps
- Alarm Cablelock
- All Safers
- Plastic Lanyards
- Auto Pegs
