Incorrect Price Guide - Markdowns

All customer pricing enquiries should be assessed with a thorough review of the area where the product was located. The label and any other pricing signage displayed should be checked to ensure pricing is not misleading to the customer.

Step | Action

Step 1
- Check the area from where the product was selected. Attempt to identify any pricing labels/signage that could have caused customer confusion.
- Check that the description matches the product on the pricing label, or the item is placed or filled in a way that makes it clear to the customer that the label is for the item.

Scenario 1 - Pricing displayed was incorrect or misleading

Where there is a store error:
• The price displayed (label or other signage material) is lower than the scanned price; AND
• The description on the label/signage matches the product.

The business must honour the price until it is remedied.

Step | Action

Step 2 – Price difference under $10 and less than 5 units:
For Kmart: If the price difference is less than $10 and less than 5 units the team member must honour the lower price. The incorrect label and details should be attached to the override slips and reason code 6 'Price Difference' should be used for the markdown at checkouts. The label and/or price signage should be updated immediately.

For Target: If the price difference is less than $10 and less than 5 units the team member must honour the lower price. Apply reason code 11, 'Incorrect SPL' for price correction at checkout. The label and/or price signage should be updated immediately.

Step 2 – Price difference Over $10 or less than 5 units:
If the price difference is more than $10 or more than 5 units a manager will need to approve the markdown and further assess the situation to ensure the pricing was a store error and/or misleading. The manager may reach out to the Store Operation Support team or Zone Manager if there are unusual circumstances needing further guidance.

Scenario 2 - Pricing enquiry where price label/signage is correct and not misleading

Where there is no store error:
• Displayed product does not match the product description on the label/signage: AND
• The pricing signage displayed is not misleading.

The business does not have to honour the price if:
• The product has been placed at an incorrect location.
• The customer has seen a label for a different product and assumed it was for the item they were wanting to purchase.

Step | Action

Step 2 | Inform the customer of situation and findings and escalate to a manager for assistance if required.

Example-
[Image shows Stitch plush toys on display]
Customer queries the $79 price of the boxed item. Review confirms that the pricing displayed is not misleading. The label for the adjacent product has been moved slightly too far left. The $39 label is clearly for the larger Stitch item on the right.

Inform the customer of situation and escalate to a manager for assistance if required.
