LAY-BY REFUND AUTHORISATION

What is it for?
Lay-by refunds occur when a customer:

• Cancels a lay-by and obtains a refund for the deposit / payment(s) made;

• Obtains a refund of deposit / payment(s) on a lay-by that the store has been previously returned to stock; or

• The store processes an 'item price change', at the customer's request that causes the automatic finalisation of the lay-by and a partial refund to the customer.

What does it look like?
[Image shows a store accounting report with various labeled components:]

- Refunds Lay-by Dep / Pay & RTS can D/P (Progressive total) (D)
- Daily total
- Number of receipts that should be on hand (Progressive total)
- Total Refund amount for each Lay-by register (E)
- Signatures required (A), (B), (C)

What should I look for?

Where lay-by refunds are performed, the lay-by customer cancellation must be witnessed and another team member must be authorise (sign) the refund receipt. In addition, a customer signature must be obtained for all lay-by refunds as confirmation that the amount has been received.

A. STAFF - Team member's signature conducting the transaction.

B. AUTHORISED BY - Second team member's signature (at the time of the transaction) to verify transaction / refund was valid.

C. CUST SIGNATURE - Customer's signature to verify monies received.

Additionally, all Refunds processed are to be reconciled to the Store Accounting Totals Report (SATR):

D. Combined total of the 'Refunds: Lay-by Dep/Pay' and 'Refunds: RTS Can D/P' figures printed on the Store Accounting Totals Report for the lay-by registers.

E. Add the 'refund' amount on the lay-by receipts together to get the total amount refunded from each lay-by register.

Any variance between the lay-by refund receipts and the Store Accounting Totals report is to be investigated. If a variance is noted, check the 'Item price change' receipts for the week, to see if a price change receipt should be included in the layby receipts total.

For information on the reconciliation process, refer to the Refund Reconciliation process.
