Store Operations Manager
Position Description

As Australia's largest and most loved retailer, we've been brightening the everyday lives of families for over 50 years. With stores, distribution centres, and offices across Australia, New Zealand, and beyond, our 47,000-strong diverse and inclusive team works together to offer families on-trend products at incredibly low prices. We're results-driven and evolving rapidly, offering challenging work you can be proud of. At Kmart, we value your uniqueness because it's not just what you do, but who you are, that makes everyday living brighter.

Position Title | Function | Reports To | Level of Role | Years of Experience Required for Role | GRIA Level
---|---|---|---|---|---
Store Operations Manager | Stores | Store Manager | Line Manager | 3 - 5 years | Retail Employee level 7

Kmart Store Operation Managers play a critical role in driving store performance and team success by ensuring operational excellence, delivering exceptional customer experiences, and maintaining compliance across our stores. As a key part of our store leadership team you are responsible for supporting financial outcomes, and creating a positive work culture for our team members. You lead by example, balancing day-to-day priorities with long-term growth and development.

Position Relationships
Key Internal Stakeholders | Key External Stakeholders | Number of Direct Reports | Number of Indirect Reports
---|---|---|---
Line Managers, Team Members, State Support Team, National Office Team | Kmart 3rd Party Providers, Customers, Centre Management, Government Officials | 0 | 50+

Experience and Expertise:
• Store Operations Managers bring a strong background in administrative functions, ensuring accurate rostering, payroll administration and compliance leadership.
• Skilled in utilising data and reporting tools to track store performance, managing budgets and support data driven decision making.
• Experience in workforce management systems to optimise store team rosters, maintaining compliance with all applicable laws, as well as Kmart's operational guidelines.
• Proficient in financial management, cost control, expense management and supporting overall store profitability.
• Experienced in managing payroll requirements, communicating to team members queries and impacts relating to their pay.
• Confident understanding of enterprise agreements, the General Retail Industry Award (GRIA), and roster compliance to ensure legal obligations around payroll and employee entitlements are met.

Technical Ability:
• Proficient in business administration tools and technology to manage people, inventory and budgets.
• Strong in data analysis to monitor sales, forecast trends, and drive strategic decision making.
• Experience in rostering and scheduling systems to ensure optimal team coverage, compliance, and productivity.
• Confident decision-maker who understands store operations and procedures.
• Proven leadership skills in building relationships, managing teams and driving positive change effectively.

Who you are:
• You are someone with strong organisation skills, detail focused and can prioritise effectively.
• You are proactive and self-motivated, seeking out opportunity and acting on them.
• You are a great communicator adjusting your style to motivate the team and handle customer needs.
• You are a team player leading by example, delegating well and valuing contribution from others.
• You effectively manage multiple priorities, balancing operational demands, team needs and customer expectations to achieve business outcomes.
