OVERDUE LAYBY REPORT

What is it for?
This report documents all laybys that have exceeded their term and is automatically generated each Monday morning. All laybys in the "These Laybys should be Returned to Stock" section should be cancelled.

What does it look like?
[Report details showing customer transactions with completion dates, charge numbers, customer names, phone numbers, fixture locations, and outstanding balances]

What should I look for?
A. All cancel (RTS) receipts are attached / filed with the report.
B. Parcel contents have been physically ticked (✓) to APN details printed on the receipt.
C. Management signs the report to confirm that all receipts are on hand.

[The image shows example receipts and annotations indicating where:
- Lay-bys are ticked to confirm cancellation (B)
- Cancel receipts are attached/filed with the report (A)
- Parcel contents are ticked to indicate checked (B)
- Manager's signature on the report (C)]
