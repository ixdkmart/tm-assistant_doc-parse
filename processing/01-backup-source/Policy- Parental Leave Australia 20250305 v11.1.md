Policy – Parental Leave Australia

INTRODUCTION AND PURPOSE
At Kmart Australia Limited and Target Australia Pty Ltd (collectively referred to as the Company), we recognise that family is fundamental to our team members lives and to the success of our business. Our Parental Leave Policy (Policy) reflects our commitment to supporting team members during significant life transitions, whether through birth, adoption, or surrogacy. This Policy aims to provide equitable and inclusive leave options, enabling parents to balance their responsibilities at home, create meaningful connections with their children, and feel valued and supported in their roles both as caregivers and team members. By fostering an inclusive environment for parents, we assist in enabling the following:

• Enhancing team member wellbeing
• Enhancing team member engagement
• Promoting gender equity in the workplace
• Building a workforce that reflects the communities with which Kmart and Target operate
• Encouraging work life blend.

POLICY APPLICATION
This Policy applies to all employees of the Company (Team Members) employed in Australia. Where a Team Member's employment is covered by the terms of an industrial instrument, such as an enterprise agreement or a modern award, the terms of the industrial instrument will apply. However, if this Policy contains a more beneficial term, this Policy will apply to the extent of any inconsistency.

This Policy should be read in conjunction with any other relevant policies or procedures.

This Policy can be varied or withdrawn at any time and does not form part of, and is not incorporated into, a Team Member's contract of employment

DEFINITIONS
Adoption is the legal process of which a child under the age of 16 years legally becomes the child of the adoptive parent.

Federal Government Funded Paid Parental Leave is the Government funded paid parental leave scheme administered by the Department of Social Services and Services Australia. Please contact the Department of Social Services and Services Australia for further information

Flexible Unpaid Leave is Unpaid Parental Leave that can be taken flexibly by a Team Member. Flexible Unpaid Leave can be taken any time within 24 months of the child's birth or date of placement. It can be taken in one single continuous period of one or more days, or as separate periods of one or more days. If a pregnant Team Member is working in the six (6) weeks before the expected birth of the child, they can start taking Flexible Unpaid Leave during this time. Flexible Unpaid Leave can be taken for up to 130 days.

Extended Leave is up to an additional 12 months of Unpaid Parental Leave, which a Team Member may request after already taking twelve (12) months Unpaid Leave. This must be agreed to by the Company.

Keeping in Touch Days are paid days where a Team Member will perform work duties such as meetings, training sessions or events. These days are designed to help Team Members stay connected with their workplace, receive updates on changes or developments, and facilitate their transition back to work. Team Members are able to access up to 10 keeping in touch days for each 12-month period of unpaid parental leave, in agreement with the Company.

Non-Primary Caregiver: The Company defines a Non- Primary Caregiver as a Team Member who shares or will share the caregiving of the child after the birth, placement or relevant court order in the case of Surrogacy, but is not the Primary Caregiver.

Paid Parental Leave is the designated period, outlined within this Policy, that is offered by the Company during which eligible Team Members may take leave from work following the birth, Adoption, or placement of a child via Surrogacy at their full rate of pay. This leave enables team members to bond with their new child and support their family during this important transition.

Parental Leave means Unpaid Parental Leave and Paid Parental Leave, collectively.

Permitted Work Period is a period during a planned Parental Leave period where a Team Member is permitted to work instead of taking Parental Leave because their child is in hospital as a result of:
• being born prematurely;
• developing a complication or contracting an illness during the child's period of gestation or at birth, or following the child's birth.

Pregnancy Loss is the loss of a pregnancy before 20 weeks of gestation.

Primary Caregiver: The Company defines Primary Caregiver as a person who takes primary responsibility, or who will take primary responsibility, for the care of the child after the birth, placement or relevant court order in the case of Surrogacy during the majority of typical Company work hours or rostered hours.

Safe Job is a role that does not pose risks to the health and safety of a pregnant Team Member. It typically has the same ordinary hours of work as the Team Member's present position, or a different number of hours if the Team Member agrees.

Stillbirth is the birth of a baby where the baby weighed at least 400 grams at delivery or reached at least twenty (20) weeks gestation but hasn't breathed or had a heartbeat since their delivery.

Surrogacy means the legal agreement whereby the surrogate mother agrees to bear a child for another person or persons, who will become the child's legal parent(s) or have legal parental responsibilities after birth and in accordance with a Court order.

Special Leave is unpaid leave that a pregnant Team Member can take if they are unable to work due to a pregnancy related illness or if they experience pregnancy loss, miscarriage or termination (but not Stillbirth).

Unpaid Parental Leave is a period of time during which a Team Member is approved to be absent from work without pay and includes periods of Flexible Unpaid Leave and Extended Leave.

ELIGIBILITY                                  Paid Parental Leave

Eligible Team Members are those who:

• Are employed in a permanent (i.e. full-time or part-time) capacity. To clarify, Team Members employed under a fixed or maximum-term contract (including Limited Tenure contracts) are excluded; and

• Have completed six (6) consecutive months of continuous service with the Company immediately prior to the date the leave starts.

• Will be (or would have been, in the unfortunate event that the child(ren) is Stillborn or dies within the first 24 months of life) the Primary Caregiver or Non-Primary Caregiver following the birth of a child, the placement of adopted child(ren) under 16 years of age, or in accordance with the relevant Court order in the case of Surrogacy.

Team Members who are not eligible for the Company's Paid Parental Leave may be eligible for Federal Government Funded Paid Parental Leave.

Unpaid Parental Leave

Team members who are eligible for Paid Parental Leave and have completed six (6) months continuous service, may access up to twelve (12) months Unpaid Parental Leave directly after taking Paid Parental Leave. For all other Team Members, eligibility to Unpaid Parental Leave is in accordance with the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) (FW Act) as set out below.

Team Members are eligible for Unpaid Parental Leave, in accordance with the FW Act, if they have completed 12 months' continuous service by:
• the expected date of birth of the child; or
• in the case of Adoption, the date on which the Team Member's period of leave is to start.

This includes casual Team Members who have been working on a regular and systematic basis for 12 months and there is a reasonable expectation they will continue employment if it had not been for the expected birth or Adoption of the child.

ENTITLEMENT                           Team Member's expecting or welcoming a child(ren)

Eligible Team Members are entitled to the following prior to the birth, Adoption or placement with the relevant court order:

• A pregnant Team Member is entitled to be transferred to an available Safe Job if the Team Member has provided evidence (for example a medical certificate) that would satisfy a reasonable person that they are fit for work, but it is inadvisable for them to continue in their present position during a period because of illness or risks arising out of the pregnancy, or hazards connected with that position.

A pregnant Team Member who is eligible for Unpaid Parental Leave can take unpaid Special Leave if they're unfit for work because they are pregnant and have a pregnancy-related illness or if their pregnancy ends after 12 weeks through miscarriage or termination (but not Stillbirth).

Irrespective of their length of service, Team Members (including casual Team Members) are entitled to up to two (2) days of unpaid pre-Adoption leave to attend any interviews or examinations required to obtain approval for the Team Member's Adoption of a child(ren), when such appointments cannot be scheduled out of work hours. However, a Team Member will not be entitled to take pre-Adoption leave where they could instead take some other form of leave (such as personal leave).

Irrespective of their length of service, Team Members (including casual Team Members) are entitled to up to two (2) days of unpaid pre-Surrogacy leave to attend interviews or examinations, when such appointments cannot be scheduled out of work hours. However, a Team Member will not be entitled to take pre-Surrogacy leave where they could instead take some other form of leave (such as personal leave).

Paid Parental Leave

• Team Members eligible for Paid Parental Leave and who are or will be the Primary Caregiver are entitled to access up to twelve (12) weeks' Paid Parental Leave, in one continuous block. Primary Caregivers must commence their Paid Parental Leave at the same time that their period of Unpaid Parental Leave commences.

• Team Members eligible for Paid Parental Leave and who are or will be the Non-Primary Caregiver are entitled to access up to two (2) weeks' paid leave which must commence within twelve (12) months after the birth of the child, Adoption of the child or relevant court order, in the case of Surrogacy.

• Paid Parental Leave must be taken in one continuous block.

• During Paid Parental Leave, eligible Team Members will be paid as follows:
   ○ At their base rate of pay, based on their ordinary hours of work immediately prior to accessing Paid Parental Leave.
   ○ All other conditions of team members' employment will remain the same. This includes the ongoing accrual of leave, the maintenance of service recognition dates, and continued eligibility to participate in bonus programs (if applicable).
   ○ Superannuation contributions will be made as per legislation and in line with the team members contract and/or applicable industrial instrument during the period of paid leave.

• Eligible Non-Primary Caregivers who become Primary Caregivers within the first twenty-four (24) months following the birth, Adoption, or relevant court order in cases of Surrogacy, are entitled to up to twelve (12) weeks of Paid Parental Leave. Any Paid Parental Leave that has already been taken by the Team Member as a Non-Primary Caregiver will be deducted from the twelve (12) week Paid Parental Leave

Unpaid Parental Leave

• Subject to meeting the Unpaid Parental Leave eligibility requirements set out above, and regardless of whether they qualify for Paid Parental Leave, Team Members are entitled to up to twelve (12) months of Unpaid Parental Leave.

• Team Members who have taken twelve (12) months of Unpaid Parental Leave may request up to an additional twelve (12) months of Extended Leave. If approved, Extended Leave must start immediately after the initial Unpaid Parental Leave period ends.

• Eligible Team Members can take Flexible Unpaid Leave as part of their Unpaid Parental Leave and/or Extended Leave.

• Team Members are entitled Keeping in Touch days whilst on Unpaid Parental Leave and/or Extended Leave. They can be taken as half day or full day increments, and they do not need to be taken consecutively.

• Unpaid Parental Leave, including the Extended Leave, must commence and conclude within twenty-four (24) months of the birth or Adoption of a child(ren).

• During Unpaid Parental Leave, team members will continue to receive company superannuation contributions calculated at their base rate in line with their contract and/or applicable industrial instrument until they reach 52 weeks of parental leave.

Hospitalised Child(ren)

The Company may agree to a Permitted Work Period where a Team Member's child is in hospital as a result of:

• being born prematurely;

• developing a complication or contracting an illness during the child's period of gestation or at birth, or following the child's birth.

A Permitted Work Period does not break the continuity of Unpaid Parental Leave and extends the period of Unpaid Parental Leave by the duration of the Permitted Work Period.

Pregnancy loss, Stillbirth and Infant Loss

• In the unfortunate case of a pregnancy loss, Stillbirth or an infant death during the first twenty-four (24) months of life, Team Members may be entitled to access up to five (5) days of compassionate leave.

• In the unfortunate case of a Stillbirth or an infant death during the first twenty-four (24) months of life, Primary Caregivers eligible for Paid Parental Leave are entitled to access up to twelve (12) weeks' paid leave, and Non-Primary Caregivers eligible for Paid Parental Leave can access up to two (2) weeks paid leave, in one continuous block. Any Paid Parental Leave that has already been taken by the Team Member will be deducted from their entitlement. During this period of leave, eligible Team Members will be paid as follows:

   ○ At their base rate of pay, based on their ordinary hours of work immediately prior to accessing Paid Parental Leave.
   ○ All other conditions of team members' employment will remain the same. This includes the ongoing accrual of leave, the maintenance of service recognition dates, and continued eligibility to participate in bonus programs (if applicable).
   ○ Superannuation contributions will be made as per legislation and in line with the team members contract and/or applicable industrial instrument during the period of paid leave.

• All Team Members eligible for Unpaid Parental Leave may take up to twelve (12) months of Unpaid Parental Leave in the case of a Stillbirth or infant loss within the first twenty-four (24) months of life.

A pregnant Team Member who is eligible for unpaid parental leave can take unpaid Special Leave if they experience pregnancy loss after 12 weeks.

APPLYING FOR PARENTAL LEAVE    Initiating Parental Leave

• Kmart monthly paid eligible Team Members are required to enter the leave request in SuccessFactors.

• Kmart weekly paid eligible Team Members, need to complete a Parental Leave Application Form Australia (available on KIT) and provide this to their line manager who then forwards to Kmart Payroll for processing.

• Target monthly paid eligible Team Members are required to fill in the Parental leave service request on KIT.

• Target weekly paid eligible Team Members are required to request their Store Manager complete the Parental leave service request on KIT.

• It is a requirement that at least 10 weeks' notice is provided before the date the Team Member wishes to commence Parental Leave, unless this isn't practicable.

RETURNING TO WORK
Team Members must notify their line manager in writing to confirm the date on which they will return to work, at least four (4) weeks prior to their approved return to work date. However, the Company encourages Team Members to provide as much notice as possible of their return-to-work date.

Team Members wanting to return to work flexibly should refer to the Requests for Flexible Working Arrangements Policy or Flexible Work Policy, as applicable. If possible, it is recommended that Team Members start such discussions with their line manager before going on Parental Leave. Options include, but not limited to, a gradual return to work, varying start and finish times, or reduced hours.

COMPANY PROPERTY
Before commencing a period of Parental Leave, Team Members are required to return to their line manager any Company equipment/property in their possession or control or the period of the absence, except for their discount card(s). Company equipment/property includes but is not limited to:

• Company mobile phone; and
• Company laptop computer.

If a team member wishes to access the Kmart and Target systems during their parental leave for purposes such as Keeping in Touch Days, they must provide IT with four (4) weeks' notice prior to commencing leave, by submitting an ActionIT request for a virtual desktop.

Further, if Team members have a car on a novated lease or a job requirement vehicle they should refer to the Vehicle Leasing Policy.
