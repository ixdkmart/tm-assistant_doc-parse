Policy – Failure to Attend Work

INTRODUCTION
AND PURPOSE
Failure to attend work without notifying Kmart, Target and Anko (together referred to as "the Company") is unacceptable and may result in disciplinary action.

POLICY APPLICATION
This Failure to Attend Work Policy (Policy) applies to all employees of the Company (Team Members).

Team Members covered by an enterprise agreement must ensure they meet the requirements stipulated by their enterprise agreement in addition to this Policy.

COUNTRY APPLICATION
Australia, New Zealand, India, Canada, USA, UK, France, Philippines and all Anko Sourcing office locations.

POLICY
Failure to attend work without notifying the Company is unacceptable and may result in disciplinary action.

Failure to attend for work for three consecutive rostered shifts without notifying the Company may be deemed as abandonment of employment.

Key Elements of Abandonment of Employment

Key elements of abandonment of employment are when:
• the Team Member has failed to attend for work for three consecutive rostered shifts;
• There has been no contact with the Company from the Team Member (or another person on their behalf);
• The Company has made genuine attempts to contact the Team Member upon the Team Member's failure to attend the three consecutive rostered shifts (including using emergency contact details);
• The Company does not have knowledge of extenuating circumstances, which may have led to the Team Member's failure to attend work for three consecutive shifts;
• The Line Manager has not previously approved the Team Member's absence from work.

Obligations

Team Members must:
• Notify their Line Manager when they are unable to attend work;
• Arrange for another person to contact their Line Manager if they are unable to do so.

Line Managers, in establishing the whereabouts and general welfare of a Team Member, must:
• Contact the Team Member in relation to their failure to attend work;
• Make a note of all discussions with the Team Member in relation to their failure to attend work;
• Provide the Team Member with the opportunity to explain the reason for their failure to attend work;
