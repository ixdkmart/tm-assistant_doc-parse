## Page 2

Purpose

The purpose of the referral program is to encourage Kmart and Target team members in Australia and New Zealand to share current external vacancies within their networks. This is a great opportunity to encourage candidates who are aligned with our values and ways of working, to apply for available positions.

The program is designed to financially reward team members for referring suitable external candidates, who are successful in accepting a role at Kmart or Target.

The referred talent must remain with the business for at least six months and achieve a minimum Achievement and Growth rating of two ('Meets Expectations'). Successful referrers will receive a minimum payment of $1,000, paid in two instalments. Certain roles may offer a higher referral payment, which will be communicated via a Talent Acquisition team communication. Referral payment amounts are reviewed regularly and may be adjusted at any time. If a higher payment is advertised in a Talent Acquisition communication, it applies only to the specific role and promotional period stated. This higher amount does not extend to all advertised positions with the same title.

Terms and Conditions

Payment terms
• Payment will be made in two instalments:
    o Part 1: 50% will be paid when the referred candidate starts employment
    o Part 2: The remaining 50% will be paid after the candidate successfully completes the six-month qualifying period
• Payment will be made in the next available pay cycle:

All referral payments are subject to tax and superannuation deductions as required by law.

Referring team members must:
• Be a Kmart or Target team member
• Be employed at the time of payment (for casual team members this means you need to have worked a shift in the last 90 days)
• Not be a Talent Acquisition Partner, People &Capability Business Partner (for the function they support), KTLT member, those in a Head of, General Manager, Divisional Merchandise Manager (DMM) roles or the hiring manager of the position.

Referral payments won't be paid for referral of:
• Candidates to wage-based roles in stores, DC's or Mulgrave and Williams Landing (e.g. – store based casual roles)
• Existing Kmart and Target team members
• Team Members currently employed by any Wesfarmers entity, or who have been employed by a Wesfarmers entity in the preceding two years.
• Candidates who have applied to positions at Kmart and Target within the last six months.
• Candidates that have been presented to Kmart and Target directly or through an agency in the last 12 months.

## Page 3

General Terms and Conditions

• This program only applies to referrals of talent for roles advertised across Australia and New Zealand.

• If more than one team member refers the same candidate, the referrer who first referred the talent (as determined by the Talent Acquisition team) will be considered eligible for the referral payment. Subsequent referrers will not be eligible.

• The referring team member must remain employed at the time the payment instalment or instalments are due to be eligible for payment.

• If the referred candidate is not successful for the role, they are referred but is successful for another role that meets the criteria within a six-month period, the referring team member may still be eligible for the referral payment. This will be determined by the Talent Acquisition team on a case-by-case basis.

• All referrals must meet specific job qualifications, have the necessary skills and experience to perform the job and be successful in the recruitment process.

• The referring team member will be notified by the Talent Acquisition Partner if their referral is successful. No other details of the Talent Acquisition process will be disclosed to the referrer due to privacy legislation.

• The referral program is subject to change and may be varied or terminated at any time by Kmart and Target at its discretion.

Please email KmartGroupCareers@kmart.com.au if you have any questions on the referral program.
