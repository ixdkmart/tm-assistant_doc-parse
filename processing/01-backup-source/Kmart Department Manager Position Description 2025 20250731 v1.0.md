Department Manager
Position Description

As Australia's largest and most loved retailer, we've been brightening the everyday lives of families for over 50 years. With stores, distribution centres, and offices across Australia, New Zealand, and beyond, our 47,000-strong diverse and inclusive team works together to offer families on-trend products at incredibly low prices. We're results-driven and evolving rapidly, offering challenging work you can be proud of. At Kmart, we value your uniqueness because it's not just what you do, but who you are, that makes everyday living brighter.

Position Title | Function | Reports To | Years of Experience Required for Role | GRIA Level
---|---|---|---|---
Department Manager | Stores | Store Manager | 3 - 5 years | Retail Employee level 6

Kmart Department Managers are crucial to our company's success. They lead team members to deliver a high standards of customer experience through effective store merchandising, replenishment and customer service. This role spans across our Kids, Home and Clothing worlds, requiring strong product knowledge and ability to adapt to varied priorities. Department Managers ensure a safe and compliant store environment through effective process execution and team leadership. As process leaders and implementers of business initiatives, they navigate complexity to keep the store running smoothly and delivering strong results.

Experience and Expertise:
• Department Managers at Kmart play an important role in managing the day-to-day operations of our stores. Using data to track department performance, forecasting sales and managing department budgets to maximise productivity.
• Each Department Manager is responsible for leading operations within a designated department or area of the store. This includes driving performance, supporting overall operations, and ensuring smooth execution of daily tasks.
• Lead teams to deliver exceptional customer service by implementing insights and driving continuous improvement.
• Build team capability through coaching, training and effective recruitment selection fostering a high-performance culture and ensuring strong team engagement through leading by our values, ways of working and expected behaviours.
• Manage inventory flow, including overseeing and leading online operations.
• Implement safety processes to ensure compliance with regulations. Delivering safety training, supporting injury management and promoting a safe work environment.

Technical Ability:
• Experience using business administration tools and technology to manage people, process, inventory and budgets
• A strong ability to interpret and use data to monitor sales, forecast trends, and drive strategic decision making.
• Experience in rostering and scheduling systems to ensure optimal staff coverage, compliance, and productivity.
• Confident decision maker who understands store operations and procedures.
• Proven leadership skills in building relationships, managing teams and driving positive change effectively.

Who you are:
• You are someone with strong organisational skills, attention to detail and can prioritise effectively.
• You are proactive and self-motivated, seeking out opportunities and acting on them.
• You are a great communicator, adjusting your style to motivate the team and handle customer needs.
• You are a team player leading by example, delegating well and valuing the contribution from others.
• You effectively manage multiple priorities, balancing operational demands, team needs and customer expectations to achieve business outcomes.
