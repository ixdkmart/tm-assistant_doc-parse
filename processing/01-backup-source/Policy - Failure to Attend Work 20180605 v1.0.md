Policy - Failure to Attend Work

INTRODUCTION AND PURPOSE
Failure to attend for work without notifying the company is unacceptable and may result in disciplinary action.

Failure to attend for work for three consecutive rostered shifts may be deemed abandonment of employment

POLICY APPLICATION
This policy applies to all Kmart Limited team members.

COUNTRY APPLICATION
Australia

POLICY
Key Elements of Abandonment of Employment

Key elements of abandonment of employment are when:

• the team member has failed to attend for work
• there has been no contact with the company from the team member (or another person on their behalf)
• the company has made genuine attempts to contact the team member upon the team member's failure to attend a rostered shift (including using emergency contact details)
• the company does not have knowledge of extenuating circumstances which may have lead to the team member's failure to attend for three consecutive shifts.

Responsibilities

Team members must:
• notify their line manager when they are unable to attend work
• arrange for another person to contact their line manager if they are unable to do so.

Line Managers, in establishing the whereabouts and general welfare of a team member, must:
• contact the team member in relation to their failure to attend work
• make a note of all discussions with the team member in relation to their failure to attend work
• provide the team member with the opportunity to explain the reason for their failure to attend for work take appropriate coaching or disciplinary action.

Procedures

Line managers should refer to their own manager for advice in the first instance.
