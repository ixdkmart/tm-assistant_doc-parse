Mobile Phone and Nintendo Returns
Quick reference guide

Check first
Check phone for signs of physical damage, such as scratches, cracked or broken screen, or liquid damage. Signs of unauthorised repairs or tampering and ensure all accessories and manuals are returned. If any issues are found, the phone cannot be returned

DOA / ELF - 30 days from purchase
Important reminders:
*Follow the below steps
*Offer an exchange or refund
*A copy of receipt must be attached to emails and sent with the product
*Phones must be factory reset/removed from the cloud before being sent

Warranty - 2 years from purchase excluding iPhone,12 months
Important reminders:
*Follow the below steps
*A copy of the receipt must be attached to emails and sent with the product
*Phone must be factory reset/removed from cloud before being sent
