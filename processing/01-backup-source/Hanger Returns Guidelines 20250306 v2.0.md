Hanger Return Guidelines

Apparel hangers are part of Kmart's reuse circular economy. Hangers are collected at the registers as customers purchase products and are taken to back of house and placed in allocated PACT hanger boxes.

When hanger boxes are full, stores need to send to the DC where it is then shipped to a resorting facility for re-distribution to suppliers for reuse.

IMPORTANT: Do not send hangers boxes to RLO - they are backloaded to DC's directly.

Replenishment Hanger Box Process

Step | Action

Step 1 | Stores must always have sufficient hanger boxes in stock to manage the weekly volume of hanger returns, with additional volumes required during peak periods. Hanger boxes must only be used for returning hangers.

Info | Hanger boxes can be ordered via a PDT, with the maximum of 10 units per order.

Step 2 | To order hanger boxes via a PDT:
• Select 2 – Store Functions.
• Select 3 – Inventory Management.
• Select 2 – Ordering.
• Select 1 – Normal Orders.
• Enter a section ID of 3, then enter the keycode:
    ○ AUS = 41685204
    ○ NZ = 41688366
• Enter the quantity of boxes required (maximum order of 10).
• When complete select 'Function Quit' to save and submit the order.

Result | Hanger boxes will be sent to your store via your local DC.
