Policy - Privacy PCI

INTRODUCTION AND PURPOSE
PCI is the international security standard that has been adopted by organizations to prevent credit card fraud and the hacking of company security systems. If Kmart fails to comply with its PCI obligations the business could be liable for significant financial penalties. All team members must comply with this policy and may be disciplined for committing a breach of this policy.

POLICY APPLICATION
This policy applies to all employees (expressed as 'team members' throughout this policy) and contractors of Kmart Limited and its related bodies corporate ('Kmart'), and to any other person who has access to payment card details.

Throughout this policy, references made to team members will also apply to contractors.

COUNTRY APPLICATION
Australia, New Zealand

POLICY
Key Obligations for Team Members in Stores

1. As a general rule, credit card details must not be recorded or retained under any circumstance.

2. Team members must not record, save or create databases containing credit card information.

3. Team members must not transmit credit card details via email. Kmart IT will endeavor to block any email containing credit card information as email is not considered to be a secure method of transmitting credit card information.

4. When customers seek to pay their outstanding layby accounts over the phone, or for stores that provide the "Shop From Home" option whereby customers place orders over the phone or by fax, the following process must be adopted:

   I. The team member processing the order must not record the customer's credit card information. This means that the credit card number must be keyed into the EFT device as the customer provides the information over the phone. If the team member is not at a register when the customer phones through the order, they must record other relevant information for the order (but NOT the credit card details) and then phone the customer back to process the payment when they are at a register.

   II. The shop from home form that can be downloaded from Kmart's website has had the credit card option removed to enable payment processing over the phone only. If a customer uses an old form, the team member should phone the customer to confirm the order and destroy the written record of the credit card number (using a cross-cut shredder).

   III. If a customer places an order via an email that is not detected by the IT filter, a team member must phone the customer to verify the authenticity of the order and, provided the order is legitimate, the information must then be treated in the same manner as per clauses
