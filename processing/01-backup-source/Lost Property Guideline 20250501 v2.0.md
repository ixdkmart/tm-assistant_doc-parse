Lost Property Guidelines

Lost property is when there is no intention to leave the property behind. All lost property found within a store (including forgotten change) must be handed promptly to a member of the store management team. This guide steps through the process of receipting, retention, collection and disposal of lost property.

Processing Lost Property

The store management team are responsible for handling and management of lost property within their Kmart store. Any lost property should be handed to the store management team for registration, safe keeping and to facilitate either the return of property to its rightful owner or, in the case of valuable items, hand over to Police (see Appendix 1 for examples of types of found items that can be handed to the Police). The time frame in which lost property must be held is embedded in the Management & Disposal Methods section (pages 2-4).

Lost property items delivered to a member of the store management team will be registered with:
• name of finder (if known)
• contact address and/or email (not required for a team member)
• telephone number (no required for a team member)
• date
• where the item was found

All lost property must be itemised in the Lost Property Register (refer to Appendix 2 for an example), dated and name of the team member entering the item(s).

The article(s) should be placed in a plastic bag and marked with the sticker in Appendix 3.

Our company has no claim on money or goods found by customers, and only holds the lost property at the request of the finder. If any itemised lost property is of value, that property will be passed on to the Police, as per the above process.

Storing Lost Property

Keep all small valuables and cash in a locked drawer within a secure environment. Other found lost property of low value (see Appendix 2 for examples) can be placed in a storage container (for example a 60L, written off as store use). This is to be placed in the same secure environment, which may be a locked office or an old cash vault, if applicable. All property must be bagged (clear plastic bags available to order from Bluestar) and tagged (sticker - example in Appendix 3) for easy identification.

Customer Notification

When valuable items are found, and information is available to identify and contact that person, a reasonable effort must be made to see that the rightful owner is advised.
If unsuccessful at contacting the customer, the process for valuable items will be followed. Refer to Management & Disposal Methods section (pages 2-4).

Claiming Lost Property

All other Lost Property held within store and not managed in the manner as outlined in the Management & Disposal Methods of unclaimed Lost Property section (pages 2-4), cannot be claimed by the finder until the 3-month in-store retaining period has expired, unless it is by the rightful owner.

Upon claiming, the customer is to complete the Lost Property Surrender/ Collection Form (Refer to Appendix 3) and the Lost Property Register is signed. This instruction does not apply to articles found by team members.

Before handing over any article of lost property, ensure the claimant can adequately identify the lost property article or contents.

For property that was surrendered to the Police, the customer is to be referred to the appropriate Police Station together with the reference number. However, for property that could not be handed to the police, and was surrendered to the shopping centre management, refer the customer to the lost property section of the shopping centre to collect their missing item/s.

When any lost property is claimed, an entry listing must be made in to the Lost Property Register and the claimant completes the Lost Property Surrender/ Collection Form.

For property already collected by the rightful owner
Due to Privacy Laws, if a claimant has collected the lost property, a finder seeking verification of its collection should be given verbal confirmation only. They are not entitled to view the Lost Property Register or be provided with any further information regarding the individual who collected the lost property.

Rewards Offered to Team Members
On occasion, a customer who is reunited with their lost property, may want to offer the finder a reward, who in some instances could be one of our team members. The offering of a gift or money as a reward is strictly disallowed under the Gifts and Entertainment Policy. However, sometimes the customer may provide no choice by leaving the reward or gift. Therefore, the line Manager can decide on the following action:

• the money can go into the store's social fund, or;
• the money can go to the businesses' current nominated charity through the register.

If the gift is food based, it must be shared amongst other team members at an appropriate time, and donate any other gifts such as a beverage, to a social club for raffles etc.

Upon accepting the gift or money as a reward, the details of the reward and circumstances in which it was given must be entered into the Gift and Entertainment Reporting, available on KIT, to ensure transparency.

Management & Disposal Methods for Unclaimed Lost Property
The following are approved methods of disposal or management of unclaimed lost property. All actions must be entered into the Lost Property Register, should they be either collected or taken to the Police station, along with completing the Lost Property Surrender/ Collection Form. The people responsible for taking the items to the Police station, are in the roles of a member of the store management team or a Profit Protection Business Partner.

Credit Cards

Management | Disposal Methods
When a credit card is handed to a manager as lost property, the Lost Property Register is used to record the following details:
• Name of the card holder; and
• Financial institution the credit card belongs to, for example ANZ, NAB etc.

A Do Not record the credit card number in the Lost Property Register.

• Hold a lost credit card in the lost property storage area for no longer than 48 hours or next available business day. After this time destroy the credit card.
• If a customer enquires about a lost credit card, within the 48-hour period, they must provide identification, preferably photo ID, and advise which financial institution it is with, to obtain the lost credit card.
• If the credit card was destroyed, explain that it has been destroyed for security reasons and refer them to their financial institution.
• Credit cards can only be held in the designated lost property space for no longer than 48 hours in a business week. After this time the credit card must be destroyed by cutting the card across the name and number sections and in half again. **This must be done in front of another team member or Manager.
• An entry into the Lost Property Register is to be made to record the destruction, dated and signed.

Electrical Devices
Any electrical devices that have the potential to hold personal information such as mobile phones, iPads, tablets, laptops, cameras, smart watches etc.

Management | Disposal Methods
• Hold the device for a minimum of 48 hours in a business week and then a member of the store management team should take the item(s) to the local police station within no greater than 7 days.
• The item(s) must be signed out of the register prior to leaving the store, countersigned by another team member, then taken to the Police station to surrender the item/s. A receipt with a reference number is to be obtained and to the reference number recorded in the register.
• Failing being able to hand in to the Police station for whatever reason, if the store is attached to a shopping centre, the item/s can be handed in to them to manage. Details of which will need to be recorded in the register.
• If a store is a standalone or store management refuse to receive the item/s, they must be retained in store for a minimum of three months and thereafter to contact the Compliance team for further actions.

Cut into pieces and placed in the secure destruction bin.

Cash Under $50
Management | Disposal Methods
• Cash $50 and under, in the form of change, found in the store, including at registers or at a nearby vicinity, must be held aside for 48 hours, to allow time for a customer to return to collect it. It must be reported immediately to a member of the store management team, who will place it in a locked space, such as an under the desk drawer, filing cabinet, vault, safe, tank safe etc., for safe keeping.
• An entry listing must be made into the Lost Property Register and countersigned by another team member. If the customer returns to collect their lost money the claimant will be required to complete the Lost Property Surrender/Collection Form.

The amount is to be added as a clearance to the nearest register and a note of the variance to be completed under the 'reason for short or over' column, Or, given to the store nominated charity.

Valuable Items & Cash Above $50
Valuable items such as jewellery, cash over $50, handbags, wallets/purses with cards containing personal information and forms of ID such as passports Medicare cards etc.

Management | Disposal Methods
• To safeguard against misappropriation of stock, a member of the store management team must examine Kmart purchases found in store, and if in doubt about their authenticity, check the sale on the Escape electronic journals and CCTV footage.
• If the customer can be identified but cannot be contacted via phone, hold the property for a minimum of 48 hours in a business week and then a member of the store management team take the item/s to the local Police station within no greater than 7 days. A receipt with a reference number is to be obtained and to the reference number recorded in the register.
• Failing being able to hand in to the Police station for whatever reason, if the store is attached to a shopping centre, the item/s can be handed in to them to manage. Details of which will need to be recorded in the register.
• If a store is a standalone or centre management ref use to receive the item/s, they must be retained in store for a minimum of three months and thereafter to contact the Compliance team for further actions.
• The item(s) must be signed out of the Register prior to leaving the store.

Goods Estimated Value Under $50 Found by Customer
Management:
• To safeguard against misappropriation of stock, a member of the store management team must examine Kmart purchases found in store, and if in doubt about their authenticity, check at the sale on the Escape electronic journals and CCTV footage.
• If the lost property and money is of a low value (estimated under $100) and does not have the potential to contain another person's personal information, it can be released to the customer (finder) after a holding period of three months.
• If there is personal information, and the customer who owns the property cannot be contacted, these items must go through the process of being handed in to the Police.
• Phone the customer who found the items originally, requesting they call into the store to collect the found lost property
• An entry listing must be made in to the Lost Property Register for what is collected and the claimant completes the Lost Property Surrender/Collection Form.

Disposal Methods:
Goods to be disposed of at the Store Manager's discretion.

Goods Found by Team Members
Management:
• Items that are stocked by the store must be returned to stock.
• Items not stocked by the store must be held for three months and thereafter can be released to the finder and signed for by the finder in the Found Lost Property Register.
• Alternatively, if a team member does not wish to claim the goods, they can be donated to a charity or disposed of.

Disposal Methods:
If the team member does not claim the property and it's not worthy of charity, the property can be disposed of in the bin at the back of the store.

A Lottery or Scratchy Ticket
Management:
After a holding period of three months, the lottery/scratchy ticket can be either:
• Given to the team member who found it; or
• Used by the store for a social club event
The above is at the discretion of the store manager and both options are required to be entered into the Lost Property Register.

Disposal Methods:
N/A.

Flybuys or Other Rewards Cards
Management:
These cards are held for three months and then destroyed.

Disposal Methods:
Cut cards in half.

Gift Cards
Management:
Any Lost Kmart gift cards found with any value should be held for three months. Thereafter the card will need to be cancelled by contacting KmartBanking@kmart.com.au and providing the gift card number. This must be entered into the Lost property Register.

Disposal Methods:
N/A.

Perishables e.g. Food Stuffs
Management:
If found by a customer, it is to be held for 24 hours and then destroyed. If found by a team member, disperse amongst the team or dispose of.

Disposal Methods:
N/A.

Resources

Failure to Follow Process
Team members who fail to hand in lost property, may be disciplined under the Kmart Code of Conduct. Depending on the circumstances, such actions may be considered a serious breach of the code. The code requires that team members act in ana ethical, honest and lawful manner and be accountable for their actions and decisions at all times. If a team member does not comply with the code of conduct, disciplinary action may include termination of employment.
