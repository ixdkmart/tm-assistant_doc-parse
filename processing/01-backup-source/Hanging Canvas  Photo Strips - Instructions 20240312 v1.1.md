## Page 1

INSTRUCTIONS FOR HANGING PRODUCT RANGES
www.kmartphotos.com.au

Tools required:
Guillotine and Single-hole punch (new product)

1. Using a guillotine, trim excess white edges of the photo strip/canvas to the edge.
* If the photo strip/canvas includes a white border as part of the layout, trim to the edge of the pink section.
* DO NOT trim the 'pink' border as this is needed to hold the hanging bars.

2. Using the single-hole punch, punch 2 x holes on pink border at the top and 2 x holes on the bottom pink border as shown.

3. Using TOP x 2 timber bars – place bar with rope at the back of the print & place black pins through holes. Align holes in top bar over black pins and push down firmly to lock in place.

4. Using BOTTOM x 2 timber bars, place bar with black pins through the back of print & lock bar with holes over the top to lock in place. Roll completed photo strip/canvas & place in poster bag for customer collection.
