Sales Assistants are at the core of what we do at Kmart. As part of a dynamic team, you bring our shopping experience to life by unpacking product, moving products, locating items and creating a positive atmosphere for customers excited to find their next purchase.

Role Purpose:
• Collaborate effectively with your team mates to achieve the store goals.
• Maintain a positive attitude to create a welcoming environment for team and customers.
• Ensure work areas are consistently clean and organised, enhancing the overall shopping experience.
• Follow safety guidelines and directions to keep both our team members and customers safe.

Who you are:
• You are someone who enjoys working and collaborating with others.
• You are trustworthy and reliable, delivering on your commitments.
• You are open and supportive, encouraging others to speak up.
• You treat others with dignity, courtesy and respect.

Position Description

Tasks Available in store as a Sales Assistant:
Team members may be rotated across different store tasks based on operational needs. Some areas require specific training before you can work in them. Your store management team will allocate tasks based on workload and training compliance.

Working In: | Task: | Task could include working on:
--------------------|-----------------|--------------------------------

Service:
- Customer Greeter: Main entrance greeter
- 2nd Entry Greeter: Second entrance greeter, where your store has a second entrance
- Center SCOs: Working in the central self serve checkouts (SCOs)
- Front SCOs: Working in the front SCOs
- Checkouts: Working on service registers, including returns and refunds
- Service Desk: Service Desk, including returns and refunds, laybys, photo centre, click & collect pickup
- Online: Supporting online orders, picking, packing, click & collect
- Dual SCO: Second SCOs team member to support customers
- Fitting Rooms: All tasks related to fitting rooms, click and collect pickup

Clothing, Kids, Home & Floor:
- Refresh: Refresh process
- Shopkeeping: Shopkeeping tasks
- Presentation: Presentation process
- Returns: Any shop floor returns
- RLO: Processing of salvage or claimable items, marking down any items that can be sold instore
- IPM: Printing and verify host reports and shelf talkers, immediate price changes or withdrawn from sale recalls. Customer pick up dockets and report
- Pricing & Ticketing: Actioning host and any markdowns or markups

Capabilities required in role:

Curiosity: Actively asking questions, embracing current ideas and seeking clarification when unclear. Remaining open minded and onboard with ways to improve store operations.

Strives for Improvement: Works to meet the goals set out for them by your line manager. Maintains work focus despite distractions or operational setbacks. Open to feedback, and works on improving performance where needed. Puts in effort needed to meet goals and expected results.

Collaborates: Shares information with store management so there are no surprises. Involves others as appropriate to carry out individual and group goals. Welcomes and acknowledges the ideas and input of others.

Ensures Accountability: Takes responsibility for following policies, procedures, and work expectations. Seeks input to ensure tasks are completed correctly and to standard. Communicates availability clearly and in advance to store leaders. Shares useful ideas to improve how the team works.
