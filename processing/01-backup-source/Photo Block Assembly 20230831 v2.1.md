Mounted Photo Assembly
Quick Reference

Gather materials
1. Customer's photo print
2. Mounted photo frame
3. Alignment tool
4. Roller
5. Cutter
6. Photo gloves
7. Cutting mat (optional)

TIP: Mounted Photo frames are available in several sizes. Select the correct size for the photo you are mounting.

Prepare the frame
1. Remove the frame from the protective sleeve and place it white side up. Save the sleeve.

2. Place the alignment tool against one corner of the frame. The tool must be in close contact with the frame along two sides.

3. Peel back 2 to 3 inches of the white release paper from the corner of the frame opposite the tool. Crease the liner so the flap lays flat.

Align the photo with the frame
4. Put on the photo gloves and brush any debris off of the back of the customer's photo. The back of the photo must be clean.

TIP: Always wear photo gloves when handling customer photos.

5. Place the photo on top of the frame, taking care not to stick the photo to the exposed adhesive yet. Align the photo by pushing it against the two ledges on the alignment tool, so it contacts firmly all along two sides.

6. Holding the aligned photo against the tool, gently press the corner of the photo opposite the tool onto the exposed adhesive.

Remove the alignment tool.

Fix the photo to the frame
7. Lift the loose end of the photo and gently peel away the rest of the release paper. Do not allow the photo to touch the newly-exposed adhesive yet.

8. Roll the photo lightly onto the frame, working from the stuck corner outward to avoid trapping air bubbles.

9. Roll over the print again in all directions, this time pressing firmly to fully adhere the entire photo to the frame.

Trim the photo
10. Flip the photo mount face down. Press firmly.

⚠ CAUTION: Avoid contact with the blade. It is extremely sharp. Do not cut towards yourself. Cut sideways so blade is not facing your body.

11. Use blade firmly then return blade back into secure area after completion.

12. Position the cutter on one side of the frame as shown. Hold the cutter so the blade is at a slight angle to avoid damaging the frame as you cut. Press firmly for a clean cut.

13. Maintain this cutter orientation while pushing the cutter along the edge of the frame with firm pressure to make the clean cut.

14. Repeat steps 12 and 13 to trim the other three sides of the photo flush with the frame. Place the finished photo frame into the protective sleeve.

IMPORTANT: If more than moderate pressure is needed to make the cut, the blade is too dull. Replace the cutter. (Keycode 42223672)

Tell the customer how to assemble the photo frame stand
1. Remove the stand from the slot: Push down on one end of the stand and then pull up on the other.

2. Screw the stand into the black plastic plug.

3. The stand should be in the hole nearest the bottom edge of the photo. If it is not, remove the stand and plug by pulling on the stand. Then reinsert them into the appropriate hole.
