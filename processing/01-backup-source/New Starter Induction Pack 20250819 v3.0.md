Enthusiasts welcome.

Welcome to team Kmart

We're excited you're here!

This guide will take you through how to get setup and ready for your first shift at Kmart. Preparing you to join our passionate team in stores, making everyday living brighter for our customers.

What to expect:

• Once you have completed your onboarding within SuccessFactors, you will be sent a welcome text message with your username and temporary password.
• On your first login, you will have to change the temporary password.
• You'll use these login details for various work-related applications such as SuccessFactors, Our Learning Hub (Learning Management System), etc.
• Keep these login details safe as you'll need them to get set up in store.
• Your store will call you to schedule your first shift instore, this will be a training shift where you will get to know our broader business, your role and the information you need to commence your employment with Kmart.
• If you don't hear from your store, we would recommend giving them a call. You can find their contact information here: Store Locator

## Page 4

New Starter Induction Journey

Here's a snapshot of what your induction journey looks like. See the next pages for detailed steps.

1. Newly hired team member completes the pre-employment compliance via SuccessFactors

2. Receive your username and temporary password via a welcome text message

3. Your store will contact you to schedule your first shift, if you don't hear from them within 2 days, make sure to reach out directly.

4. Shift 1: Welcome to Kmart – Arrive in store, set up your MyApps and complete the 3-hour online training in store

5. Shift 2: Store Discovery – Attend the store discovery group workshop

6. Shift 3: Buddy Shift – Complete role-based learning pathway(s), shadow a buddy and complete Safe Work Practises (SWPs)

7. Congratulations! You're now set up to commence your role with Kmart. Make sure to reach out to your manager if you require any additional training.

[Side note]
Complete mandatory online modules within your first 30 days. Additional training may be required based on your role in store.

Steps to follow

BEFORE YOUR SHIFT 1

Step 1
Receive a welcome text message with your login details
After completing your onboarding in SuccessFactors, you'll receive a welcome text with your username and temporary password. Keep these login details safe, as you'll need them to set up in-store.

Step 2
Expect a call from your store to schedule your Shift 1
Your store will call to schedule your "Shift 1". This involves going into the store for a welcome and completing a 3-hour training shift on Our Learning Hub.

SHIFT 1: WELCOME SHIFT - IN-STORE TRAINING (3 hrs paid shift)

Step 3
Welcome to Kmart
This is your Day 1/Shift 1 in-store. Your manager will welcome you, go over our values and strategy, and share any store-specific information, such as emergency procedures.

Step 4
Start your store induction program
Go to the MyApps website and login using the details from your welcome text message. The system will prompt you to change your temporary password the first time you log in.

If you have any questions, please speak to your manager for support.

Log in to Our Learning Hub and complete the 3-hour "Kmart New Store Team Member (Wages) Training" (screenshots below).

These mandatory modules cover compliance, safety and essential knowledge for all team members.

Note: You will be paid for this 3-hour training.

Step 6
Know when your next shift is
After finishing your online training, you'll be notified about your next shift, which is the store discovery group workshop.

SHIFT 2: STORE DISCOVERY (3 hrs paid shift)

Step 7
Attend a store discovery group workshop
• This is a store tour that covers different areas of the store, health and safety details and key information about working at Kmart.
• Your manager will explain your role and the specific learning pathway you need to complete during your next shift.
• For example, if you're a Customer Greeter, you'll complete the "Pathway to Customer Greeter."

Step 8
Know when your next shift is
After finishing your store discovery, you'll be notified when your next shift is, which is the Buddy shift.

SHIFT 3: BUDDY SHIFT (3 hrs paid shift)

Step 9
Complete your role-specific pathway in-store
• Arrive in store and complete your role-specific pathway as advised by your manager.
For example, if you're a Customer Greeter, you'll complete the "Pathway to Customer Greeter" program on Our Learning Hub.
• Finish this learning before working with a buddy or manager on the floor.

## Page 8

Step 10: Shadow a buddy
Your buddy or manager will guide you as you shadow them and help you apply what you've learnt.

Step 11: Complete Safe Work Practices (SWPs) for your role
Safety first! Before using equipment, you must finish all applicable SWPs for the role and get them signed off. Your manager will work with you on these.

Step 12: Complete the "New Store Team Member Buddy Shift" questionnaire
After finishing the buddy shift, complete this questionnaire on Our Learning Hub at the end of the shift.

YOUR FIRST WORKING SHIFT

Step 13: Start work!
Now it's time to start in your new role!
Check in with your manager on your first shift if any further training is required for your role, such as SWPs or specialised role workshops.

YOUR FIRST 30 DAYS

Step 14: Complete your first 30 days online learning in-store
• Select the tile below in Our Learning Hub and complete the online modules listed for your first 30 days.
• These modules are mandatory for all team members regardless of role and cover the core knowledge and skills.
