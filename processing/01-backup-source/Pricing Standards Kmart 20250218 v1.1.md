TICKET FRAME DISPLAY STANDARDS

Pricing panel inserts - Instructions

General
1. The 'our price' panel insert must be the first panel (LHS) displayed in the ticket frame
2. Single digit = thick blank insert before $
Double digit = thin blank insert before $
3. The $ is placed directly to the left of the price point
4. Always ensure the price is centred
5. Wherever possible, a thick panel insert should be used at the end of the ticket frame
6. Rounded price points do not require '00' after the number.

Clearance
1. The 'clearance' panel insert must be the first panel (LHS) displayed in the ticket frame (replaces the 'our price' panel as required)
2. Single digit = thick blank insert before $
Double digit = thin blank insert before $
3. If there is more than one price point – display the highest price in the ticket frame. General rule: Single price whenever possible.

1500 Ends Planogramed home location – Instructions

1. Use the small screw-in ticket frame only
2. Screw the holder into the allocated screw point in the centre of the top tie bar of End panel frame
3. Slide the small ticket frame into the mounted holder
4. Display the pricing panel inserts according to the ticket frame panel standards. (Pg 5)

6. End must be single price point*

1200 & 1500 Gondola : Apparel – Instructions

2. Screw in the holder into the allocated screw point in the top tie bar of the module

TICKET FRAME DISPLAY STANDARDS

2100 Gondola Wall with VM: Apparel – Instructions

1. Use the small screw-in ticket frame and holder
2. Screw in the holder into the allocated screw point in the top tie bar of the module
3. Slide the small ticket frame into the mounted holder
4. Display the pricing panel inserts according to the ticket frame panel standards (Pg 5). Do not cover the price with products
5. Always follow the flow first. Price tickets can be used on the wall if it meets the following criteria:
   a. The entire wall is ONE price.
      • A maximum of 3 price flip tickets can be used, positioned every other module across the wall.
   OR
   a. The wall is made up of a MAXIMUM THREE different price points.
      • All 3 price points must be able to be displayed. Lowest price on left to highest price on the right. If the Red Price Wall POS is on the right, then the lowest price must lead in from the Red Price Wall POS module.
      • Price points should start and end at clear module breaks. The garments of one price point should not cross into the start of another module of a different price point.
      • The flip ticket must be positioned to the left of the first vertical run where each different price point starts.
6. Where the flip ticket is visible on both sides of the wall and the other side is not a single price point, leave the back of the ticket frame blank.

SHELF EDGE LABEL DISPLAY STANDARDS

Shelves - Instructions

1. Shelves: Shelf edge labels are to be placed into the data strips

2. Labels must be displayed at the left edge of each product range

3. Vertically duplicated stock requires one shelf edge label on the left edge of each row

4. Horizontally duplicated stock requires one shelf edge label on the left edge of the product range, at the start of each module

5. Gondola sides: Shelf edge labels are the primary method of communicating price. Ticket frames are not to be used.

Equipment: Ticket strip to timber
Re-order Code: C2862-03
Website: www.kmart.ticonline.com.au

SHELF TALKER DISPLAY STANDARDS

Clearance lines (Yellow) – Instructions

1. Shelving: remove the original shelf edge label from the data stripping (legal requirement)

2. The top of the shelf talker (white section) is to be folded and slid into the data stripping (shelves) or the end of the dual peg arms (hang sell)

3. A ticket stabiliser is also required to hold the shelf talker in place

4. The yellow shelf talker is only to be displayed on lines that are on 'clearance'

5. They are to replace the shelf edge labels & align with the left edge of a product range

6. Vertically duplicated stock requires x1 yellow talker on the left edge of each shelf

7. Horizontally duplicated stock requires x1 yellow shelf talker on the left edge at the start of each module

8. Apparel: ensure that a yellow pricing gun sticker is also covering the original price on the swing tags. (refer page 23)

Equipment: Ticket stabiliser
Re-order Code: 36000170
Website: www.bspg.com.au/kmart

SHELF TALKER DISPLAY STANDARDS

Clearance lines on Tables - Instructions

Sometimes only part of a range/table line will go on 'clearance', whilst the remaining will stay at the original price

1. Lines remaining at the original price are to be merchandised vertically in their original location on the table.

3. The small ticket frame is to display the original price

4. 'Clearance' lines are to be displayed vertically in their original location on the table.

5. Yellow shelf talkers are to be attached using blutack to the power table shelves directly in front of each 'clearance' line.

SWING TAGS DISPLAY STANDARDS

Regular price change (Blue) – Instructions

1. The swing tags are to be re-priced using a blue sticker from the pricing gun

2. Remove the original price from the swing tag – tear along the perforated line

3. Regular price changes: Re-price using a blue sticker. Place the sticker on the swing tag without covering the barcode

4. When ticket frames are also on display, ensure the pricing panels are updated to reflect the new price.
