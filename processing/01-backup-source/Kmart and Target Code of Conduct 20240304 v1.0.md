Kmart and Target Code of Conduct (the 'Code')

This Code outlines the standards of conduct, expected to be met by all Kmart and Target Team Members and all other persons conducting a business or undertaking for, or on behalf of, Kmart or Target. The Code applies when dealing with each other, customers, suppliers, contractors, shareholders and anyone we interact within our business.

This Code is not incorporated into your employment agreement and may be amended or revised at any time.

Responsibilities

All Kmart and Target Team Members and Workers are expected to be familiar with, understand and fulfil the following responsibilities.

Everyone

At work and work-related places and social situations, or any situation you are representing Kmart or Target, you must:
› Embrace and act in line with our Values and expected standards of behaviour.
› Make truthful statements, promises or commitments that you can meet.
› Act in an ethical, honest, professional and lawful manner and be accountable for your actions and decisions.
› Treat others as you would wish to be treated and trust, support and respect fellow team members.
› Fulfil your contract of employment and undertake your duties and exercise any powers given to you faithfully, with care and diligence.
› Comply with all lawful and reasonable instructions or directions by Kmart and Target and conduct yourself appropriately and in a reasonable and lawful manner with all persons with which we conduct business.
› Learn about and comply with all applicable internal policies and procedures (including when the document sets higher standards than the law) and all relevant laws.
› Perform your duties in a way that minimise negative environmental, social and community impacts and maximises workplace safety.
› Role model and share your knowledge about this Code with other team members.
› Raise situations you think could be in breach of this Code, as set out in this document.
› Encourage others to raise concerns in a timely manner.

Managers

In addition to everyone's responsibilities, as managers you must also:
› Lead by example.
› Comply with this Code, company policies, procedures and all laws that apply to your area of responsibility.
› Contribute to our reputation as a good corporate citizen, which seeks the respect of the communities and environments in which we operate.
› Exercise fairness, courtesy, respect, consideration and sensitivity in all dealings within the workplace and with customers, suppliers and the public generally.
› Build team members' knowledge of and compliance with this Code, company policies, procedures and all relevant laws.
› Provide an environment where team members can raise their concerns and discuss them openly, without fearing or experiencing negative consequences.
› Promptly manage or escalate behaviour that is inconsistent with this Code.
› Lead and manage team members in line with our Values and expected standards of behaviour.

Diversity, equal opportunity and workplace behaviour

Kmart and Target are committed to diversity, equal opportunity and providing a safe and healthy workplace. We recognise and appreciate the value of our differences, both internally (amongst team members) and externally (amongst customers, suppliers, contractors and anyone we interact with in our work).

Kmart and Target do not accept harassment (including sex-based and sexual harassment), grooming for sexual conduct with a child, hostile work environments, discrimination, bullying, victimisation, vilification, or any other inappropriate workplace behaviour.

I will:
› Treat everyone that I interact with in the course of my employment with dignity, courtesy and respect.
› Contribute positively to an environment where team members and others I interact with can work and shop, without the distress or interference caused by any inappropriate workplace behaviour.
› Not unlawfully discriminate against people, including in the provision of our goods and services and in all aspects of employment (e.g. recruitment, training, promotion, remuneration and benefits and termination of employment on prohibited discriminatory grounds, such as age, gender, race, disability, religion, etc.).
› Not make any disparaging or untruthful remarks about other team members, customers, competitors, contractors or suppliers.

The above requirements also extend to your behaviour and activity which may occur on online forums and any environment in connection with the workplace.

Safety, health and wellbeing

Kmart and Target will provide a working and shopping environment where team members, customers and visitors are safe. We will not accept behaviour that puts the health and safety of team members, customers, suppliers, contractors or anyone we interact with in our work at risk. This includes physical and psychological violence or harm and threats of violence or harm.

I will:
› Behave in a way that ensures my health and safety and the health and safety of others.
› I will not put the health and safety of others or myself at risk.
› Follow all safety requirements, safe-work practices and directions.
› Follow any reasonable instructions, policies or procedures issued by Kmart or Target in relation to work health and safety.
› Be familiar with and follow all emergency and response plans and procedures.
› Attend fit for work, not under the influence of alcohol, drugs or other substances (this includes the misuse of prescription or non-prescription medications).
› Not possess or traffic illegal drugs or weapons.
› Not smoke or vape on company premises, except in designated areas.
› Maintain personal presentation standards.
› Immediately report any team member putting themselves, other team members or anyone in the workplace at risk of harm or injury.
› Immediately and accurately report to a manager any hazards, near misses, incidents or injuries that occur or witness in or in connection with my employment.
› Immediately report to a manager any health and safety issues, hazards or concerns.

Fair trading and anti-bribery

Kmart and Target will provide customers with the confidence that we will always be open, honest and trustworthy in all our dealings with them.

I will:
› Deal openly, honestly and fairly with customers.
› Follow safety and product information standards and action product withdrawals and recalls.
› Honour customers' rights to a refund or exchange.

Kmart and Target will work with suppliers of goods and services and all third parties with whom we have (or may have) business dealings with, in a constructive, lawful, fair and appropriate way.

I will:
› Ensure third party relationships are based on price, quality, service and reputation.
› Deal fairly and honestly with suppliers and all third parties and not use undue pressure, coercion or unfair tactics.
› Not directly or indirectly give, offer, accept, promise or request cash or any other incentive or benefit, favourable treatment, inducement or reward in any form in connection with my employment which might compromise, or be seen to compromise our reputation.
› Not engage in conduct involving money laundering, bribes, 'kick-backs', secret commissions, facilitation payments or other unlawful or in breach of policy payments.
› Not make any political donations for or on behalf of Kmart or Target.
› Not make any charitable donation for or on behalf of Kmart or Target, without prior approval.
› Take all practical steps to ensure that agents, contractors, intermediaries or business partners do not engage in conduct in contravention of this Code.
› Report any known or suspected activity involving any form of bribery or conflict of interest, as set out within this document.

Kmart and Target will interact with competitors in a fair and reasonable manner.

I will:
› Deal fairly with competitors and not influence or restrict their dealings.
› Not enter into any discussions or agreements with competitors, regarding prices, discounts or sales.
› Not misuse our position to restrict competition in the marketplace.
› Comply with and promote all relevant competition and consumer laws.

Conflict of interest

Situations can sometimes arise where a team member has interests, which conflict or have the potential to conflict with those of Kmart or Target. For example, a conflict of interest may arise where a team member makes a business decision or influences a decision (of a supplier, customer, contractor or another team member) that directly or indirectly provides a benefit to themselves or a related third party, such as a partner, relative, business or associate.

A conflict of interest may also arise where you are in a personal relationship with:
› a direct report or other team member (where your relationship could create a conflict or potential conflict with your employment duties); or
› a customer or supplier, that may prejudice or influence your business relationships or compromise or prevent the business securing the most commercially favourable terms for purchase or supply of goods and services.

These relationships may influence a team member's ability to work objectively and effectively.

We recognise there is potential for a conflict of interest in a range of situations involving team members, customers, suppliers and other parties and will work with team members to resolve potential or actual conflicts of interest.

I will:
› Declare any actual, potential or perceived conflict of interest to my line manager or the People &Capability Advisory Centre on 1800 425 717.
› Ensure that my personal or other business interests will not conflict with my duties and obligations to Kmart and Target.
› Not accept gifts from customers, agents, contractors or suppliers or any third party with whom we have business dealings or might have business dealings.
› Only accept offers to attend events or entertainment where it is an essential part of doing business, or an existing business relationship exists, and my General Manager has approved it.

› Never offer or accept gifts or hospitality, without prior written approval from the relevant Anti-bribery Compliance Officer.
› Never request any invitation for entertainment or hospitality and never accept any form of complimentary travel or accommodation from a third party with which Kmart or Target has business dealings or potential business dealings with.
› Only purchase merchandise directly from suppliers when it is an approved business requirement.
› Not reference Kmart or Target's name or ABN on any personal parcel.
› Maintain accurate records and never engage in fraud.
› Comply with Kmart and Target's policies and procedures regarding conflicts of interest.
› Immediately report any actual or suspected breaches of these obligations, as set out within this document.

Confidentiality of information and corporate reputation

Information is considered confidential when it is not freely available to the public. Most of the information used throughout Kmart and Target is confidential. If you are in doubt, treat information as confidential.

Kmart and Target will not accept the unauthorised disclosure of confidential information and will not tolerate you doing or saying anything that might damage our reputation in any online or other forum in the public domain.

I will:
› Keep Kmart and Target's and their customers' and suppliers' information confidential, except where disclosure is authorised or required by law.
› Only use my own systems log-on ID and password and not provide them to another user.
› Use confidential information solely for authorised purposes, for example to perform my duties.
› Not modify or disclose confidential information (including price sensitive information about Kmart, Target or Wesfarmers) to gain an advantage or benefit for myself or someone else.
› Not provide any Kmart, Target or Wesfarmers information that is not publicly known to third parties that could affect the Wesfarmers' share price.
› Only dispose of, trade or acquire any Wesfarmers' shares or securities in accordance with applicable legislation, share plans and applicable policies and procedures.
› Immediately refer requests for information or comment from the media to Corporate Affairs.
› Not make statements, comments or post material (whether on social media or otherwise) that might damage Kmart or Target's reputation or interests or the reputation or interests of team members, customers or any person we have business dealings with.
› Not publish any confidential information online, including on any social media platforms.
› Not post derogatory, offensive, inappropriate, hateful or threatening comments or material about a team member, customer or any person with whom we have business dealings, such as contractors and suppliers.

Human rights and modern slavery

Consistent with the United Nations Universal Declaration of Human Rights*, Kmart and Target are committed to respecting human rights in our workplaces and eradicating and preventing any modern slavery practices, which may exist in our operations and supply chains.

Kmart and Target are committed to ensuring that remedial action is taken as quickly as practicable, where human rights violations or exploitative labour practices are reported or identified.

I will:
› Respect the human rights of team members.
› Comply with all applicable child employment laws, including those relating to minimum working age, working hours and the time of when hours are worked.
› Respect laws that give effect to freedom of association and collective bargaining, such as union membership or representation rights.
› Not require a team member to work in a manner that constitutes forced labour, including through the use of violence or intimidation.
› Not confiscate, withhold or threaten to withhold any identity or travel documentation (like a passport or visa) and not hold or demand any money deposit by a team member, in respect of employment that must be repaid before a team member may leave their employment.
› Not require or request a team member to work excessive overtime.
› Not require a team member to repay any of their wages or make any deduction from their wages without their agreement as per the law.
› If required by my role, promote, monitor and enforce the relevant Kmart and Target Ethical Sourcing Code (as applicable), with our suppliers.
› Act as quickly as practicable to remedy any adverse impact for any worker concerned where a human rights violation or an exploitative labour practice is reported or identified.
› Immediately report any actual or suspected breaches of these obligations, as set out within this document.

Privacy

Kmart and Target are committed to respecting privacy and safeguarding the personal information entrusted to us, by team members, potential team members,customers, suppliers, contractors and anyone else we interact with in our work. We will not accept the collection, use or disclosure of information for any purpose, other than for which it is required.

I will:
› Respect the privacy of individuals.
› Only collect personal information, as permitted under the relevant Kmart and Target policies and procedures.
› Keep confidential and protect information I have or know about team members, customers, suppliers, contractors and anyone else I interact with.
› Prevent loss, misuse and any unauthorised access, disclosure, transfer or modification of personal information.
› Collect, retain and disclose personal information appropriately and in accordance with the applicable legislation and our policies and procedures.
› Immediately report any actual or suspected breaches of these obligations, as set out within this document.

*A copy of the United Nations Universal Declaration of Human Rights can be found on the Intranet.

Company resources

Kmart and Target will not accept deliberate or reckless damage to or the theft or misuse of Kmart, Target or other Wesfarmers' business resources or the resources of others whom we interact with in our work. Resources include (but are not limited to) stock, money, equipment, stationery, files, data, records, computer hardware and software, intellectual property and company work time.

I will:
› Use Kmart, Target or other Wesfarmers' business resources for the intended purpose.
› Only dispose of any resources, if approved and accurately recorded and disposed of.
› Abide by copyright and trademark laws, including to never publish or cause to be published, Kmart or Target's company logo or trademarks on social media sites or otherwise, without express authorisation.
› Maintain accurate business records, including work time and attendance, leave records, merchandise and accounting, financial and expenditure records, business dealings and not falsify such records or cause such records to be inaccurate or falsified.
› Only enter into agreements with external parties to the extent that I have the authority and approval to do so.
› Return all company property and any confidential information from the Company in my possession, upon cessation of my employment.
› Only use my team member discount privileges in accordance with the terms of use.
› Only obtain or effect any discount on products to an authorised amount.
› Only place stock on hold and/or lay-by appropriately and in line with Company expectations (i.e. not in order to purchase it at a lower price, on a later date).
› Process markdowns correctly and not take or give unauthorised markdowns.
› Process 'quit cycle' or 'clearance' stock correctly, in accordance with policy and not purchase this stock, unless approval has been given for the purchase and the purchase price set appropriately.
› Purchase clothing under the relevant team wear offer, strictly in accordance with the rules of the offer.
› Pay for Kmart, Target and other Wesfarmers' business stock or assets, before consuming them or leaving the business with them.
› Not return stock and receive a greater refund than I originally paid.
› Not remove testers, bonus stock or samples from the workplace without authorisation.
› Process transactions, including loyalty programs, such as FlyBuys, correctly.
› If in stores, not use a standard cash register to process my own transactions or transactions for friends or family.
› If in stores, only use self-serve checkouts to conduct my own transactions or transactions for friends or family when I am on a scheduled break or outside of my rostered hours of work (but never conduct such transactions whilst I am carrying a SSCO/ACO ID badge, Barcode Card or any other SSCO/ACO Specialist equipment).
› Immediately report any team member misusing or suspected of misusing loyalty programs, discount cards, 'quit cycle' or 'clearance' purchases or team wear purchases.

Reporting a breach

If you genuinely believe that a breach of this Code has occurred, we want you to speak up. Kmart and Target are committed to making sure that you are treated fairly and do not suffer detriment because you speak up.

You can raise your concerns with:

› Your line manager or, where the breach involves your line manager, with another manager (e.g. your line manager's manager),
› The People & Capability Advisory Centre on 1800 425 717.
› The Kmart and Target Anti-bribery Officer for actual or suspected breaches of the conflict of interest, fair trading and anti-bribery reporting:
    • antibriberyofficer@kmart.com.au
› The relevant Privacy Officer for actual or suspected breaches of privacy obligations:
    • kmartprivacyofficer@kmart.com.au
    • privacyofficer@target.com.au

Alternatively, if you suspect or are aware of a breach of this Code, you can use the SpeakUp channel.

SpeakUp channel

Is a confidential hotline and reporting service provided by an external provider (i.e. Navex Global), available for Kmart and Target team members, suppliers and contractors to report incidents of misconduct.

This service is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week and can accommodate calls in all local languages where we have operations (noting that for less widely spoken languages, there may be a delay whilst you wait for an interpreter, or they may have to call you back). Alternatively, you can report online.

Once your report is submitted, the relevant team will be notified and will assess the matter promptly, to determine the best course of action in a confidential and sensitive manner. This may include an investigation, which may be conducted by an internal investigation team or an external agency/service provider.

Kmart and Target will maintain the confidentiality of reports and the identity of the reporter (if provided), except where a disclosure is required (e.g. under the law or in the interest of conducting a full and fair investigation). Where applicable, the reporter's consent will be sought before any disclosure occurs.
