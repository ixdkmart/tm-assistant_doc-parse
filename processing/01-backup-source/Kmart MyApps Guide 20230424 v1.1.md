## Page 2

Getting setup with MyApps

What is MyApps?
MyApps is a central location to access Kmart applications. Using MyApps will be the best way to access your employment information through SuccessFactors as well as your learnings through iKurate and kLearning.

MyApps is a website location, not an application that is required to be downloaded through the App Store.

Staying secure
Alongside your Username and Password, Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is used to secure your account and ensures you're the only person who can access your information by using your:

• Mobile Device
• Microsoft Authenticator App
• Desk Phone located within a Store or Distribution Centre

This guide will assist you in setting up MFA to access MyApps.

Setting up your account
• If you are a new team member, please login using your employee number and temporary password, located in your 'Welcome to Kmart' email.

• If you are an existing team member and do not know your password, please see Page 13 to reset your password.
