Voiding e-Vouchers: service desk

During the transaction (void total)

During the transaction, (up until refund/exchange has been finalised), you can void the transaction by pressing void and then total. When you do this, the e-Voucher does not get created and refunds are not issued.

After the transaction (void previous)

cash refund         Process remains unchanged, can still perform a void previous (total 10 Non sales)

EFT refund          Process remains unchanged, can not perform a void previous (total 10 non sales)

e-Voucher          If you have issued an e-Voucher and determine that it is incorrect, follow
(exchange/refund  these steps:
and exchange
only)               1. Process a sale (on a front end/sale register not the service desk) using the
                       same product that was returned and the incorrect voucher.
                    2. Attach the redeemed receipt to the incorrect voucher
                    3. Deface the voucher
                    4. Record on the K162 slip the details of the 'void' as information only
                    5. Attach the incorrect/defaced voucher (with the redeemed receipt) to the
                       service desk registers K162 slip.
                    6. Process the exchange correctly through the service desk register.

                    Note: for each voided e-Voucher, there should be an defaced issued e-Voucher
                    with a redeemed receipt.
