Policy - Personal Presentation

INTRODUCTION AND PURPOSE
This policy outlines the personal presentation standards applicable for all Kmart team members whilst at work and during work-related activities or functions.

POLICY APPLICATION
This policy applies to all Kmart team members at national office and at state or country offices not working in stores.

COUNTRY APPLICATION
Australia and New Zealand

POLICY
Presentation Standards

Team members must maintain a professional and businesslike appearance suitable to their work environment, or any Kmart work environment they are visiting which allows them to perform their work safely.

This policy must be read in conjunction with business guidelines or policies relating to:
• the Stores Team Wear Policy (if a team member is working or visiting a Kmart store); and
• any applicable safety standards.

Adherence to this Policy provides the following benefits:
• improves the visibility and identification of team members;
• ensures a consistent, identifiable image for Kmart;
• promotes a businesslike and team image;
• contributes to ensuring the safety of team members.

Responsibility

Team members must maintain appropriate personal presentation standards and adhere to business specific team wear guidelines and must at all times:
• maintain a high personal hygiene standard;
• comply with safety standards;
• maintain hair, beards, moustaches and make-up (if worn) in a clean, neat and well-groomed condition, that reflect a professional and businesslike appearance;
• maintain hair, beards, moustaches in a style or condition that is practical for the work environment and that permits a team member to perform their work safely;
• keep visible jewellery, body piercing and tattoos to a minimum that ensure a professional and businesslike appearance;
• ensure that any jewellery or body piercing is practical for the work environment and allows the team members to perform their work safely.

External business wear

When safety or operational requirements necessitate a team member to wear personal protective clothing or equipment such clothing or equipment must be worn. External business wear must be clean, ironed and maintained in good condition.

Smart business casual wear

This policy continues to apply to any nominated casual dress day and at any sites where smart business casual wear is applied permanently.

❖ smart casual dress does not include the wearing of revealing clothes and casual dress must be suitable and practical for the work environment and permit work to be carried out safely;
❖ smart casual dress must be neat, clean and well maintained;

Line managers are required to:

❖ ensure all team members comply with this Policy and any business specific business wear guidelines;
❖ ensure that this Policy and any business specific business wear guidelines are reinforced to all new team members during the recruitment process and induction;
❖ review options with a team member who believes they have a legitimate reason not to comply with this Policy or business specific business wear guidelines; (The need to review options may be the result of a team member's rehabilitation following an injury or illness or in cases where following a specific business wear guideline may be potentially discriminatory);
❖ coach a team member who does not comply with this Policy, unless such coaching would be discriminatory in the circumstances;
❖ take appropriate disciplinary action where a team member consistently fails to comply with this Policy or any business specific business wear guidelines, unless such coaching would be discriminatory in the circumstances;
❖ assess and manage claims of reimbursement for damage to personal clothing.

Assessing and Managing Claims for Damage to Personal Clothing

It is preferable to clean or repair damaged clothing where items of personal clothing are unavoidably damaged in the course of work.

Replacement may be considered more appropriate where it is deemed to be more economical or repairing/cleaning the item would not return it to the condition immediately before being damaged.

Damage to clothing must:
❖ occur during work;
❖ not be caused by normal wear and tear;
❖ not be the team member's fault.

A team member will not be reimbursed for any damage to their clothing where it can be shown that the team member failed to wear supplied protective clothing where the wearing of the protective clothing could reasonably have expected to have prevented the damage to the team member's clothing. Reimbursement does not apply to company provided clothing or footwear. If the damage to clothing and property is related to a workplace injury, the associated costs to cover the damage may, in some states, be included in a workers compensation claim.

Non-stores Team Members working or visiting stores

At all times, when work is performed at a Kmart store team members must comply with the Stores Team Wear Policy. This applies to all attendance at stores for a work-related purpose including meetings.

Breach of this Policy

Team members must comply with this Policy, breach of this Policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment.

Questions about this Policy

If you have questions about this Policy you should refer these questions to your Line Manager.
