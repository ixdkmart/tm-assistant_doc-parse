CHECKING FOR TEAM MEMBERS CREATING OWN LAYBY

How do I?

Using the 'POS Operator IDs' report printed from CC, highlight all operators with a 651 (lay-by model) then include any team members who have created any lay-bys. You will also need a copy of the 'Employee Table Listing Report' printed from T&A (Aust), 'Resource Employment Details Report' printed from Etivity (NZ).

Log into 'Charge Accounts search by name' section in CC:

From the System main menu select:
• 1 'Kmart Sales Application' and Enter
• From the 'General Sales Application' menu:
• Press F8 (Fast Path)
• From the 'Charge Account Maintenance' menu select:
• 3 – 'Search by Name' and Enter
• Type the surnames of each team members who work in the lay-by department.

Review if lay-by team members are creating their own lay-by or that of relatives living at the same address.

What do I do with the results?

If you find any team members who have created their own or a family member's (living at the same address) lay-by and the charge is still current, check the contents of the layby parcels. Discuss the Code of Conduct with the team members concerned.
