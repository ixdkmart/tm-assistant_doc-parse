Policy - Customer Trolley Management

INTRODUCTION AND PURPOSE
Every day, thousands of our customers make use of a shopping trolley. Kmart has hundreds of thousands of dollars invested in the provision of trolley services, in the form of maintenance, collection, replacement and upgrading of these assets.

Kmart Australia Ltd ("Kmart") has decided to implement this trolley management policy to introduce a holistic approach to managing this asset. Whilst the business understands that there will always be loss in terms of trolley theft and abandonment, we believe that there is a chance to mitigate this loss thorough appropriate end-to-end management.

POLICY APPLICATION
Kmart store teams, store operations, facilities, procurement

COUNTRY APPLICATION
Australia and New Zealand

POLICY
Procurement
Kmart will approach its dealings with contractors in two ways:
• Through the procurement team (procurement) at the contracting phase of the engagement, purchase and collection; and
• Through the store management team during the operation of the contract (as discussed under the "Store Operations" section of this document)

As a responsible retailer, Kmart will always engage and have dealings with trolley contractors in good faith.

Engagement of trolley collection contractors

Procurement will be employed to procure and engage all required trolley contractors. Procurement will also assist with the development of guidelines and the setting of relevant KPI targets. It will be a requirement of any contractual arrangements that contractors are responsible for all trolley impoundment fees and infringement notices (when at fault).

The national store services manager, in conjunction with store managers will ensure that Councils provide Kmart with appropriate reporting in terms of impound timeframes.

Monitoring of trolley contractors

Kmart believes that a large portion of overall trolley collection contractor management should be delegated to store

Purchasing trolleys

The supplier management team will be engaged to ensure that trolleys are purchased from an appropriate source, specification and cost as defined by various business stakeholders. The team will assist the facilities team in ordering trolley fleets for new stores.

Trolley security systems

Trolley security mechanisms (i.e. coin locks, gatekeeper wheels etc.) will only be installed in the following situations:
• Where the local council has enacted legislation requiring our business to install coin-operated trolleys; or
• Where all other retailers within the centre have installed a trolley security system and Kmart has decided that the system is required.

Note: it is Kmart policy to refuse the installation of coin-operated trolleys wherever possible. This should be the stance provided to all Councils and centre owners (where appropriate). Any escalations should be brought to the attention of the national store services manager.

Trolley contractor specification

Store managers should be aware of their trolley collection arrangements and the services that those contractors provide. For clarification on specific contractual arrangements, stores should contact the national store services manager.

Facilities and maintenance

General responsibilities

The facilities team (facilities) will be responsible for:
• Maintenance of trolleys and trolley security systems ongoing
• Reallocation of emergency trolleys during high trade periods
• Provision of a regulator and/ or Council escalation point

Maintenance of trolleys

Facilities will be responsible for the ongoing maintenance of trolleys.

To assist facilities store teams must:
• Monitor trolley safety;
• Isolate and label any trolley that is unsafe to use;
• Contact property team support to report damaged trolleys; and
• Confirm that services and repairs are actioned.

Council engagement and our expectations

Kmart is committed to ensuring that Councils are provided with appropriate support for trolley management. However, Kmart believes that it is reasonable to expect that all Councils ensure the following:

• Nominate an officer/ officers to be responsible for liaison with our stores;
• Make a contact list of Council officers responsible for trolley management available to all our stores;
• Develop a policy on time limits for collection of trolleys;
• Enforce penalties on individuals who abandon trolleys in public places (for example - by amending their respective littering legislation);
• Aid our business in the design and implementation of any public education campaigns;
• Council should provide at least 24 hours' notice to Kmart when they find an abandoned trolley.

Working together with Councils

Kmart will aim to reach a mutually beneficial agreement with Councils regarding the collection of abandoned trolleys. Kmart will also aim to provide Councils with a tailored approach to trolley management - without deviating from this management plan.

Kmart expects that to work cohesively with Councils. We should be expected to employ educational initiatives for our customers. Kmart will undertake the following:
• Kmart will provide an 1800 number as a contact point for councils when reporting on abandoned trolley;
• Kmart will change the artwork on trolley handles to display:
    o The relevant 1800 telephone number; and

Purchasing Trolleys

Facilities will also be responsible for the purchase of new trolley fleets for existing stores in accordance with contracts and pricing obtained by then the supplier management team Store operations shall be responsible for the overall management of fleet numbers at all stores and, in conjunction with facilities, shall plan for future deployments, reallocations and disposals of the asset as required.

Store Operations

General responsibilities

Operations will be responsible for the day-to-day management of shopping trolleys. Store managers will be accountable for ensuring that their store has an adequate amount of trolleys for our customers.

Store management teams

Store management teams will be responsible for managing trolley contractors on a day-to-day basis. Stores will be expected to ensure the following:
• Appropriate sign in procedures are followed (including induction card and work method statement checks);
• Contractors are safe and courteous when working around customers and team members;
• Contractors wear high visibility uniforms always and possess a driver's license (where applicable);
• Contractors provide a plan of all street runs and a map of their coverage areas;
• Contractors ensure there is always an adequate supply of trolleys and provided for customer use - empty bays in store should not be tolerated;

• Large amounts of trolleys left in the car park or in the surrounding store areas should not be tolerated and returned to a trolley bay or to the correct internal store area

• Customer trolleys should not be used in the stockroom for replenishment purposes

• All complaints regarding contractor performance should be resolved at store level (where possible) using the below process
1. Discuss concerns directly with the contractor; (i.e. schedule regular meetings with onsite contractor)
2. If further discussion is needed - discuss with the supervisor
3. Should this not resolve the issue a Corrective Action Report (CAR) should be completed and provided to the contractor supervisor;
4. Where the CAR fails to deliver the desired outcome, the matter is then escalated to the national store services manager

Note: in any event the store manager should engage the principal trolley contractor when they are attempting to resolve a problem with their respective site contractors.

Contractor safety management

Trolley collection requires special management routines. In general, contractors and store teams should be guided as follows.
• Check that trolleys are in a safe operational condition prior to collection. If trolleys are faulty, tag it 'out of service' and report it to a manager so that a repair can be arranged. For example, check wheels are not damaged, seat belts are functional and there are no sharp protruding edges.
• Ensure personal protective equipment is worn, including safety vest/ high visibility clothing and weather permitting – hat, sunscreen, sunglasses and raincoat
• When undertaking collections in warmer weather maintain appropriate fluid intake to avoid dehydration and where possible rotate with fellow team members
• Always be vigilant and alert for moving vehicles in the carpark, in particular cars reversing for spaces
• The use of elasticated straps is strictly prohibited, as they can easily dislodge and cause serious injury
• Collectors should never use mobile phones and/ or similar devices while collecting trolleys

Additionally, from a safe process perspective, trolley contractors should:

1. Plan their route and determine the safest route to travel back and forth between shopping trolley bays and the store

2. Use the purpose designed trolley strap to secure trolleys when moving them, ensuring they collect no more than 10 trolleys at a time. This number should be reduced if required to ensure they always maintain control of them

3. Push the trolleys rather than pulling them along

4. When moving trolleys remember to give way to pedestrians

5. If trolleys are wet, ensure they are dried to minimize slip hazards in store. In the event water remains on the floor, the collector should arrange for it to be cleaned up and appropriate 'wet floor signage displayed

6. If required, the collector should also carefully remove rubbish out of any trolleys and wash their hands

The above guidelines should be read in conjunction with any contractual arrangements and safe work practices which are prepared from time to time by Kmart and or the contractor.

Dealing with Councils and the community

Given Kmart is an international business, and the disparity of council stances in relation to trolley management, Kmart has decided to co-ordinate Council engagements from national office - whilst using store managers as representatives and initial contact points for the Council and community.

The national store services manager will ensure that the store managers are provided with adequate support and guidance when dealing with their respective Council. The national store services manager will always make store managers aware of the business stance (and any changes).

With regards to other tenants within shopping centres, please see related resources, tools and links section in this policy for suggested letters that should be provided if you notice trolley misuse.

Store managers should also ensure that they forward any trolley related Council or centre management communications to the national store services manager for a response to be provided.

Removal of unsafe trolleys

Trolleys that are removed from service due to damage or operational issues are to be in the dedicated 'trolley graveyard' as indicated by the store manager.

Corporate affairs and sustainability

Kmart will engage the community where appropriate and provide the means to drive a cultural change - so that customers understand that trolleys are provided as a service whilst they shop in our stores.

Kmart will ensure the corporate affairs and sustainability team is engaged when entering any kind of public education program.

Kmart believes that any kind of education campaign should be general in nature to cover the range of our customers effectively.

Examples of public education Kmart employs include:
• Signage within stores and car parks
• Signage at entry and exit points of stores and car parks
• Signage on trolleys
• Council information regarding penalties for people leaving trolleys unattended in public places
• In-store radio announcements
• Trolley design changes and handlebar artwork
