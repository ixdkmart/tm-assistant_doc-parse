Price Matching Process
Upon Customer Request

We have aligned the process across both brands for matching a price in store when a customer requests.

Only on customer request Kmart and Target will match the advertised price on any identical stocked national ranged item we sell in store (excludes competitors online only products, see exclusions below).

This is an internal policy only and not for display to customers.

Assisting the customer's request:
• Customer is to request a price match.
• Customer is to take the product and their proof of lower competitor price to, Kmart Services Desk (NZ Central Registers) or Target Customer Service Point, for verification.
• If further confirmation is required, the team member is to contact the retail competitor to confirm advert is valid and in stock.
• Once verified, purchase must be made in that visit and in reasonable retail quantities for personal use.

Key conditions/points for applying the process:
• Products are to be identical i.e. the same brand, same model and same colour.
• Products are in stock and available in store at another Australian retailer in the required quantities the customer wishes to purchase.
• Proof of lower competitor price includes catalogue advert (newspaper or magazine), social media advert, online advert or digital catalogue, competitor website instore price or a customer photo from an eligible competitor store.
• For Percentage (%) Off offers we will match the competitor's price for products that meet the above criteria after the discount has been applied to competitors' regular price (not the % off our price).
    - Example; Competitor Regular price $10, less 20% discount = $8

The process does not apply to the following Exclusions:
• Online only offers, products on clearance, online retailers, clearance & warehouse outlets.
• iTunes vouchers, gift cards and Anko products
• International advertisements, international online retailers and promotions.

Note: This communication is for internal Kmart Group use only, and any content in part or full is not to be shared externally through any means including social media.
