Licence to Greet Competency Checklist

For a Team Member to be allocated to the Role of the Customer Greeter, they must be:
• A suitable candidate for the role (as per the Customer Greeter position description.
• Have completed all induction training when they first joined Kmart.
• Have completed the body worn safety camera LMS module (where the store has a safety camera).
• Have been a team member at the store for a minimum of 3 months and worked 60 hours).

It is recommended that all scenario-based training outlined below be provided with the support of an allocated buddy who already has their licence to greet qualification.

The below observations should be carried out, to ensure the team member is competent. If any items are marked not yet competent, managers should work with the team member with additional training and coaching until they are deemed competent.

Requirement/Training | What Competent Looks Like | C | NYC

Team member to discuss how they would manage the following customer threatening situations:
• Verbal threatening situation.
• Physical threatening situation.
• Weapons used in a threatening situation.

• Putting the 4Ds into effect.
• Notifying a manager.

Team member discusses how to identify suspicious behaviour and how to report it.

Offenders can be anyone, there is no standard profile but there are red flags to look for:
• Bringing in large or multiple bags and frequently looking around or avoiding a team member
• Who display unusual behaviour, like heading straight to high-value areas or bypassing checkouts
• People entering and exiting the store as a group
• Monitoring people loitering around the store or entry/exits.

If any suspicious behaviour is observed, it must be reported to a manager over a P2T.

Team member demonstrates reporting a Profit Protection incident.

Team member to advise where to locate forms and complete a mock up incident.

Team member demonstrates how to use the receipt scanner, including bag checks. (reference the Receipt Scanner Process Guide in this scenario).

Team members greets the customer upon exit and asks, may I scan your receipt please. Scan the receipt and respond as per the guide to each alert. In conjunction conduct a bag check to ensure all products have been paid for.

Team Member can describe what to do in each of the following incidents:

1. A trolley run (no payment).
2. Customer has a receipt but listed items in bag not on receipt. Customer has no receipt on way out.
3. Customer has no receipt for a refund.
4. Refusing to show receipt for scanning or bag checks.

1. Ask the customer to stop, otherwise let them go and report to a manager, completing a incident form. Never chase the customer out of the store.
2. Team member to advise customer, there are items here not listed on the receipt, could you go back to the registers so these items can be paid for. If refused, let them go and notify a manager, completing a incident form.
3. Identify what the refund type is. If a change of mind advises the customer they will need a receipt. If faulty, direct them to the service desk and advise the service desk team the customer is on their way.
4. Continue to greet the customer with 'have a nice day' and let them go.
