2023 KAwards Criteria

Think customer
- Makes a significant impact on customers and considers the customer first in all decisions
- Delivers continued, exceptional customer experiences that significantly exceed customer expectations
- Role models and actively promotes a customer-focused culture, including anticipating customer needs and prioritising work activities to deliver

Take care
An individual or team nominated for a KAward in this category will have gone above and beyond their role to create team members health and wellbeing a prioritised, resulting in a significant positive impact on team members wellbeing at scale.
- Proactively embeds and actively creates a culture of care for others
- Has a significant positive impact on the health, safety, and wellbeing of others
- Consistently promotes and role models safe work practices and behaviours

Live integrity
An individual or team nominated for a KAward in this category will have demonstrated integrity in all that they do. They regularly and honestly speak up about concerns and will have played a key role improving the integrity of our processes, policies or the way we work across Kmart.
- Exemplifies ethical behaviours and consistently conducts themselves in an open and honest way
- Highly trustworthy and credible and does what they say they will
- Role models good decision making and chooses to do the right thing, even if it's difficult

Grow together
An individual or team nominated for a KAward in this category will consistently role model cross-collaboration and partnering with stakeholders outside of their own team. They actively share knowledge with the intent to improve how things are done and seek to create the best outcomes by working with others.
- Consistently inspires others to succeed and deliver results together
- Demonstrates leadership by the way they share knowledge in areas they do not work
- Values and seeks out the opinion of others to create a culture of cross-functional collaboration

Reach higher
An individual or team nominated for a KAward in this category will have played a key role in an initiative that has delivered an impact to the Kmart business on a large scale. They will be early adopters of new and better ways to do things and drive positive outcomes through their influence on others or teams.
- Embraces and leads significant change to benefit Kmart
- Delivers exceptional outcomes by making the complex simple
- Is ambitious for growth and is unwavering in their pursuit to improve themselves and Kmart

Deadly
For being committed to reconciliation by fostering respect, creating opportunities, and building a culture where First Nations people feel welcome within our business. An individual or team nominated for a KAward in this category will have forged strong relationships with First Nations communities and have made significant steps to strengthen the role Kmart plays in their communities.
- Relationships: Fosters connections between Kmart and Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities with authenticity and respect
- Respect: Creates a culture that ensures customers, team members, community members and suppliers feel welcome in our business
- Opportunities: Increases Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander representation in our workforce to better reflect the communities in which we operate
