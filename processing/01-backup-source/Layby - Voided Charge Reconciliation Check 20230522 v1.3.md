## Page 1

VOIDED LAY-BY CHARGE PAYMENTS RECONCILIATION

What is this for?
Voided lay-by payments are to be reconciled using the Escape system as there is no record of the voids listed on the SATR.

When a transaction has been voided, the 'Print on Demand' receipt is to be attached to the register's K162 slip with an explanation then with the Customer Service Manager's authorisation or a member of the management team.

Voided lay-by payment transactions should be followed by a correct transaction for that charge number.

What should the Escape search look like?
[Image shows Escape system search interface with various fields and options]

'-v and paymnts' is reviewed on all registers as lay-by payments can be made on any register.

Once you have located any transactions you should also investigate why the transaction was voided.
