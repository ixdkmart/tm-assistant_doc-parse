Primary Purpose of Role
Customer Greeter. Ability to acknowledge, assist and direct customers in a professional and friendly manner as they arrive in store. Acknowledge customers as they leave in a professional and friendly manner checking receipts using the receipt scanner and checking bags to ensure items in bags match what is on the customer receipt as they leave the store. Maintain strong awareness to escalating customer situations and utilise advanced de-escalation strategies to promote positive customer experience where possible.

Position Scope

Area of Accountability | Key Responsibilities & Deliverables | Performance Measures & Targets

Customer, contractors and visitors: 
Ensuring Verified is completed as Contractors and Visitors arrive on site. Manager called once the Contractors and / or Visitors arrive

>Verified filled out as per Policy and Procedure
>Validation by Operations Manager / Service Manager
>All Contractors and Visitors are signed in and out, to ensure accurate record keeping

Greeting Customers and assisting with enquiries:
Greets & acknowledges customers upon entering and exiting the store. Creates positive and inclusive interaction with all customers. Engages with customers to assist with general enquiries relating to refunds/exchanges, store layout, general product location, customer service areas, current catalogue/ promotion

>Ability to assist / direct customers to areas of the store. >NPS ratings of store
>Sound knowledge of where products are located around the store

Store entrance presentation, housekeeping and PPE:
Maintaining appearance of store entry by ensuring the current catalogues are on display, the area is free from slip or trip hazards e.g. rubbish, coat hangers. Removing trolleys and baskets and stocking in appropriate locations and wiping down trolleys and baskets. Maintaining PPE in good order (Hand sanitiser)

>Feedback from managers during floor walks and customer paths
>Area visually clear and maintained to the highest of standards

Safe handling of Customer threatening Situations:
Have a sound understanding of all Kmart Group's Policies and Procedures that relate to Customer Threatening Situations. Maintain composure while implementing the strategies surrounding, Distance, De-escalation, Disengaging and Debriefing. Responsible to ensure the end to end scope of de-escalation is followed at all times and advanced problem solving strategies are used to navigate the unique customer interactions.

>Feedback from observations conducted by store leadership team.
>Licence to greet available upon request.
>Demonstrate ability to perform with a member of the store leadership team.
