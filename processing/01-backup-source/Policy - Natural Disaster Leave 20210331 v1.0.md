Policy - Natural Disaster Leave

INTRODUCTION AND PURPOSE
This policy sets out a team member's benefit to leave when impacted by natural disasters which affect them personally and their immediate family.

The policy outlines a team members entitlement to take natural disaster leave in the circumstances which qualify as a natural disaster.

POLICY APPLICATION
Full-time, part-time and casual team members of Kmart will receive the benefit of natural disaster leave in accordance with this policy, unless they are covered by an Enterprise Agreement or Award which provides a more beneficial benefit.

This policy can be varied or withdrawn at any time and does not form part of a team members contract of employment.

COUNTRY APPLICATION
Australia

POLICY
Natural Disaster
For the purposes of this policy a natural disaster is defined to include a serious disruption to community life, threatening death, injury or damage to property.

Natural disasters include cyclones, flooding and bushfires.

The purpose of the entitlement is for impacted team members to:
• ensure the protection of themselves and their families;
• secure their residence;
• undertake temporary repairs;
• restore belongings;
• clean up.

Up to 3 days leave per year
A full-time or part-time team member who is impacted by a natural disaster or whose immediate family is impacted by a natural disaster is entitled to access up to 3 days paid leave per year (pro rata for a part time team member), non-cumulative.

Payment for natural disaster leave for permanent team members is at the relevant base rate of pay.

A casual team member can take up to 3 days unpaid leave per year.

Notice and expected period of absence
Notice must be given to Kmart as soon as possible, which may be at a time after the leave has started.

A team member, where possible, must advise Kmart of the period or expected period of the absence.

Evidence
Where required by their Line Manager, a team member will provide evidence that the absence is because the team member has been or will be impacted by a natural disaster.

Continuous Service
Leave, whether paid or unpaid, granted in accordance with this policy will be treated as continuous service.

Applying for Natural Disaster Leave
Team members apply for Natural Disaster Leave on-line through Success Factors.
Line managers authorise Natural Disaster Leave on-line through Success Factors.

Employees Affected by School Closures and other Carer's Responsibilities
This policy does not apply to absences from work resulting from a need to provide care or support to an immediate family or household member, resulting from a natural disaster.

Please refer to the policy Personal Leave Policy.

RELATED RESOURCES, TOOLS AND LINKS
