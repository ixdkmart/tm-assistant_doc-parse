Refund Approval Guide

All refunds must be processed as per the limits and requirements outlined below and are recorded within the EJ and Escape system.

Refund Limit | Authorised By | Authorisation Requirements

Refund transactions over $100 and up to $250.00. | Level 2 or 3 Team Member.
• Must be present when the refund is being processed.
• Must not share authorisation barcodes.
• Must sight all items being returned and check all items being refunded, ensuring they are the same items listed on the receipt.
• Sight refund issued using the original payment method or as per the refund and returns guidelines/policy.
• Cannot process or approve a refund for themselves and the refund should be processed by a different Manager or Team Member.

Refunds over $250.00. | Managers with Level 3 ID.
• Must be present when the refund is being processed.
• Must not share authorisation barcodes.
• Must sight all items being returned and check all items being refunded, ensuring they are the same items listed on the receipt.
• Sight refund issued using the original payment method or as per the refund and returns guidelines/policy.
• Cannot process their own refunds.

The operator ID that is used to approve a refund is shown on the POS Electronic Journal and Back Office Escape systems, and the store copy of the refund receipt. This assists the management team in identifying the operator that has approved the refund.
