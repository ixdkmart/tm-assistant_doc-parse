Teamwear Recycling Guide

Introduction
Kmart in conjunction with a clothing recycling company called Upparel have come together to provide a sustainable option for managing old used Kmart teamwear.

Teamwear Collection
Step | Action
Step 1 | Set up designated dumpbin in the stores tearoom.
Step 2 | Ensure all team members are aware that any old unneeded teamwear can be returned and placed in dumpbin for recycling. Note teamwear should be returned clean (washed & dry).
Step 3 | Regularly monitor the returned teamwear being placed in the bin.
Step 4 | When the dumpbin becomes full the teamwear should be sent to the designated recycling company, see below process.
Note: | This is only for Kmart Teamwear tops (shirts, polos, jackets, etc) and excludes footwear and any other clothing.

Teamwear Recycling Return
Step | Action
Step 1 | When the dumpbin becomes full teamwear is to be sent to the recycling company via the RLO as follows.
Step 2 | Sort through dumpbin contents and place the teamwear in empty cartons, isolate any non-teamwear items as they are not to be sent.
Note: Dumpbin can become heavy when full of teamwear, sort into the empty cartons in tearoom.
Step 3 | Seal the carton/s and place a 'Upparel Recycling' sticker on the top of the carton/s, write store number at the bottom of sticker.
(stickers reorderable from IVE Group, code KMA95026422).
Step 4 | Place the carton/s on the RLO Claimable pallet (there is no need to scan the carton into the system).
Step 5 | When the Claimable pallet is full send to RLO as per normal process.
Note: | Only Kmart Teamwear tops (shirts, polos, jackets, etc) are to be returned, excludes footwear and any other clothing.
