Policy - Team Member Discount Card

INTRODUCTION
AND PURPOSE

Kmart and Target ("the Company") provide a discount card ("card") to eligible team members and retirees as a reward for their contribution to the business.

POLICY
APPLICATION

This policy applies to all team members employed by the Company and retirees who meet the eligibility criteria defined below. The Company may vary this policy or end discount privileges at any time and without notice. Team members should not view discount privileges as an automatic right.

COUNTRY
APPLICATION

All

CONDITIONS OF
USE

Eligibility

Permanent Australia and New Zealand employed team members
Permanent full-time and part-time team members are entitled to two cards following commencement of employment.

Ongoing Casual Australia and New Zealand employed team members
Ongoing casual team members are entitled to one card after completing three months' employment. Ongoing casual team members who are rehired must again complete three months' employment before they are entitled to a card. Team members hired as holiday casuals are not eligible unless they become ongoing.

Australia and New Zealand retirees
Team members who are 55 years or over and retire after a minimum 15 years' service in Australia or New Zealand, are entitled to one retiree card for an indefinite period from the date of retirement.

Ongoing provision of a card to retirees is subject to review from time to time. Upon retirement the existing team member card is cancelled and a new retiree card is sent to the retiree's home address.

Retiree cards can be used by the retiree and their partner only and can include both their signatures on the one card.

On the death of a retiree, the card may be retained by their partner. No other person may use the card.

Globally employed team members
Anko, Anko Global and Sourcing team members travelling to Australia and/or New Zealand for business or leisure are eligible to receive a temporary card for the duration of their trip. Team members should notify their Executive Assistant or Office Manager to obtain the card, which will be deactivated at the conclusion of travel.

Globally employed General Managers will be automatically issued a permanent card and frequent travellers may be issued a permanent card on request to the Australian Remuneration and Benefits team.

Discount cards
New team member and retiree cards are ordered twice per month and sent to the team member's mailing address as recorded in SuccessFactors. It can take up to six weeks from commencement or the three-month anniversary, as applicable, for the card(s) to arrive. The letter enclosed with the card(s) details the current discounts available.

Cards are issued based on the eligibility criteria. No discount is available until the valid card or cards have been received and signed.

Each card issued to Australia and New Zealand employed permanent team members will be issued in the name of the team member, with provision for a single user's signature on the back of the card. The second card is provided to allow discounts on household purchases for one immediate family member. It is recognised that modern families may not all reside in the same household and therefore the second card may be issued to one immediate family member who resides outside the team member's household. The immediate family member's signature should appear on the second card, not the team member's.

Australia and New Zealand employed ongoing casual team members may use their card or may nominate one immediate family member to use the card. Only the individual person whose signature appears on the back of the card may use the card.

Globally employed team members will receive a card with no team member name but should sign the back of the card issued to them.

By signing their card upon receipt, the team member acknowledges and accepts this policy and the conditions of use.

The card(s) remains the property of the Company and may be retained at point of sale if invalid or if fraudulently used. The card(s) must not be copied or reproduced and must be returned to the Company when requested.

Immediate family member
An immediate family member for the purpose of team discount is a spouse or former spouse, de facto partner or former de facto partner, child, parent, grandparent, grandchild or sibling of an employee, or a child, parent, grandparent, grandchild or sibling of an employee's spouse or de facto partner. It includes step-relations (e.g. step-parents and step-children) as well as adoptive relations.

Discount card usage
The card(s) can only be used for purchases for the team member's, and where applicable their immediate family member's, household consumption and cannot be used to make purchases for non-household or business use.

No person other than the team member or their immediate family member who has signed the back of the card (an authorised user) may use it.

The discount can be received by authorised users in the following ways:
• when the card is presented to the point-of-sale operator prior to the completion of the transaction and provided the authorised user signs a receipt or charge slip if requested. If the card is not presented, no discount can be given.
• at Kmart and Target self-service checkouts, by scanning the card.
• online via Kmart or Target's apps and websites, by entering the barcode number into the payment section when finalising the transaction.

Kmart store and distribution centre team members are entitled to an additional discount on uniform items specified on the inclusion list in the Kmart Workwear Clothing Discount Policy. This policy is available via the Company's internal communication channels and sets out the steps to receive this additional discount.

To receive a refund for transactions where the team member discount has been applied, the team member must show their card for the refund to be processed.

Team members with access to the Kmart Team App can scan their card barcode into the app to use for purchases and returns in lieu of their physical card. The home screen of the app with team member name will also need to be shown.

Leave of absence and parental leave
Team members on approved leave of absence or parental leave retain their team member discount card privileges.

Discount rates
Discount rates vary from business to business and between categories of merchandise and can change from time to time. The discount may not be available in conjunction with other discounts or promotional offers.

The Company reserves the right to change or discontinue any discount at any time and without notice. Current discount rates are published via the Company's internal communications channels.

Finalisation of employment
Upon leaving the Company, entitlement to the team discount ceases and all cards issued to the team member will be deactivated and will no longer be valid for use.

Team members should return their discount cards, along with other company property, to their manager as part of their offboarding process.

Replacement cards
If a card is lost or stolen, team members will be required to complete the appropriate team member or retiree discount card replacement form.

Replacement cards will be processed in the next bi-monthly order upon receipt of the fully completed form.

Abuse of discount or privileges
Failure to comply with or abuse of this policy may result in the loss of discount entitlement and possible disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment.

Non-compliance or abuse at point of sale will result in seizure of the card.
