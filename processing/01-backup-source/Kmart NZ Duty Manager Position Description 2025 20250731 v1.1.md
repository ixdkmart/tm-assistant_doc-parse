Duty Manager (NZ)
Position Description

As one of Aotearoa's most loved retailers, Kmart has been bringing everyday products at low prices to Kiwi families for over 35 years. With stores and a passionate team across the country, we are proud to deliver great value on trend products that make everyday living brighter. Our team is diverse, inclusive, and driven by results. We constantly focus on evolving our experience to meet the needs of our community. At Kmart, we celebrate what makes you unique. It's not just what you do, but who you are that helps make a difference every day.

Position Title | Function | Reports To | Level of Role | Years of Experience Required for Role
---|---|---|---|---
Department Supervisor | Stores | Department Manager | Team Member | 1 Year in retail environment

Kmart Duty Managers, are the key support for Department Managers across our store categories. They support with processing inventory, rolling out decant, sequence and leading our presentation teams when required. Duty Managers are supporters in how we operate across our stores to make the most of all our shifts and continue to lead our processes. Looking at Visual merchandising and being the expert in their categories to get the product in the right place, always at the right time.

Position Relationships:
Key Internal Stakeholders | Key External Stakeholders | Number of Direct Reports | Number of Indirect Reports
---|---|---|---
Team Members, Line Manager, Store Manager | Center Management, Security, Customers | 0 | 0

Experience and Expertise:
• Duty Managers should have a good understanding of Kmart store layout and understanding of daily processes
• Have confidence in being able to give basic direction to other team members when processing daily and weekly tasks.
• Ability to adjust their communication style to get the best out of the store teams to boost productivity
• Good understanding of Kmart safety procedures and able to support in case of any incidents and support with retraining where required.
• High level of self motivation, a proactive mindset and the ability to spot and respond to opportunities for improvement.

Technical Ability:
• Strong attention to detail, good planning and organisation skills
• Ability to manage multiple priorities effectively
• Socially and personally aware of immediate customer demands and challenges.
• Ensure team members receive appropriate safety instruction, training and supervision whilst working in the department.

Who you are:
• You are someone who enjoys leading through others
• You are a team player who works well with others to achieve store objectives
• You establish and have proven to demonstrate high standards of work and presentation.
• You have an ability to make time responsive decisions and support decision making with your department manager.
