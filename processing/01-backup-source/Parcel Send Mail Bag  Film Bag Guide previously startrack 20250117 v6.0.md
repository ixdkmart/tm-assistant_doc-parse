How to book a mail bag for collection with Parcel send

Step 1
Log in to the Australia post log in page via the Australia post parcel send link available on KIT
The email address for your log is the K####ma02@kmart.com.au
If you have forgotten your password, you can select "forgot password" on the log in page and instructions will be sent to your email

Step 2
Select the parcel send app- Make sure the mail bag account is selected at the top
Note: You can see there is 3 accounts the Store Named one if for token returns, the mail bag, one is selected for sending the mail bag to national office and Photo Crate is for sending film direct to the vendor address to be processed.

Step 3
You will now see the parcel send booking system

To continue to book the mail bag for collection select create return
Note: For film please see further down in the process as this is a transfer.

Stationary:

For log in support contact: MAILROOM-MULGRAVEKMART@kmart.com.au
