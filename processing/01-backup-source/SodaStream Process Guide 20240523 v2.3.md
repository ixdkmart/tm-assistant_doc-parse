Soda Stream Sale Process

Stores will no longer be receiving exchange cylinders from SodaStream to be held at the Service Desk. Instead we will be selling stock of the 60L single cylinder (KC 41614198) which will be merchandised on the shop floor and can be purchased through any register.

Step 1
Update 'Soda Stream' planogram to include new POS.

Soda Steam planogram available on Kit: Resources>Stores>Merchandise>Home World>Kitchen & Dining
POS: Will be delivered to stores via Soda Stream, or installed by Soda Stream Representative

Step 2
Customer can purchase 60L single cylinder (KC 41614198) at any service point (Manned Register / SCO / Service Desk). Customer/TM scans the boxed full cylinder. Item will scan as Visual Verify on register.

Customer/TM to scan box.
Do not scan empty cylinder.
Item will flag as 'Visual Verify Item'

Step 3
If customer is just purchasing a new cylinder without wanting to exchange with an empty, select 'OK' and continue with transaction as per normal process

If customer is exchanging an empty cylinder, navigate to 'Price Change' and select Reason Code 'Price/Scan Error'.
Change price to $19AU
Retrieve empty canister from customer.
Customer/TM can continue on with transaction.

Step 4
Selected the 'Further Options' icon
Select 'Price Change'
