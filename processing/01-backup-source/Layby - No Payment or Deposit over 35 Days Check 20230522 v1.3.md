## Page 1

35 DAYS CHARGES WITH NO DEPOSIT / PAYMENTS

What is it for?
This report is automatically printed each Monday and allows the store to review any laybys that have not had payments made (and may be potentially unwanted) and return to stock those not required, prior to their 8-week term.

What does it look like?
[Sample report image showing:]
- Charges have been cancelled (A)
- Store cancel receipts attached to report (B)
- Parcel contents have been ticked (C)
- Management signature (D)

What should I look for?
A. All charges in the Charges more than 35 days old section have been cancelled.
B. Store cancel receipts have been attached to the report
C. Parcel contents have been physically ticked to APN details printed on the receipt.
D. Management signs the report to confirm all dockets are on hand.
