Policy – Team Member Discount Card

INTRODUCTION
AND PURPOSE
Kmart and Target (together referred to as "the Company") provide a physical or digital discount card ("card") to eligible Team Members and Retirees for household use as a reward for their contribution to the business.

POLICY APPLICATION
This policy applies to all Team Members employed by the Company and Retirees who meet the eligibility criteria defined in this policy. The Company may vary this policy or amend or end discount privileges at any time and without notice. Team Members should not view discount privileges as an entitlement.

COUNTRY APPLICATION
All

POLICY
Eligibility

Permanent Team Members (Australia and New Zealand)
Permanent full-time and part-time Team Members are entitled to two cards following commencement of employment.

Fixed-term Team Members (Australia and New Zealand)
Fixed-term full-time and part-time Team Members are entitled to one card following commencement of employment.

Ongoing casual Team Members (Australia and New Zealand)
Ongoing casual Team Members are entitled to one card after completing three months' employment. Ongoing casual Team Members who are rehired must again complete three months' employment before they are entitled to a card.

Team Members hired as holiday casuals are not eligible unless they become ongoing. Team Members hired as holiday casuals that are offered ongoing casual employment are entitled to one card after they have completed a minimum of three months' employment and their ongoing casual employment is confirmed.

Retirees (Australia and New Zealand)
Team Members who are 55 years or over and retire after a minimum 15 years' service in Australia or New Zealand, are entitled to one Retiree physical card for an indefinite period from the date of retirement. Ongoing provision of a card to Retirees is subject to review from time to time.

Upon retirement the existing Team Member card(s) are deactivated and a new Retiree card is sent to the Retiree's home address.

Retiree cards can be used by the Retiree and their partner only and can include both their signatures on the one card. In the event of the death of a Retiree, the card may be retained by their partner. No other person may use the card.

Global Team Members (outside of Australia and New Zealand)
Anko, Anko Sourcing and Anko Global Team Members travelling to Australia and/or New Zealand for business or leisure are eligible to receive a temporary physical card for the duration of their trip. Team Members should notify their Executive Assistant or Office Manager to obtain the card, which will be deactivated at the conclusion of travel.

Globally employed General Managers will be automatically issued a permanent physical card and other frequent travellers may be issued a permanent physical card on request to the Australian Remuneration and Benefits team.

Discount cards

Digital discount cards
Digital cards will be automatically provided to eligible permanent and fixed-term Team Members within one week from commencement and to eligible ongoing casual Team Members within one week of passing their three-month service anniversary. Digital cards are issued via the Company's internal communications application (currently Workvivo).

As part of the onboarding process into Kmart or Target, the Team Member will be required to acknowledge and accept this policy and the conditions of discount card use.

Physical discount cards
Retirees, globally employed Team Members and Secondary Card Holders are entitled to physical cards only. Physical cards are also available to eligible permanent and fixed-term Team Members who do not wish to use a digital card. Physical cards are obtained by completing the Team Member Discount Card Form found on the Brighter Benefits space on Workvivo.

Physical cards are ordered once per month and sent to the Team Member's mailing address as recorded in SuccessFactors or the address of the nominated Secondary Card Holder as advised by the Team Member. It can take up to six weeks from ordering for the card(s) to arrive.

No discount is available until the valid card or cards have been received and signed and only the individual person whose name and signature appears on the back of the card may use the card. Globally employed Team Members will receive a physical card with no Team Member name but should sign the back of the card issued to them. By signing their card upon receipt, the Team Member or Secondary Card Holder acknowledges and accepts this policy and the conditions of discount card use.

The card(s) remains the property of the Company and may be retained at point of sale if invalid or if fraudulently used. The card(s) must not be copied or reproduced and must be returned to the Company when requested.

Replacement cards

If a physical card is lost or stolen, the replacement form should be completed via Workvivo. Retirees should contact the Advisory Centre to order a replacement card.

Replacement physical cards will be processed in the next monthly order upon receipt of the fully completed form.

Immediate family member cards
A second (physical) card is available to permanent Team Members to allow discounts on household purchases for one immediate family member (also referred to as the "Secondary Card Holder" in this policy). It is recognised that modern families may not all reside in the same household and therefore the second card may be issued to one immediate family member who resides outside the Team Member's household. The secondary card will be ordered in the name of the immediate family member and must only be used by the person whose name is on the card. The immediate family member's signature should appear on the second card, not the Team Member's.

Australia and New Zealand employed ongoing casual and fixed-term Team Members may use their digital or physical card or may nominate one immediate family member to use their card. If a physical card is ordered for use by an immediate family member, the casual or fixed-term Team Member's digital card will be deactivated.

Immediate family member definition
An immediate family member for the purpose of team discount is a spouse or former spouse, de facto partner or former de facto partner, child, parent, grandparent, grandchild, sibling, aunty, uncle, cousin, niece or nephew of an employee, or a child, parent, grandparent, grandchild or sibling of an employee's spouse or de facto partner. It includes step-relations (e.g. stepparents and stepchildren) as well as adoptive relations.

Discount card usage
The card(s) can only be used for purchases for the Team Member's, and, where applicable, their immediate family member's, household consumption and cannot be used to make purchases for non-household or business use.

No person other than the Team Member or their immediate family member whose name and signature appears on the card (an authorised user) may use it.

The discount can be received by authorised users in the following ways:
• when the physical or digital card is presented to the point-of-sale operator prior to the completion of the transaction and provided the authorised user signs a receipt or charge slip if requested. If the card is not presented, no discount can be given.
• at Kmart and Target self-service checkouts, by scanning the physical or digital card.
• online via Kmart or Target's apps and websites, by entering the barcode number into the payment section when finalising the transaction.

To receive a refund for transactions where the Team Member discount has been applied, the Team Member must show their card for the refund to be processed.

Leave of absence and parental leave
Team Members on approved leave of absence or parental leave retain their Team Member discount card privileges.

Discount rates
Discount rates vary from business to business and between categories of merchandise and can change from time to time. The discount may not be available in conjunction with other discounts or promotional offers.

Current discount details can be found on the Brighter Benefits space on Workvivo.

The Company reserves the right to change or discontinue any discount at any time and without notice.

Finalisation of employment
Upon leaving the Company, entitlement to the team discount ceases and all cards issued to the Team Member will be deactivated and will no longer be valid for use.

Team Members should return any physical cards, along with other company property, to their manager as part of their offboarding process.

Abuse of discount or privileges
Failure to comply with or abuse of this policy may result in the loss of discount entitlement and possible disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment.

Non-compliance or abuse at point of sale will result in seizure and/or deactivation of the card.
