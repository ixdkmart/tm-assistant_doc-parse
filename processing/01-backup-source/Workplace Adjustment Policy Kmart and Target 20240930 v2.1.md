Policy – Workplace Adjustments

INTRODUCTION AND PURPOSE
Workplace Adjustments provide candidates and Team Members with disability greater accessibility to the workplace in order to apply for a position, undertake meaningful and equitable employment, assist them to perform the inherent requirements of their role, and to progress their career.

At Kmart and Target (together referred to as "the Company"), we encourage diversity and inclusion as we know when people from varying backgrounds and cultures come together it enables us to innovate, make better business decisions and create the most value for our Team Members, our customers and our community.

This Workplace Adjustments Policy (Policy) outlines the responsibilities of our Company leaders and employees to effectively implement workplace adjustments for Team Members and candidates. The Company recognise that improving accessibility within the workplace will not only meet the legislative requirements under the Disability Discrimination Act 1992 (DDA) and other relevant legislation but will assist us to attract and retain a diverse workforce. By introducing workplace adjustments, we will assist in enabling the following:

• Assist in enabling an individual to perform the inherent requirements of their role;
• Enable an individual to have equal opportunity in recruitment processes, promotion and ongoing development;
• Compliance with legislative requirements;
• Maximise productivity;
• Build a diverse and inclusive workforce;
• Improve accessibility within our working environments;
• Improve the experience for customers and Team Members; and
• Attract and retain great talent.

POLICY APPLICATION
This Policy applies to all Team Members of Kmart Australia and Target Australia. Where a Team Member's employment is covered by the terms of an industrial instrument, such as an enterprise agreement or a modern award, the terms of the industrial instrument will apply. However, if this Policy contains a more beneficial term, this Policy will apply to the extent of any inconsistency.

This Policy should be read in conjunction with any other relevant policies or procedures.

This Policy can be varied or withdrawn at any time and does not form part of, and is not incorporated into, a Team Member's contract of employment

COUNTRY APPLICATION
Australia

DEFINITIONS
Disability: For the purposes of this Policy, the Company adopt the definition of disability set out by the Disability Discrimination Act 1992 (Cth). In summary, disability can include physical, intellectual, psychiatric, sensory, mental health, neurological, learning disability, physical disfigurement and immunological - the presence in the body of disease-causing organisms.

Inherent Requirements: Inherent requirements are the core activities, tasks or functions that are required to perform a role productively, effectively and safely. The meaning of inherent requirements is affected by the Disability Discrimination Act 1992 (Cth) and other applicable law.

Job Access: Job Access is an Australian Government hub for workplace and employment information for people with disability, employers and service providers. Further information can be found https://www.jobaccess.gov.au/employment-assistance-fund-eaf

Workplace Adjustment: A workplace adjustment (also referred to as reasonable adjustments within the Disability Discrimination Act 1992 (Cth)) is a change to a work process, practice, procedure or environment. The provision of workplace adjustments will be unique for each Team Member because the impact of disability or health condition is different for each person. The meaning of workplace adjustment / reasonable adjustment is affected by the Disability Discrimination Act 1992 (Cth) and other applicable law.

Unjustifiable Hardship: In some situations, the Company will be unable to make a requested workplace adjustment, on the basis that it would amount to an unjustifiable hardship. An adjustment may amount to an unjustifiable hardship on grounds of the required expenditure, resourcing, or other relevant factors. The meaning of unjustifiable hardship is affected by the Disability Discrimination Act 1992 (Cth) and other applicable law.

POLICY
This Policy will be used to consider workplace adjustments for all eligible Team Members with a disability that impacts their ability to meet the inherent requirements of their role. To assist us to determine whether adjustments are reasonable, the Company will consider the following principles:

a) The positive impacts the workplace adjustment will have on the Team Member's ability to carry out the inherent requirements of the role as per the Disability Discrimination Act 1992 (Cth).
b) The positive impact the adjustment will have on the Team Member's independence and ability to engage in meaningful employment.
c) How the adjustment will interact with the Team Member concerned, customer and other workers.
d) The environmental or financial impacts of the adjustment, for example restrictions to the amendment of a building
e) Any other matter relevant to whether or not the requested adjustment may amount to an unjustifiable hardship.

This Policy will operate in conjunction with workers compensation policies and injury management guidelines, the flexible working arrangements policy, the flexible work policy (Flex Where, Flex When and Flex Well Program) and any other relevant policies.

Requesting Workplace Adjustments

• For candidates, workplace adjustments can be requested within the application form. For existing Team Members all requests for workplace adjustments must be submitted using the Request for Workplace Adjustment Form which can be found on KIT. This document will be referred to by the People and Capability Advisory Centre and known as the "Workplace Adjustment Passport (WAP)". A person can request a workplace adjustment at any time throughout the recruitment process or during their employment. Examples may include attending an interview, commencing a new role and a change to an existing role.

• In the first instance Team Members should discuss the request for workplace adjustment with their Line Manager. If further support is required, the Team Member or candidate should reach out to diversity@kmart.com.au.

• For requests requiring additional support such as interpreting services, equipment and environmental changes, the People and Capability Advisory Centre will engage the appropriate stakeholders, such as a Line Manager, Team Member and/or the Diversity and Inclusion Team for support.

• When submitting a request, a Team Member may choose to provide supporting documentation such as medical evidence or photos of an adjustment upfront. Additional information may be requested from the Team Member where it is necessary to consider, process or review the requested workplace adjustment.

Reaching a decision

• All requests will be sent to and reviewed by the People and Capability Advisory Centre in consultation with the Team Member concerned, the relevant Manager and other relevant stakeholders where necessary.

• Requests should take into consideration the Team Member's needs, workplace adjustment principles outlined in this policy, obligations under the Disability Discrimination Act 1992 (Cth), Flexible Working Policy and Injury Management policies, if applicable and other applicable law.

• Supporting documentation, such as medical evidence, a work capacity checklist or workplace assessments, may be required to be provided during this process.

• Responses to a request for a workplace adjustment will generally be provided within 30 days. Note that requests for flexible working arrangements made in accordance with the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) will generally be dealt with in accordance with the Flexible Working Arrangement process, and the timeframes required for those requests. For any modifications eligible for Job Access Funding this timeframe may be extended in line with Job Access requirements. The Diversity and Inclusion team will facilitate and provide updates as necessary.

Agreed workplace adjustments

Where the Company and an individual Team Member agree to a workplace adjustment, the following will apply:

• The workplace adjustment will be implemented as quickly as possible, and the Team Member will be made aware of timeframes.

• In some instances, other stakeholders or functions such IT or Property may need to be engaged to rollout the agreed workplace adjustment.

• Where applicable, details of supported wage will be provided to Advisory by the Line Manager.

• Workplace Adjustment Passports will be filed in the People and Capability Advisory Centre.

• All such arrangements will be subject to regular periodic review to ensure the needs of the Team Member, their team and the business are being met.
