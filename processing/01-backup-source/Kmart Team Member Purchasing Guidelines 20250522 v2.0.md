Team Member Purchasing Guidelines
(Sales, Collectables & Sought After Items)

Purpose
These guidelines outline the expectations and responsibilities of Team Members when purchasing or handling Kmart or Target merchandise, particularly sales items, collectables, and high-demand products while they are working. It ensures fairness, transparency, and alignment with the Kmart and Target's Code of Conduct and the Team Member Discount Card Policy.

Scope
This applies to all Team Members across Kmart stores in Australia and New Zealand and Target stores in Australia.

Guidelines
1. Adherence to the Kmart and Target Code of Conduct
All purchases must comply with the Company's Code of Conduct and the terms of use for the Team Member Discount Card Policy.

2. No Holding of Merchandise
Team Members must not reserve or hold items off the shopfloor or stockroom/reserves for future purchase. All items must remain available for customer to purchase.

3. Purchasing Timing
• All team member purchases (including online) must only be made:
   a) on a designated break;
   b) before the start of a shift; or
   c) after a shift has ended.
• Team Members are not permitted to process their own purchases/transactions while they are working. Purchases in store must be made during store opening hours.

4. Availability of Items
• Saleable items on the shopfloor must remain available to customers unless they are being purchased as permitted in accordance with these guidelines (e.g. outside of working times, or during a break).
• Items located in the stockroom must not be set aside or purchased before being made available to customers.
• Purchasing large quantities of stock must be approved by the Store Manager or Manager in Charge.

5. Use of Discount Card
• The Team Member discount card must be used in accordance with the Team Member Discount Card Policy.
• No person other than the Team Member or their immediate family member who has signed the back of the card (an authorised user) may use the Team Member discount card.
• Failure to comply with the Team Member Discount Policy may result in the loss of the discount entitlement and possible disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment.
• Non-compliance or abuse at point of sale may result in seizure of the card.

Examples & Clarification

Question | Answer

I'm looking for a specific Pokémon card. Can I buy it if I find it in the store? | Yes, only during your break or outside of a shift if the item is on the shopfloor and available to customers. The item must not be sourced from the stockroom or held aside to purchase later.

What is a large quantity? | What is considered a large quantity will depend on the circumstances. A large quantity will generally be an amount that could be resold for commercial purposes, buying most or all of one item or style could be considered a large quantity purchase.

Where can I find the Kmart and Target Code of Conduct and the Team Member Discount Card Policy? | These documents are available on KIT.
