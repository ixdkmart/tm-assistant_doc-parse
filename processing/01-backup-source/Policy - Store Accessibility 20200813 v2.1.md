Policy - Store Accessibility Policy

INTRODUCTION AND PURPOSE
Provide for equitable and dignified access to new stores and those areas of existing stores that undergo renovation or upgrade that require building approval; and

Provide greater certainty to those involved in the design, construction, certification and management of Kmart stores in relation to the level of access required.

POLICY APPLICATION
This policy applies to all team members working in the Store Operations, Facilities, Property and Store Development functions for Australian and New Zealand Stores

COUNTRY APPLICATION
Australia and New Zealand

POLICY
General Guidelines for Store Design, Construction and Development

Main Store Entrances

Kmart shall endeavour to design its stores on a single level selling floor model, ensuring maximum accessibility for customers, team members and other stakeholders. Kmart shall endeavour to ensure that there is level access at main store entrances. Where level access is not possible due to design restrictions, Kmart shall negotiate with its landlord for the provision of a ramp or lift access.

Back Dock Entrances

Kmart does not consider the back dock to be an "access point" for customer to our store. However, Kmart is committed to ensuring that all our customers be provided with equitable and dignified access when they wish to collect a bulky or oversized purchase from one of our back-dock areas. To ensure this commitment, Kmart shall provide the following amenities at the back-dock areas for all new or refurbished stores (in accordance with the note contained in this section below) to facilitate safe, equitable access:

• Designated customer car parking reserved for customer pick-ups only. These car spaces will be sized, where appropriate, in accordance with statutory requirements for accessible car spaces. Line marking and signage will also be provided to further highlight these car spaces;

• A bell or similar ringing device to alert Kmart team members of a waiting customer;

• A platform lift table for transporting bulky items from the dock level to the driveway level;
• Lighting for the back dock, customer parking and pickup areas; and
• Bollards and rails to ensure that customers and team members are kept segregated from reversing vehicles.

Note: the above section of the Policy in relation to back dock entrances shall apply whenever the business makes an application for a building approval for works being undertaken at a store that may impact access. Development approval may have been given some time before 1 May 2011, but it is only if building approval is applied for on or after 1 May 2011 that this section of the policy shall apply.

General Guidelines for Store Team Members

Kmart is required to comply with disability legislation across Australia, which mandates that a person with a disability has a right to have access to places used by the public (except where it would constitute unjustifiable hardship on the company). As such, it is against the law for public places to be inaccessible to people with a disability.

Kmart team members must ensure that they act in accordance with our internal equal opportunity policies and procedures, as amended from time to time.

When assisting customers with a disability, team members must ensure that they treat all customers with dignity and respect. Team members should understand that, with reference to this policy, when assisting a customer who may have a disability at the back dock, additional assistance may be required to ensure that the customer is afforded the best level of customer service possible. This may include but is not limited to helping the customer place any purchases in their vehicle or ensuring that the customer accesses and exits the loading dock area in a safe manner.

If any team member is unsure about how to provide an approbative level of customer service, then they should seek advice or assistance from their line manager.

General Guidelines for Store Management Team Members

In addition to the duties and responsibilities outlined in the "General Guidelines for Store Team Members" section above, it is expected that store management team members will:
