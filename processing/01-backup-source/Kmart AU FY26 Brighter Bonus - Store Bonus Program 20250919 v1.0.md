Brighter Bonus

Store Bonus Program

As a valued team member, you are eligible for a bonus when your store delivers sales growth against the same quarter last year. You can help grow the bonus pool by delivering exceptional service to our customers for a quality shopping experience.

How it works

Each quarter in FY26, a bonus pool will be created based on a percentage of your store's sales above sales for the same quarter last year. 1% of every dollar above last year's sales for the same quarter will form the store's bonus pool.

The bonus pool will then be shared amongst eligible wages-paid team members based on their hours worked for the quarter.

Each eligible team member's individual bonus is calculated as follows: the team member's total hours worked (divided by) the total store hours worked (multiplied by) the bonus pool.

Quarterly Sales TY | Quarterly Sales LY | Increase on Last Year | Bonus Pool Calculation | Bonus Pool

$1.1m | $1.0m | $100k | $100k multiplied by 1% | $1,000

$2.0m | $1.0m | $1.0m | $1.0m multiplied by 1% | $10,000

Example calculation of a team member bonus based on a bonus pool of $10,000

[Table]
Total store hours worked for the quarter | 15,000
Total hours worked by team member | 320
Team member % share of store bonus pool | 320 / 15,000 = 2.13%
Team member bonus* | $10,000 x 2.13% = $213

* Bonus will be awarded in the form of a benefit card

Program rules

Eligibility

To be considered eligible for a bonus, each wages-paid team member must:

• have worked a minimum of 30 hours during the quarter; and

• be employed by Kmart Group ("the Company") at the time of benefit card distribution or any cash payment.

Program administration

• Bonus payments will be made to eligible team members four times per year (as close to the end of the quarter as possible), based on the sales results of each store for each financial quarter (ending September, December, March and June).

• Annual leave, long service leave and time in lieu cash outs will not be included as hours worked for the purpose of the bonus calculation.

• Bonus amounts will be rounded down to the nearest dollar.

• Payment will be made in the form of a tax-free store benefit card up to the value of $250 per quarter and can be used at any Kmart store to purchase goods. Bonus amounts over $250 will be paid into the team member's nominated bank account, less any amounts required by law to withhold.

• Individual team member benefit cards will only be issued where a minimum bonus pool of $1,000 for Kmart stores and $200 for Khub stores has been achieved, and to those team members who meet the eligibility criteria as set out in these Program Rules.

• Where the total bonus pool is less than $1,000 for Kmart stores and $200 for Khub stores, the bonus pool will be allocated to the store for nominated in-store activities e.g. morning tea, team function, etc.

• The minimum individual team member benefit card issued will be $10. No benefit cards will be issued (and no payments will be made) for individual bonus amounts less than $10.

• Eligible team members who transfer between stores during the quarter will have their bonus calculated on a pro-rata basis according to their hours worked in each store.

• Eligible team members who transfer from wages to salary or salary to wages during the quarter will have their bonus pro-rated for their hours worked as a wages team member.

• If at any time during the period being measured and up until the time the bonus is due to be awarded or paid, the team member fails to meet expected standards of performance, behaviour or conduct, the team member may not be eligible for the bonus, at the Company's discretion.

• If allegations of misconduct have been made against a team member and the matter is being investigated, any bonus otherwise owed to that team member may be withheld pending the finalisation of the investigation.

• The Brighter Bonus - Store Bonus Program will be reviewed on an annual basis.

• The Company may change the terms and conditions of the Brighter Bonus - Store Bonus Program and amend payment levels or calculations at any time at the absolute discretion of the Managing Director, Kmart Group.

Benefit card use

• Benefit cards cannot be used for the purchase of any Kmart or third-party gift cards.

• Team members must present their team member discount card when using a benefit card.

• Benefit cards cannot be used online.

• Team members cannot use self-checkout when using their benefit card (i.e. must be served by a checkout operator at the point of sale).

• Team members cannot on-sell a benefit card. The name on the physical team member discount card or the Workvivo account for a digital discount card must match the team member details on the benefit card.

• The gift card refund policy will apply for goods purchased with a benefit card.
