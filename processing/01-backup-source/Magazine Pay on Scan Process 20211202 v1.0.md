QRC - Magazine Pay on Scan Process

The magazine category operates through a pay on scan process, where Kmart only pays the for magazines that are sold. Claims are automatically reconciled based on the scan data from each magazine and creating an auto replenishment when magazines ell quickly or in high volumes.

Magazine Pay on Scan Process
Step | Action
Info | This process is to be completed every Monday and Thursday when magazine deliveries arrive at store.
Step 1 | The receiving Team member receives a magazine delivery, checking the delivery documentation and ensuring the quantities are accurate.
Step 2 | If there is a discrepancy between the number of copies received and the amount specified on the delivery docket, raise a claim for replacement in the supplier portal as per the usual process. (Refer to the Magazine Returns - Ovato User Guide via the Kmart Portal.
Step 3 | Merchandise magazines in their display locations upon delivery. Expire titles should immediately be removed from the shop floor and securely destroyed.
Step 4 | Fill back stock and always maintain fixture stock on hand daily.

Reporting a Short Delivery
Step | Action
Info | To minimise stock loss and maintain product availability, it is critical that all short deliveries are reported so replacement stock can be sent.
Step 1 | Go to the Ovato website: http://www.gordonotch.com.au/gqweb/ and log into your account.
Info | The Ovato portal username is your Ovato account number. This can be located on the tax invoice
[Tax invoice image showing account number 837 245]
Your Ovato Portal password is set as kmart + your store number e.g. kmart1167
Step 2 | Select 'Report Shortages'
