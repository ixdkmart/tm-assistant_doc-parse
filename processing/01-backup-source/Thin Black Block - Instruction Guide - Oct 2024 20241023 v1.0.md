THIN BLACK PHOTO BLOCK - Assembly Guide

What You will need:
1. Photo Print
2. Block & black peg
3. Alignment Tool
4. Roller
5. Cutter
6. Photo Gloves
7. Cutting mat

1. Place the alignment tool against one corner of the Black Block.
   The tool must be in close contact with the tile along two sides.

2. Peel back 2 to 3 inches of the white release paper from the corner of the frame opposite the tool.
   Crease the layer so the flap lays flat.

3. Put on the photo gloves & brush any debris off the back of the customer's photo to ensure it is clean.

4. Place the photo in the corner of the alignment tool.
   Be sure to align to the corner to ensure there is no photo overhang.

5. Slowly peel away the white layer & roll the image onto the tile using the small roller.

Roll over the entire photo in all directions pressing firmly to fully adhere the photo to the block.

6. * Wear Safety Gloves when using a cutter *

Flip the Block face down & press firmly.

Using the roller cutter, trim the excess photo on two sides.

Hold the cutter at a slight angle to avoid damaging the panel.
Press firmly for a clean cut.

7. Place the finished Block into the protective sleeve along with the 1 x black peg.

Customers are to insert the black peg into the appropriate hole at the back before displaying the block.
