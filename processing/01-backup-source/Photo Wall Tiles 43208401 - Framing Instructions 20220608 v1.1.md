## Page 2

Framing Steps:

1. Place photo within the front frame (image 2), facing out
2. Place cardboard insert (image 4) BEHIND the photo. This will keep the photo tightly in place.
3. Clip both front and back of the frame together
4. Apply the double sided adhesive label in position on the back of the frame (as shown in image 5)

Final product can be placed inside the consumable pack with hanging instructions ready for customer collection.

How to...
1. Add image and backing to the frame
2. Click back in place and firmly to secure
3. Display or give!
