Watch out for.... Bike returns

Stores can order spare parts for customer returns and replacements via the service agent Hunter Leisure directly. Hunter Leisure hold most moving parts and accessories for bikes other than the pre-assembled components.

The most common return reason for customer built bikes is the cables being installed the wrong way around. The handle bars feed into the slot incorrectly, resulting in the cables causing issues with breaking, gears not changing properly, or catching on wheel spokes

Tips and Tricks before refunding....

Before refunding has your team checked for hidden accessories?

Sometimes product accessories can be hidden in tricky places and can be missed by our customers. Below are some suggestions on where to check:

• Appliance cords/product accessories may be inside product or wrapped up around the bottom

• Wireless mouse USB can be found underneath the mouse

• Screw packs for furniture can sometimes be stuck to the box with tape

Where to find instruction manuals for part numbers

Providing part numbers or pictures of the part can be extremely useful and can ensure an order is raised quickly.

1. Using the Kmart website www.kmart.com.au or www.kmart.co.nz enter the keycode or a description into the top search bar

2. Click on the item

3. Scroll down and find instruction manual under product details, click to open attachment

4. Obtain part number from instruction manual
