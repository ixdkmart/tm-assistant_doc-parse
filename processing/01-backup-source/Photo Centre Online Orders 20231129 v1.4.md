Important Information - Kmart Photos Online / Network Orders

1. Releasing a Network/Online Order for Print:

• Firstly, check that you have all the consumables to complete the order.
• Check that all printers are ON and NOT showing any errors.
    ○ If errors are found - DO NOT release the order
    ○ If no errors are found - before releasing the order, please check that it shows HOLD
• Select the order and click on Release Network Order, order will release into your Active Orders & begin printing.
• Once the order has finished printing click on the Release button.
    ○ If you do happen to cancel the order after printing, please contact the customer via their contact details.
• Once the order has finished printing the customer will be notified automatically within 12 hours, this allows time for you to make and complete all items of the order.
• PLEASE NOTE - If you do not have the time to complete the order within this timeframe, DO NOT RELEASE, instead contact the customer to advise of the delay.

2. Order Errors (Order fails to validate) – What to Do:

• DO NOT DELETE an order unless Kodak advises to do so or if you have been in contact with the customer.
• DO NOT press DELETE (Point 2 on below image) the order when in error (Point 1 on image below) as this will delete the order from your system.

• How to Clear an ERROR to allow printing:
    ○ Run a Device Health Check (Tools>Diagnostics>Health Checks>Device Health Checks)
    ○ If printer is not found or in error, reboot the printer and then reboot the APEX.
    ○ If printer is found and showing no error, reboot the APEX.
    ○ Check that the printer, media or finishing material hasn't been disabled on the APEX under Set Media/Equipment Availability (see Activate and Deactivate products on APEX or Self-Service Print Station below)

4. Enable/Disable Products on APEX or Self-Service Print Station:

• Manage products that are temporarily unavailable due to:
   o Equipment issues
   o Consumable/media out of stock
• Function is available on APEX and SPS only
• Products must be marked on both APEX and SPS if both have items unavailable
• This function will disable the item online so can't be ordered from web and app

Instructions:
1. On the APEX, select:
   • Tools
   • Set Media / Equipment Availability

2. Select one of the following:
   • Equipment out of Service – to disable a piece of equipment that is out of service
   • Media out of Stock – to select media that may be out of stock
   • Finishing Material out of Stock – to select consumable that is out of stock e.g. canvas frames
