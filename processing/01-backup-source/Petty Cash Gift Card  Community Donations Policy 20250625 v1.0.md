Petty Cash

Kmart Petty Cash Policy

The following guidelines must be adhered to for all petty cash transactions:

• Petty cash transaction can only be requested and processed by a manager and/or a Cash Office Team Member.

• Store/DC Management authorisation must be obtained prior to a petty cash transaction being made (this request must also include what the petty cash is for, value and why the purchase is required).

• In the event the Store Manager is unavailable or on leave, the Store Manager can elect another Manager within the store as the delegate to authorise petty cash requests.

• No manager can process their own petty cash transaction.

• All petty cash transactions must be reconciled daily.

• Petty cash can only be used for the authorised transactions outlined in this policy to the authorised level.

• Managers must be aware of authorisation levels outlined in these guidelines. Under no circumstances are large petty cash purchases to be divided into smaller amounts to enable lower or no level of authorisation. This includes instances such as morning tea where purchases are made through multiple retailers and would still require authorisation as the purchase is for a single use/event.

• Stores must use the ordering options e.g. stationery items are ordered through the Officeworks portal (refer to 'Petty Cash Unauthorised Transactions' of this guide).

• Regular vendor and ongoing service providers are to be paid by the expense process once an invoice is issued via accounts payable e.g. bike assembly, rubbish removal etc.

• Petty cash items should be purchased from retailers or service providers that can provide a compliant tax invoice that contains ABN number, GST amount, date, and invoice total.

• All Petty Cash expenditure must have a tax invoice/receipt attached to the Store Cash Transaction Form (SCTF).

• Personal rewards (such as Flybuys) cannot be included in the purchase transaction.

• Breaches of this policy will be treated seriously and may result in disciplinary action.

Petty Cash Tax Invoice/Receipt Required Criteria

A tax invoice/receipt must meet the following criteria:

• Intact with no marks or defacing or fuel/alcohol offers torn off.

• Original, i.e. not a photocopy (unless a Manager/Team Member requires a copy of the receipt for warranty purposes).

• Current/recent date.

• Business name where item/service was obtained.

• ABN of the business.

• Legible detail of items/services purchased.

• SCTF petty cash transaction must include evidence such as machine cash count, bag number and written approvals.

Community donations must have the request received on a formal letterhead or email with contact details before being for filled.

Gift Cards

Kmart Gift Card Policy
The following guidelines must be adhered to for all gift card transactions:

• Gift cards should only be purchased as required.

• Gift card purchases must be authorised prior to purchase as per the authorisations level listing in these guidelines.

• Managers cannot process their own gift card transactions and must be transacted by another Manager in the store.

• In the event the Store Manager is unavailable or on leave, the Store Manager can elect another Manager as the delegate to authorise petty cash requests.

• Customer satisfaction gift cards can be purchased and approved by a Store Manager up to the value of $100. Any gift cards above this value require Zone/DC Manager approval, and Community Team within Corporate Affairs notified.

• Gift cards provided for customer satisfaction or team reward should be 'Kmart only' gift cards, not any other brand.

• Breaches of this policy will be treated very seriously and may result in disciplinary action.

Community Store Donations Process

Step 1
Kmart stores can donate Kmart only gift cards or stock via petty cash on the SCTF to support local community groups, to the value of up to $100 (per request).

Step 2
Any groups seeking support through a donation needs to provide a written request (via letter or email) on their letter head, or with community group details, explaining who they are, what they are asking for and the intended purpose.

Step 3
It is up to the Store Manager to either approve or decline the request.

Step 4
ALL Community donations must be logged on the day the donation is issued and actioned on CMS on the SCTF breakdown via the donation web form location on Kit under quick links.

Step 5
If a request is declined, a polite response needs to be sent to the community group – please see attached a templated response for use.

Step 6
Any donations must be charged from your stores kitty – no gift cards are to be charged to Kmart Community – without prior authorization.

Step 7
If you receive a request, over the $100 donation amount, please send any requests through to Kmartcommunity@kmart.com.au for consideration.

What If Scenarios
Question | Answer

What if an item I need listed in the unauthorised transactions portals does not have the item I need?
If you require an item from a supplier e.g. Stationery and it is urgently required either:
• Store use the item from stock in the store and use a checkseal to show it is for store use; or
• Seek approval from your Zone Manager.

What if I receive a request to donate a gift card to a school or charity?
Refer to the Community Donations Tracking Sheet via Kit for instructions, or as directed by National Office.

Can I purchase a gift card other than a Kmart gift card for reward and recognition?
Only Kmart gift cards should be purchased and provide to team members as reward and recognition. A morning tea can also be provided as per the budget guidelines outlined in this policy.

What if I need to make a Petty cash transaction for something not covered in this policy?
Speak to your Zone Manager for guidance and provide what the petty cash request is for, the value and the reason is needed.

Can I use my Flybuys card and /or Staff Discount card for a Petty Cash Transaction?
No these cannot be included in the transaction.
