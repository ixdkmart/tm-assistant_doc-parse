Policy – Continuity of Service

INTRODUCTION
AND PURPOSE
Kmart and Target (together referred to as "the Company") recognise continuity of service for the purpose of restoring long service leave entitlements and for the recognition of service milestones.

POLICY APPLICATION
This policy applies to all team members and should be applied where the team member's Award or Collective Agreement is silent on continuity of service.

The policy and its definition of continuous service has no application in relation to the entitlement to, or calculation of, a team member's redundancy payments.

COUNTRY APPLICATION
Australia and New Zealand

POLICY
Eligibility

Entitlement to continuity of service for long service leave varies in each state and territory as legislation prescribes different eligibility criteria and break periods. However, the maximum break in service for granting continuity of service is three months.

Team members who have rejoined the Company with a break in service of three months or less will automatically be granted continuity of service as part of the rehire process, except where legislative criteria have not been met.

Entitlement under continuity of service

Long service leave

Team members who are granted continuity of service will qualify for long service leave from their original commencement date, excluding the period of absence from the Company. If the previous entitlement has been paid to the team member on termination, new accruals will commence with a zero balance, based.

All other leave entitlements accrue from the new commencement date and any personal leave previously accrued will not be reinstated.

Service recognition

Team members who are granted continuity of service will have their service recognition date adjusted for the period of time they were not employed by the Company.

Casuals with breaks in service

Casual team members who have not been scheduled to work within three months of their last shift worked will be deemed to have their contract ceased on the date of their last shift worked.
