Policy - Annual Leave

INTRODUCTION AND PURPOSE
The purpose of this policy is to:
• assist in maintaining the health and wellbeing and support work/life balance for our team members by encouraging them to take their annual leave entitlement
• prevent excessive annual leave accruals

POLICY APPLICATION
This Policy applies to permanent full-time and part-time team members employed by Kmart Australia Limited and who are paid an annualised salary.

The Policy does not apply to:
• a team member who is covered by an Enterprise Agreement or Award;
• a team member who is employed as a casual.

This Policy can be varied or withdrawn at any time and does not form part of a team members contract of employment.

COUNTRY APPLICATION
Australia

POLICY
Entitlement
For each year of service, a permanent team member is entitled to 4 weeks of paid annual leave, which accrues progressively in a year of service according to the team member's ordinary hours of work.

Payment for annual leave is at the team member's base rate of pay.

Taking Annual Leave
Annual leave can be taken as it accrues and must be approved by your line manager, before the leave is taken.

Requests to take annual leave will not unreasonably be refused, however annual leave requests may be rejected during key operational or trading periods or where there is a business need.

In line with the objectives of this policy, all permanent team members are encouraged to take their entitlement of 4 weeks annual leave each year.

Team members with more than 4 weeks annual leave accrued, are encouraged to take more than 4 weeks annual leave in year.

Team Members must not apply for a quantum of paid leave, which if approved, would result in a negative annual leave balance.

Public Holidays

If in the period that a team member has booked annual leave a public holiday falls in the place where a team member is based for work, then the team member is taken not to be on paid annual leave on that public holiday.

Occasions where annual leave may be re-credited

If the period during which a team member takes paid annual leave includes a period of personal/carer's leave, compassionate leave, emergency services leave or jury service leave (but not including unpaid parental leave), the team member is taken not to be on paid annual leave for the period of that other leave.

Notice and evidence requirements are the same as generally applies for the particular type of leave concerned.

Subject to line manager approval, the team member may:
• have their annual leave re-credited to be taken at a later date; or
• have their current period of leave extended for the period of illness.

Applying for Annual Leave

Team members can view their annual leave balances in SuccessFactors.

Annual leave can be taken in part days, single days or in weeks.

Team Members must apply for annual leave on-line through SuccessFactors. Team members are required to request and have their annual leave approved by their Line Manager prior to proceeding on leave. Subject to Kmart's operational requirements requests for annual leave will not unreasonably be refused.

Line managers authorise annual leave on-line through SuccessFactors. Line Managers are responsible to ensure that all annual leave requests by team members are entered and approved online through SuccessFactors prior to a team member taking annual leave.

Cash Out of Annual Leave

Cash out of annual leave, will not be authorized by Kmart except where permitted by legislation or where an award or enterprise agreement allows such cash out, in accordance with those terms and only by mutual agreement.

Salaried Team members wishing to cash out annual leave must complete the 'The Cashing Out of Annual leave form' and obtain authorisation from their Line Manager prior to submitting a request in SuccessFactors.

The relevant cashing out form can be accessed on KIT.

Excessive Annual Leave

Where an award or an enterprise agreement deals with excessive annual leave the terms of the award or enterprise agreement will apply and not this policy. These terms may be varied by the Fair Work Commission or successor body. In all other circumstances the terms of this policy will apply to dealing with excessive annual leave.

Where a team member has annual leave in excess of eight weeks, the line manager may, if reasonable in the circumstances, direct the team member to take an amount or amounts of annual leave accrued by that the team member, provided that the team member retains at least 4 weeks of annual leave accrual.

Line managers must review their team members' leave balances quarterly to ensure excessive leave is not being accrued by team members.

Managers are required to consult with team members with excessive annual leave balances in order to genuinely reach agreement about the taking of excessive annual leave at a time which is mutually convenient for both Kmart and the team member taking into consideration the operational needs of the business. A direction to take annual leave should only be issue after consultation has taken place.

Management of Annual Leave on Transfer

Team members who:
• transfer from full-time to part-time employment; and/or
• transfer from another Wesfarmers business to another

are encouraged to aim to have no more than four (4) weeks accrued leave based on their new hours of work at the time of transfer. Team members who transfer in circumstances detailed above are strongly encouraged to reduce their annual leave balance to comply with the objective of this policy regarding the regular taking of annual leave.
