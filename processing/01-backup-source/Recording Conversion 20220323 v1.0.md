## Page 1

Recording Conversion

Enter conversion figures into the iAuditor template titled 'Conversion'

Step 1
Ensure customer greeter has required resources:

Counting Device:
1. Hand Tally Counter
OR
2. Kmart People Counter
OR
3. Counting App on PDT.

Customer Count Sheet

Step 2
Customer greeter to count all customers entering the store (excluding Infants, Contractors & Team Members), and record totals on the Customer Count Sheet.
This can be recorded each hour and totalled at the end of the day, or a running tally can be kept throughout the day.
(Note: Kmart People counter only allows a count to 999, so if this device is being used, then it is recommended that totals are recorded every hour)

Step 3
Access the iAuditor template named 'Conversion'
Select Store
Enter Week Commencing Date (Monday). The one template will be used for the whole week, and finalised after Sundays figures have been added.

Step 4
For the day, enter the following:

1. Total amount of transactions for the day
Can be obtained from the Daily Recap Report
2. Total amount of customers for the day
Can be obtained from the Customer Count Sheet
3. Conversion Percentage
Manual calculation: Amount of Transactions / Number of Customers x 100

Eg. 1,860 Transactions / 9,000 Customers = 0.206
0.206 x 100 = 20.6%

Step 6
iAuditor will remind you after each day the audit is not complete until seven days of figures have been submitted. Click 'Save in Progress'.
