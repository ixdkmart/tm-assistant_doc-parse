Position Description

As Australia's largest and most loved retailer, we've been brightening the everyday lives of families for over 50 years. With stores, distribution centres, and offices across Australia, New Zealand, and beyond, our 47,000-strong diverse and inclusive team works together to offer families on-trend products at incredibly low prices. At Kmart, we value your uniqueness because it's not just what you do, but who you are, that makes everyday living brighter.

Position Title | Function | Reports To | Years of Experience Required for Role | KNA 2024 Classification (if applicable) | GRIA Level (if applicable)
---|---|---|---|---|---
Assistant Supervisor | Stores | Department Manager or Supervisor | At least 1 Year in a retail environment | Assistant Supervisor | Retail Employee level 4

Assistant Supervisors support Kmart and K-Hub store leaders across a number of processes in store. They support with inventory processes; sequence and decant, as well as presentation standards in store through 'A Great Place to Shop' (AGPTS). Where required, Assistant Supervisors can support in leading the efficient closing of the store at the end of trade. Assistant Supervisors play a key role in leading individual store processes geared towards efficient flow of inventory and great standards in stores. The role of an Assistant Supervisor also requires problem solving within the skill set, experience and classification level.

Key Internal Stakeholders | Key External Stakeholders | Number of Direct Reports | Number of Indirect Reports
---|---|---|---
Team Members, Department Manager, Store Manager | Center Management, Security, Customers | 0 | 0

Experience and Expertise:
• Assistant Supervisors should have a good understanding of Kmart store layout and understanding of daily processes.
• Have confidence in being able to give basic direction to other team members when processing daily and weekly tasks.
• Ability to adjust their communication style to get the best out of the store teams to boost productivity.
• Good understanding of Kmart safety procedures and able to support in case of any incidents and support with retraining where required.
• High level of self-motivation and a proactive mindset.

Technical Ability:
• Attention to detail, good planning and organisation skills.
• Ability to manage multiple priorities effectively.
• Socially and personally aware of immediate customer demands and challenges.

Who you are:
• You are a team player who works well with others to achieve store objectives.
• You establish and have proven to demonstrate high standards of work and presentation.
• You have the ability to make time responsive decisions within your skillset, experience and classification level and if required, support decision making with your Department Manager or Store Manager.
