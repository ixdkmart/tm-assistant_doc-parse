Kmart Refunds & Returns

Kmart processes refunds for customer every day for orders/products in circumstances where an order is missing an item, the item is faulty/damaged, or the customer has changed their mind.

It's important that every refund is checked carefully before and while being processed using the guidelines outlined in this guide, to ensure the customer's refund or return is processed correctly in line with the Kmart Return Policy (Click Here).

Refunds MUST be Issued Using the Original Payment Method

• Refunds must be made to the original payment method.

• This can be identified by looking at the bottom of the receipt presented.

• For card payments refer to the last four digits and ensure they match the last four digits on the card the customer presents for the refund.

• In this example the customer paid with a NAB savings account card and the last four digits to compare are 4893.

• If the customer is unable to provide the card that matches the original purchase, an Exchange Evoucher may be issued.

PLEASE NOTE: Digital receipts are now available for customers who sign up to the Kmart app. Customers may present for a refund using this digital receipt.
Digital receipts will display the last three digits of the card used in the original transaction, however if you scan the receipt the last four digits of the card used in the original transaction will appear at the top right of the POS screen. Ensure this number matches the card the refund is being placed onto, to ensure the refund is paid to the original payment method.

POS Prompt: A prompt will display at POS when performing a refund to an EFT account. When 'cards' is selected as a tender, a message pop up box appears with 'Match last 4 digits of card/digital wallet with receipt'.

Refunds Without a Receipt
Refunds without a proof of purchase cannot be issued unless the product is faulty.
As a result, we no longer require the capture of customer ID.

Split Payment Refunds
• Same as single type of payment – split refunds must issue using the original payment methods.
• For card payments refer to the last four digits match the last four digits on the card the customer presents for the refund.
• In this example a savings account was used with four digits 7167, and a gift card.
• Gift cards cannot be refunded.
• Do not process an online refund any other way to avoid duplicate refunds (CSC have no visibility to refunds processed outside this process and this could result in duplicate refunds being received).

Online Refunds
• Online refunds can only be refunded by the original payment method.
• Use the POS 'Lookup' function to search for online order refunds, or by scanning the barcode on the customer's invoice.
• View the original transaction details to check that none of the items have been previously refunded.
• Once the online refund is completed through POS it will be credited back onto the customer's account the purchase was made onto.

Gift Card & Exchange Voucher Refunds
Gift cards and exchange vouchers cannot be refunded once purchased. Gift cards and exchange vouchers are not redeemable for cash as outlined in the terms and conditions of these cards.

If a customer returns an item paid for with a gift card or returns voucher, they can only receive the value of the refund back as an exchange voucher.

Apple/Google Pay Refunds
• The customer needs to present the card within their digital wallet from their mobile device, with the device number that is listed on the receipt.
• For this type of payment refer to the last four digits on the receipt and match them to the last four digits displayed on the digital card.
• In this example the receipt needs to show the last four digits as 3318.

Afterpay Refunds

Afterpay refunds are processed the same way as you would process a refund for a Visa or Mastercard refund in store. The customer completes the refund by accessing their Afterpay Card directly from their digital wallet or via the steps below using the Afterpay application:

1. The customer oepns their afterpay app on their mobile device and selects the 'Card' tab.
2. They click on 'How it works' located above their pre-approved spend limit.
3. They scroll down to the 'How do I return an order' section and tap on 'Refund to Afterpay Card' button.
4. The customer taps their Afterpay Card on your card terminal to ccomplete the trasnaction.

Note: If the origfinal payment was made suing Afterpay, the refund must go back onto the customers Afterpay card and no toher tender type.

To ensure the refund goes back onto the customers Afterpay card cross check the last 4-digits on the customers Afterpay card with the digits on the receipt.

Apple Devices:
1. Open the settings app.
2. Scroll down to 'Wallet & Apple Pay'
3. Tap the Afterpay Card.

Android Devices:
1. Open the 'Google Wallet' app.
2. Tap the Afterpay Card.
3. Numbers are displayed on the card or as a virtual account number.

Change of Mind Returns

Customer may return items as they have simply changed their mind on the purchase. Refunds and returns can be provided if:

• The customer has their original proof of purchase e.g. receipt.
• The item being returned is within 60 days of purchase
• The item/s are in resaleable condition; meaning that:
   o It is in its original packaging, including instruction manuals and all accessories; and
   o It is unworn, unused, unassembled and in its original condition; and
   o In the case of mobile phones and internet connected electronic devices (such as Google mini and Wi-Fi connected home accessories), the original plastic wrapping or seal sticker securing the product closed, is intact; and
• The item is not one where a change of mind is not available.

Refunds must not be provided unless a proof of purchase is provided. Managers may use their discretion in special circumstances e.g. gifted items.

Change of Mind Items Not Available for Refunds & Returns

The following items cannot be refunded or returned under any circumstances when a customer changes their mind on the purchase:

• Cosmetics (if the hygiene seal is broken)
• Beauty products
• Fragrances
• Printer cartridges
• Tents
• Personalised printed materials (for example but not limited to photos, canvas, calendars, photo gifting etc.
• Gift cards (including third party gift cards) and phone recharge cards

• Hosiery
• Underwear
• Swimwear
• Pierced Jewellery
• Food & drink products
• Bedding and accessories e.g. mattresses, air mattresses, sleeping bags, pillow protectors etc. where seal and/or packing has been opened.

Faulty Refunds & Returns
Kmart will accept faulty product returns with the choice of an exchange, an exchange voucher, or a refund. For refunds the customer will need to provide one of the following proof of purchase:
• The Kmart receipt (including the barcode).
• Online proof of purchase (includes Zip, Afterpay, Paypal statements etc).
• A finanical statement (includes flybuys, credit card statement).

If the customer returns a faulty Anko product without a receipt, the customer should be provided with an exchange voucher.

If a customer presents for a refund for a faulty item and they do not have their original payment method request a manager to assist to validate available details e.g. a bank statement so they can then authorise a refund to different tender.

IMPORTANT:
Team members are not to test electrical products returned as faulty. Testing of a returned faulty electrical product can only be performed by a licenced electrician.

One Pass Members
One Pass members are eligible for 365 change of mind returns, and can access digital receipts via their One Pass account. A return can be provided as long as:
• The customer has a proof of purchase.
• The customer shows they are a current paid or trial member.

What If Scenarios
Scenario | Response
The customer does not have the original payment card anymore? | As a refund cannot be provided, an Exchange Only Evoucher may be issued.
The customer does not have a driver's licence | Customers need to provide a form of ID for a refund. Other forms of ID that can be used are any government issued ID's. This only applies to AUS stores. NZ stores cannot offer a refund without a receipt in any instance.
The refund has been processed, but the customer has not received the refund into their account. | This will depend on who the customer banks with. It can take up to five business days. The customer should be referred to their financial institution
The customer ordered online; will they get the delivery fee refunded also? | If most of an order has not been fulfilled or is faulty/ damaged, yes. For change of mind, no.
