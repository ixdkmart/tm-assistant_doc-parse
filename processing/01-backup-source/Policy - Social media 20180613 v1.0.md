Policy - Social Media

INTRODUCTION AND PURPOSE

Social media offers people the opportunity to create, share and exchange information and ideas in virtual networks and communities online.

This policy is intended to provide the framework which regulates the acceptable use of social media by Kmart team members, contractors and consultants.

Globally the uptake and participation in social media is growing rapidly. This interest also extends to companies who are realising that social media offers new opportunities to engage in conversations with customers and other communities with shared interests.

Kmart's view on social media is that it can assist with corporate and business engagement, however all team members need to be aware of how they interact and engage when using social media.

It is important for team members to remember that social media platforms are public forums. Team members should not make the assumption that the comments or materials that they post on social media are private. Team members should also not rely on a social media platform's default security settings to provide a guarantee of privacy. It's important to review the privacy settings on each social media account.

Social media posts made by team members can be copied or reproduced elsewhere, including on other open forums without any privacy settings. Any team member who posts comments or material online should make an assumption that at some point their identity and the nature of their employment may be revealed.

The policy protects the interests of team members and Kmart. It ensures that team members who use social media either as part of their job, or in a personal capacity understand Kmart's expectations where the social media engagement is about Kmart, its products, its team members, and / or other related stakeholders or organisations.

When engaging on social media platforms about Kmart, Kmart asks that team members:
• be clear that they are a Kmart team member;
• take responsibility for ensuring that any references to Kmart are factually correct and accurate and do not breach any confidentiality requirements; and;
• show courtesy for individuals and the communities where they interact.

Given the uptake of social media and its development, this policy will be reviewed regularly to ensure it remains relevant and applicable.

POLICY APPLICATION

Kmart's social media policy applies to all Kmart team members, contractors, or consultants employed or retained by Kmart.

This policy applies:
• during work hours;
• when social media is accessed using Kmart computers, equipment or servers;
• when social media posts discuss or mention Kmart or Kmart team members or specific stores, even outside work hours using personal IT equipment. Kmart considers such circumstances to have a requisite connection to workplace.

Kmart understands that there is value in using social media to build more meaningful relationships with customers, communities and other relevant stakeholders. As a result Kmart has determined social media channels that it will participate in and has assigned authorised representatives to oversee these channels. If you are discussing Kmart in your personal use of social media platforms, or you are officially accredited to represent Kmart in social media, you are required to follow this Policy.

The acceptable use of social media within Kmart is also regulated by other Kmart policies, including:
• Kmart's Electronic Communication Policy;
• Kmart's Equal Opportunity and Bullying Policy;
• Kmart's Code of Conduct; and
• Kmart's Privacy Policy

Team members need to be aware that their conduct when using social media could potentially breach other Kmart policies, and that any such conduct may also be subject to disciplinary action under those policies. For example, making of a series of threatening posts on Facebook may constitute not only a breach of this policy, but also harassment or bullying in breach of Kmart's Equal Opportunity and Bullying Policy and a breach of Kmart's Code of Conduct.

If you require clarification on this policy and how it applies to your own circumstances, please contact your Line Manager or HR Manager.

Line Managers are responsible to ensure compliance with this policy by team members.

Australia and New Zealand

POLICY
Social media tools

Social media includes, but is not limited to:
• social networking sites, such as Facebook, LinkedIn, Yammer where users connect with each other, and share content;
• micro blogging sites such as Twitter and Tumblr;
• file sharing sites such as YouTube, Instagram, Picasa and Flickr where users can upload and share photos, videos, etc.;

Responsibilities of team members

Use of social media in the work environment is permitted within reason.

Personal use (such as checking your personal Facebook page during a lunchbreak) is acceptable, as long as it does not:

• interfere with a team member's proper performance of work;
• breach our Code of Conduct, this policy or any other Kmart policies or guidelines;
• damage Kmart's reputation or disclose information that Kmart considers confidential

We have a dedicated Facebook team tasked with responding to customer inquiries or criticism. This team is responsible for engaging with customers through the Kmart Australia page. To avoid confusion please allow the Kmart Facebook team to respond to all customer comments on the page.

While we encourage team members to join our Facebook community and participate in conversations with our customers and other users, we encourage you to direct your complaints or concerns about your job or working environment to your store manager or through your State HR Manager. Social media is not the appropriate forum to discuss or raise any complaints or grievances you may have about your job or working environment.

When topics around Kmart appear within closed groups or published articles online your obligations as a team member under this policy, the Code of Conduct and Kmart's Equal Opportunity and Workplace Bullying policy apply. Should you have any concerns on topics or conversations within any closed groups please bring these to the attention the corporate affairs team to manage. This includes any questions relating to product withdraws/recalls, stock availability etc.

Examples

Examples of behaviour which Kmart deems acceptable and behaviour that may be in breach of other Kmart policies (if there is a connection to the workplace) include:

Acceptable:
• Commenting positively on a picture which is posted on Kmart's Facebook page about a new range of toys or other products;
• Using your personal Twitter account to include a picture of you wearing a Kmart product and mentioning where it's from;
• Engaging positively in a conversation with your friends online about a specific product which is available at Kmart.

Unacceptable:
• Uploading video footage taken at work to YouTube, Facebook or any other online social media site which damages or potentially damages Kmart's reputation or business interests or the reputation or interests of others connected to Kmart;

• Posting derogatory, offensive or inappropriate, threatening or hateful comments about a team member, customer or any person with whom Kmart has business dealings (e.g.: contractors or suppliers) on a friend's Facebook 'wall', in a status update or 'tweet', including where such comments constitute a breach of Kmart's Equal Opportunity Policy (for example, discrimination, bullying, harassment or sexual harassment) or a breach of the Kmart Code of Conduct;

• Posting any gratuitously or unreasonably critical comments about Kmart, its products or any person with whom Kmart has business dealings (e.g.: contractors or suppliers);

• Using any Kmart electronic resource to access online social media forums in circumstances where you may display or others view any offensive or inappropriate images, content or sounds in breach of Kmart's Equal Opportunity Policy and/or Kmart's Electronic Communications Policy and

• Using any personal electronic device in the workplace or at any work related activity (such as a work function) to access any online social media forums where team members, suppliers or customers may be exposed to offensive or inappropriate images, content or sounds in breach of Kmart's Equal Opportunity Policy

When you are considering making a comment or posting any material on social media (including in a private capacity), you should always ask yourself:

• Could my post harm or lower the reputation of Kmart or the reputation of any of Kmart's team members, customers or any persons with whom Kmart has business dealings (e.g.: contractors and suppliers)?

• Is my post lawful (e.g.: is it discriminatory, defamatory or threatening)?

• Would I be comfortable if my manager and/or my family saw the post or it was in the news?

• How would my post reflect on me personally?

Mentioning Kmart on social media platforms

Social media platforms are visible to the public and therefore are regarded in the same way as any other form of media. The nature of social networking sites and blogs means that 'any posts' or 'comments' are effectively published to the greater online community and are therefore considered to be part of the public domain.

Only authorised spokespeople may make statements about or on behalf of Kmart. Approval will need to be granted from Corporate Affairs regarding responses to any comments / enquiries. This applies even if you are using

Using images and logos on social media

Utilising Kmart logos or any form of Kmart branding on social media sites must be expressly approved by Corporate Affairs prior to including on sites. The misuse of Kmart's logo and branding may amount to a breach of Kmart's Code of Conduct and be subject to disciplinary procedures.

Using social media sites for business / marketing purposes

Kmart Corporate Affairs provides authorisation in relation to social media presence and store profiles. This is determined depending on the circumstance and team members are advised to contact Kmart Corporate Affairs if they wish to discuss.

Breach of this policy

If you are found to have breached this policy, you may be disciplined and disciplinary outcomes could range from counselling to formal warnings and potentially the termination of your employment. Further, you may be liable at common law for defamation.

Definitions

Defamation is the act of publishing information the effect of which is to lower the reputation of the (defamed) person or company in the eyes of the public at large.

Confidential information means facts or knowledge that are not in the public domain and that was originally communicated with an express or implied obligation not to disclose its contents. Confidential information is not limited to information confidential to Kmart, but also others connected with Kmart such as suppliers, which you have become aware of during the course of your employment.

Questions

Should you have any questions regarding your rights and obligations under this policy, please contact your Human Resources Manager.
