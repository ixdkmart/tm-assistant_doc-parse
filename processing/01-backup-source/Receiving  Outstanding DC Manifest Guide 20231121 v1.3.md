Receiving & Outstanding DC Manifest Guide

This guide steps through the process of managing DC manifests.

Truck Arrives (Obtain the DC Summary Manifest & 52W)
Step    Action
Step 1  Check the store name and number matches on W52.
Step 2  Check the seal is correct.
Step 3  Check the store name matches on the manifest.
Step 4  Check the pallet types received are correct on the manifest.
Step 5  Check & tick each pallet consolidation off the manifest.

Scan the Manifest
Step    Action
Step 1  Using a PDT, scan in the manifest when the load is received.
Step 2  Once the pallets have been unloaded, the Receive DC Delivery option should be selected.

Count Equipment to be Returned
Step    Action
Step 1  Count all equipment being returned (pallets etc.).
Step 2  Captive stores list these on the W52 and keep pink copy.
        Non-Captive stores fill out an equipment movement docket.
Step 3  Provide a copy to the driver and keep the pink copy for your records.

Manifest Screen Investigation

Step | Action

Step 1
Outstanding manifest details should be accessed, using the DC Manifest Enquiry screen. Manifests that have not been receipted are listed without a 'Receipt Date'. Manifest (a-kmtkmg.net) This screen should be checked daily.

Step 2
Manifests listed without a receipt date should be investigated. Stores should look for the DC Summary Manifest at the back of house to confirm if the stock was received. If found, they should scan and receipt the outstanding manifest.

Step 3
If the paper copy of the DC Summary Manifest cannot be found, stores should use the DC Manifest Enquiry screen to open the manifest details as a PDF, investigate the keycodes and QTYs and confirm if the stock has been received at the store. This can be done by counting the physical stock and comparing it to the PDT stock on hand or by checking keycodes and confirming if they have gone into negative stock on hand. If more stock is found than you should have or keycodes are in negative stock on hand, the manifest should be receipted. This process should be followed for house and standard manifests.

Step 4
If the manifest is at an offsite, they can be marked as "at offsite". The store should retain email evidence that the manifest is offsite.

Step 5
If the store finds that the manifest has not been received they should follow the blue claim process.

Info:
Manifest No: This is the manifest number that, once confirmed as received, can be entered into the PDT under Receive DC Delivery.

Manifest Type: A standard manifest is the usual type of manifest the store receives. The store gets a copy of the physical DC Summary Manifest with the W52 and the load. House manifests relate to stock that has left the DC but was missed off standard manifests.

Received and Receipt Date: manifests without the received tick and no Receipt Date have not been scanned in. This means the manifest is not yet part of the store's stock on hand.

View Manifest Report: allows store to view the keycodes as an Excel or PDF and investigate if the stock has been physically received at the site.
